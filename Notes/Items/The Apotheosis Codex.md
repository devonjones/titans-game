# The Apotheosis Codex

**Type:** Wizard's spellbook
**Former Owner:** [[Bathere]] (deceased)
**Source:** Recovered from Bathere's study at [[Dragonspear]] Castle, [[Session 07 - The Door of the Dead|Session 7]]
**Status:** Unclaimed; wizard party members (i.e. [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]) to consider

## Overview
[[Bathere]]'s personal spellbook. The title is our working name — Bathere's [[Dracolich Research|dracolich ambitions]] mean the book is effectively a playbook for his own apotheosis. Contents emphasize necromancy, control, battlefield manipulation, and a few teleportation/protection options.

## Notable Inclusions
- **Animate Dead, Vampiric Touch, Circle of Death** — the necromancy track; aligns with [[Dracolich Research]]
- **Legend Lore** — confirms [[Bathere]] was actively trying (and failed) to divine the **[[The Door of the Dead]]**
- **Sickening Radiance, Cloudkill, Synaptic Static** — his area-control options; we saw Sickening Radiance in play from [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]], presumably after consulting this book (or independently)
- **Forcecage, Maze, Teleport** — escape / control at the high end
- **Wall of Force, Globe of Invulnerability** — defensive kit for rituals in progress

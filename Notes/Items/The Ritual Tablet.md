# The Ritual Tablet

**Type:** Tablet — ritual text, written in Abyssal
**What it is:** **A ritual to resurrect the dead dragons, and *potentially* to gain control of them**
**Recovered:** [[Session 05 - Signed S|Session 5]], [[The Dead Three Temple]] — spotted by [[Vaelin]], translated by [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]
**Corrected:** GM clarification, 2026-10-22

## ⚠️ What The Correction Actually Was

This vault spent seven sessions describing this object as a **dragon-control scroll** that made the holder **commander of the revived dragons** — *"the only known leash."*

**That was wrong on two counts, and right on one.**

> **GM:** *"We have no such scroll."*
>
> **GM:** *"The tablet is a ritual to resurrect the dead dragons and potentially gain control of them."*
>
> **GM:** *"It is the one from Session 10, it just doesn't do what you thought. **Potentially** is doing the heavy lifting there."*

| | Vault said | Actually |
|---|---|---|
| **Form** | A scroll, on vellum | **A tablet** |
| **Function** | **Grants control.** Whoever holds it commands the revived dragons | **A resurrection ritual that *potentially* confers control.** Not a leash. Not a command. **"Potentially" is doing the heavy lifting** |
| **Which object** | The Session 5 find, discussed again in Session 10 | ✅ **Correct — same object** |
| **Who has it** | [[Vaelin]] | ✅ **Believed correct** — it is the Session 10 object, and that is where Session 10 put it |

**So the party does hold it.** It simply is not, and never was, a mechanism for commanding an ancient red dragon.

## What It Says

From [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s translation in [[Session 05 - Signed S|Session 5]]:

> **The Ritual of the Ancient Ones:**
> - Ritual sacrifice of **two innocents**
> - Place their essence on a **disk**
> - Take the disk to **[[Mount Hotenow]]**
> - Finish the ritual there to **raise the ancient ones**
> - Connected to the **[[Cult of the Dragon]]**
> - Names: **[[Rofidian]]** and **[[Ravica]]**

Everything the vault learned from it stands: **the dragons' names, [[Mount Hotenow]]'s significance, the disc mechanism, and the [[Cult of the Dragon]] connection.** Those were all correct and remain load-bearing.

**What does not stand** is the idea that holding it does anything to a dragon.

## What "Potentially" Means In Practice

**Assume no control until proven otherwise.** The realistic constraints on a clause like this:

- It is **part of the resurrection ritual**, so control may only be conferrable **at the moment of revival** — and both dragons were revived long ago, off-screen, by the [[Cult of the Dragon]]
- It may require **the discs** (which [[Ahmendril]] has), **the site** ([[Mount Hotenow]]), **the sacrifices**, or **the original ritualist** to invoke
- It may require a caster tier the party does not have
- It may simply not have worked

**[[Ahmendril]] ran the ritual and the dragons still attacked a city.** Whether even *he* controls them is an open question — and [[Rofidian]] has been loose and unaccounted for since [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]].

> **Practical ruling for the table: the party has no control over [[Rofidian]]. Do not plan around having a leash.**

## ⚠️ But It Is Still Why A Dragon Might Be Here

**[[Rofidian]] landed in front of the party in [[Session 17 - Something That Belongs to Me|Session 17]] and said:**

> ### ***"I believe you have something that belongs to me."***

**The party is holding the tablet that contains the ritual that raised him** — and that *potentially* claims authority over him.

**From a dragon's point of view that is his, and it is intolerable in anyone else's hands.** He does not need it to actually work; he needs it gone.

**And the party may have advertised it.** This vault described the object as a working leash for seven sessions. If any of that framing reached the fiction — a briefing to [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]], the council, or **the note the party left with Silverhand in [[Session 16 - The Masks I Am Owed|Session 16]] summarising everything they know** — then the party has been telling people it holds a dragon's leash. **That is exactly the rumour that brings a dragon to your door.**

**Other candidates for what he means** (see [[Rofidian]]): [[Ravica]]'s harvested corpse, the dragon-hoard item [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s father is researching ([[Session 16 - The Masks I Am Owed|Session 16]]), or a mask.

## The Decision In Front Of The Party

**Giving it up costs less than the vault previously implied** — it is not a leash, so handing it over surrenders no control the party actually had.

**But it is not free:**
- It is the **only physical record of the ritual** — the names, [[Mount Hotenow]], the disc mechanism
- Giving an ancient red dragon the ritual that resurrects dragons, and *potentially* commands them, hands him **[[Ravica]]** — whose corpse is in [[Waterdeep]] and whose re-resurrection [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] specifically blocked with Gentle Repose
- It is the party's **only bargaining chip** with something that could kill them all

**Copy it before any negotiation.** [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] reads Abyssal and already translated it once.

## Open Questions

1. **Does [[Vaelin]] physically have it?** The Session 10 record says so and the GM confirms it is the Session 10 object — **worth one explicit check of the character sheet**
2. **What exactly does the control clause require?** Discs, site, sacrifices, the original ritualist, a caster tier?
3. **Can it work on an already-revived dragon**, or only at the moment of revival?
4. **Does [[Ahmendril]] control the dragons?** They attacked [[Waterdeep]] — his order, or their own will?
5. **Is it what [[Rofidian]] came for?** And has the party unwittingly advertised owning a leash?
6. **Could [[Ravica]] be raised from it again?** Celleborn's **Gentle Repose** was meant to prevent exactly that
7. **Is it a tablet or a vellum scrap?** The [[Session 05 - Signed S|Session 5]] note describes *"a scrap of parchment… it's vellum."* The GM calls it a tablet. **The vault's physical description may be wrong too**

# Cloak of Tranquility

**Type:** Magic item (cloak)
**Bearer:** [[Viseli Bar]]
**First Used (on record):** [[Session 04 - Good Timing]]

## Effect (Observed)

When activated, the Cloak causes the wearer to **grow one size category**, dealing extra damage on all weapon attacks. Observed effects:
- Size increase from Medium to Large
- **+1d6 damage per hit** while enlarged
- Used as a combat amp by Viseli during fights

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 04 - Good Timing]]
[[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] activated the Cloak during the Aura of Darkness assassin fight in [[Viggio Martel|Viggio]]'s sitting room. Grew one size category, dealt extra damage. **Hit the invisible attacker for 18 damage** after activation. Saved [[Kane]]'s life.

### [[Session 09 - The Wolf and the Phoenix]]
Reminder noted: **+1d6 per hit if enlarged via the Cloak.** Standard tactic going forward.

# The Key

**Type:** Suspected artifact -- inferred third piece of the sealed Dead Three Far Realm gateway
**Origin:** Built by the **[[The Dead Three|Dead Three]]** (theorized)
**Current Possessor:** Unknown -- strongest suspect is [[Ahmendril]]
**Role in the Gateway:** The **key** -- the unlocking mechanism
**See also:** [[Notes/Items/The Door of the Dead]] (doorway), [[Notes/Items/The Stolen Keystone]] (keystone)

## Status: Theoretical

We have **not directly identified the Key.** Its existence is inferred from the three-piece "doorway + keystone + key" gateway architecture the party theorizes from:
- [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] briefing on the Dead Three building a gateway and scattering "the pieces"
- The known Door + Keystone giving us two of the three traditional arch components
- The patterned dualities in the larger plot (two discs, two dragons, two pendants on Ahmendril)

## Primary Candidate: Ahmendril's Gemstone Pendants

[[Ahmendril]] was described in [[Session 04 - Good Timing|Session 4]] as wearing **two gemstone pendants -- one blue, one red.**

**Why these are the strongest candidate:**
- He keeps them **on his person** -- consistent with the key being the part the operator personally guards
- **Dual nature** (blue + red) matches the dual-piece patterns elsewhere in his work (two discs in red/black, two dragons Rofidian + Ravica)
- The party has never investigated them; they could be inert jewelry but they could equally be a paired key set
- If Ahmendril has the Key already, his recent moves make sense:
  - He took the **Door** at Dragonspear ✓
  - The **Keystone** was just extracted from Waterdeep during a coordinated attack ✓
  - **He already has the Key** -- the pendants -- which is why he didn't need to go acquire a third piece this session

If true, **the gateway is assembled the moment the keystone reaches him.**

## Other Candidates (Lower Probability)

1. **The Discs (post-ritual)** -- two discs, drained for the dragon revival. "Drained" could mean "expended" or "imprinted with operational power." If the latter, the discs could BE the key after the dragon ritual charged them
2. **Something we haven't found yet** -- Silverhand said the pieces (plural) were "spread around"; only the Door is confirmed identified. A third piece could be in a similar hidden-in-plain-sight location somewhere else
3. **[[The Goat Head Mask|The Mask]]** -- **RULED OUT.** Ahmendril took the Door from Bathere but left the Mask behind. If the Mask were a gateway piece, Ahmendril would have taken both. He didn't, so the Mask is Bathere's personal phylactery, not gateway architecture

## Per-God Speculation (Dead Three Mapping)

If each piece is tied to one of the Dead Three:
- **Doorway → Myrkul** (death; "the Door of the Dead"; instant-death effect; gatekeeper of the dead)
- **Keystone → ???** Bane (tyranny -- "load-bearing authority," holds the structure together)? Or Bhaal (murder -- a stone, a weapon)?
- **Key → ???** The opposite of the keystone (Bhaal or Bane, whichever isn't the keystone)

If the Key is Bane's piece: it would be about **authority / command** -- the legitimate act of opening, as opposed to merely battering the door
If the Key is Bhaal's piece: it would be about **the act of cutting / piercing** -- physically intruding through the seal

**No evidence yet** to lock either mapping in. The Door = Myrkul theory is the most concrete one.

## Open Questions

1. **Is the Key actually the pendants?** Or something else entirely?
2. **Where are the pendants now?** Almost certainly still on [[Ahmendril]] -- track him, the pendants follow
3. **Can a key be a paired item** (blue pendant + red pendant = two-part key)? Yes, in many magical traditions a key requires both halves to function
4. **Is the Key separable from the operator** -- could the party steal it from Ahmendril if they reached him?
5. **If we destroy the Key, does the gateway become impossible to assemble?** Or is it temporary -- a new Zanithar makes a new key?

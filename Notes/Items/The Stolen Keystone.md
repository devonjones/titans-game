# The Stolen Keystone

**Type:** Suspected artifact -- fragment of the sealed Dead Three Far Realm gateway
**Origin:** Was set in the main entrance arch of the [[Waterdeep]] royal castle (hidden in plain sight as architecture)
**Current Possessor:** Unknown thief; over 1000 ft from the castle when [[Vaelin]] cast Locate Object at end of [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]]
**Role in the Gateway:** The **keystone** -- the structural lock holding the arch together
**See also (plot tracking):** [[Notes/Plots/The Stolen Keystone]]

## Physical Description

- The keystone of the **main entrance archway** of the [[Waterdeep]] royal castle
- Hidden in plain sight as load-bearing castle architecture
- We have not seen it directly outside of context (it was always part of the wall until taken)
- Visual appearance: presumably stone, possibly with Dead Three iconography that would have been ignored as decoration

## What We Know

- Was **deliberately removed** from the castle's main entrance during the [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] dragon attack / Tarrasque chaos
- **Not** collateral damage from the dragons -- the rest of the castle is damaged but the keystone was surgically extracted
- **Over 1000 feet from the castle** by the time [[Vaelin]] cast Locate Object at session end -- the thieves moved fast and had distance covered
- The dragon attack provided cover for the heist

## Why We Think It's a Gateway Piece

The case:
- **Hidden in plain sight** in a major landmark (Waterdeep castle) -- exactly the kind of place the Dead Three "spread the pieces around" to
- **Surgical removal** during a city-wide distraction -- argues for a planned operation, not opportunistic looting
- **Timing convergence** -- coincides with [[Ahmendril]]'s revival of [[Rofidian]] and [[Ravica]] attacking Waterdeep. The dragons attacking Waterdeep is the same operation as the keystone extraction
- **No other obvious target** -- if you're running an extraction this risky under cover of an army assault, dragons, and a Tarrasque, it's not for a generic magic component. It's for a campaign-tier artifact

Counter-case (the keystone might NOT be a gateway piece):
- The castle's enchantments may include load-bearing wizardry that's independently valuable (Waterdeep wards, Open Lord authority anchors)
- The keystone could be a pre-Silverhand magical anchor unrelated to the Dead Three
- The dragon attack could be coincidence; an unrelated faction took the opportunity

**Working assumption: it's a gateway piece**, until evidence says otherwise.

## Who Took It

Unknown. Strongest candidates:
1. **[[Ahmendril]]'s operatives** -- he benefits most; he was just at Dragonspear and the disc ritual completed; ready for the next assembly piece
2. **[[The Pencheska Conspiracy|Pencheska]] operatives** -- they were already in [[Waterdeep]] (hit the party at [[Ellenfear Manor]] the previous night); raven-mask infiltration team consistent with extraction tradecraft
3. **A third faction** working the Far Realm angle independently

The Pencheska and Ahmendril operations may be the same operation -- both report ultimately to whoever runs the [[Zanithar]] cycle.

## Three-Piece Architecture (Current Theory)

The party believes the gateway is a three-piece assembly:
1. **Doorway** -- [[Notes/Items/The Door of the Dead|The Door of the Dead]]. Ahmendril has it. **Tied to Myrkul** (death)
2. **Keystone** -- **THIS** -- just taken from Waterdeep, in transit. **Tied to Bane or Bhaal** (uncertain)
3. **Key** -- [[Notes/Items/The Key|The Key]]. Unidentified; strongest candidate is Ahmendril's two gemstone pendants. **Tied to whichever Dead Three isn't the Keystone**

## Per-God Speculation (Dead Three Mapping)

The Door = Myrkul theory is concrete. For this piece:

- **Bane (tyranny / load-bearing authority)** -- the keystone is the structural piece that locks the arch and prevents collapse. "I hold this together" maps to Bane's portfolio
- **Bhaal (murder / the killing strike)** -- a keystone is also a *stone*. Bhaal-flavor would be more about a knife or piercing weapon than architecture

**Best guess:** Keystone = Bane. The thematic match is cleaner (load-bearing, authoritative, the piece that makes the structure work). If correct, the Key is Bhaal's piece.

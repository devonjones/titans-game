# Samus's Helm

**Type:** **The Ram's Head Mask** -- crafted by [[Ahmendril]]; one of [[The Masks of Ahmendril]]
**Bearer:** [[Kane]] (carrying, **not** worn, **not** attuned)
**Source:** [[Samus]], [[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar|Session 14]], [[Troll Claw Hills]]
**Status:** **Legend Lore'd ([[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]]). Still not Identified. Do not wear.**

## What Happened

Mid-fight, with [[Samus]]'s knees wobbling and him looking dead at Kane, [[Kane]] asked:

> *"Does your helmet come with any curses or negative effects I should know about?"*

**Samus did not smile.**

**[[Kane]] felt foreboding.**

After [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] killed him, **Kane grabbed the helmet.**

## ✅ WHAT IT IS (Legend Lore, Session 15)

[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] cast **Legend Lore** on it. A group vision followed.

**[[Ahmendril]] made this mask for [[Samus]], by hand, after defeating him in battle and accepting his surrender.**

- A battlefield. [[Samus]] -- viking-esque, **not in Zhent colors**, apparently **the commander**
- [[Ahmendril]] appears: **aura of elemental energy, draws a sword, throws what is probably an Otiluke's Freezing Sphere** and annihilates a crowd. A high-level caster who is also adept with a blade
- **He beats Samus and accepts his surrender.** They negotiate. Samus nods
- **Both teleport to a wizard's library full of masks**
- **Ahmendril is wearing an owl mask.** His **pendant** hangs at his neck
- **A montage of Ahmendril crafting the ram's head mask**

### And then the post-credits scene

**[[Samus]] dies** in a later combat -- **an angel in the distance**, a **paladin stabbing him in the chest.** Fade to black.

**He comes back, kneeling before [[Ahmendril]], wearing the mask.**

> ## THIS IS A RESURRECTION ANCHOR
>
> Confirmed twice the same session -- [[Farraday]]'s Legend Lore shows the identical pattern. **The mask is how [[Ahmendril]]'s lieutenants come back.** See [[The Masks of Ahmendril]].
>
> **[[Kane]] is carrying [[Samus]].**

### What this answers

- **"Why does [[Ahmendril]] have your chain?"** [[Kane]] asked [[Samus]] that in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] and got a deflection. **The chain was hanging on Samus's face**
- **Why Samus refused to answer about a curse.** He wasn't hiding a curse. He was watching the party take the thing that brings him back
- **Why the bargain was priced in *helms*.** Not a euphemism -- **taking a lieutenant's mask may be the only way to kill one permanently**
- **Where Samus came from.** [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] said he *"rose out of nothingness to lead the [[Zhentarim]]."* He rose out of **another century**

### How long until he returns?

[[Farraday]] died ~100 years ago and returned after an **~80-year gap.** On that timeline **[[Samus]] is not coming back this campaign** -- and the party's regret about not dismembering the body was misplaced. **The body was never the anchor.**

## ⚠️ A DEVIL IS ASKING FOR IT (Session 16)

At [[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]], the pit fiend's **first words to the party** were:

> ***"Have you brought the masks? The masks I am owed?"***

Then: **"SEARCH THEM!"**

**[[Kane]] is carrying this mask, on that battlefield, right now.**

[[Samus]]'s devil contract was **paid in masks** — see [[The Devil's Claim]]. Which means this object is not merely a resurrection anchor for a dead enemy; **it is collateral on an unsettled infernal debt**, and the creditor is standing a few dozen feet away demanding delivery.

**Three live risks:**
1. **The debt may follow the object.** Holding it may make Kane the party to the contract
2. **The devil wants it physically** and ordered a search to find it
3. **Handing it over** would settle a debt the party did not incur — and arm a pit fiend with whatever the masks actually do

**[[Kane]]'s play:** he shouted at the pit fiend asking **why attack us when [[Ahmendril]] has all the masks** — attempting to redirect the claim onto the real debtor. It is true, and it is the best available card.

## Why This Is a Problem

1. **Samus refused to answer.** A dying man with nothing to lose, asked a direct question about a curse, chose silence. **Now explained -- but the silence is still data**
2. **Kane felt foreboding** -- an in-fiction sensory beat the GM chose to give him, not a joke
3. **The party has done this before.** [[The Goat Head Mask]] came off [[Bathere]]'s corpse the same way: an evil artifact with non-detection that is **still attuned to its dead owner**, that [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] herself could not destroy. The party is carrying two helms off two dead bosses now
4. **[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] explicitly asked to Identify everything before anyone does anything with the loot.** That request should be honored here first

## The "Helms" Question

In [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]], [[Samus]] priced [[Samus's Bargain|his bargain]] in an oddly specific currency:

> ***"You have already run into some of Ahmendril's lieutenants. Bring me their helms, and I will let Mark go."***

**Not heads. Helms.**

At the time that read as a euphemism. It reads differently now that Samus had a helm of his own that he wouldn't discuss, and that the party's other trophy from a dead enemy commander is **also** a piece of headgear and **also** an artifact.

**Working theory:** among these people the helm may be the **office** -- a mantle-marker rather than a battle trophy. Compare the [[Zanithar]] mantle itself, which is explicitly a **role** that is "periodically killed and replaced" rather than a person.

If that's right, then:
- [[Farraday]]'s **raven's head mask** is her helm
- Taking a lieutenant's helm may **transfer or vacate something**, not just prove a kill
- Kane may be holding [[Samus]]'s office

**Confirmed [[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]].** [[Ahmendril]] keeps **a wall of masks** in his workshop, wears an **owl mask** himself, and there is **a second owl mask on the wall.** [[Farraday]]'s raven, [[Bathere]]'s goat, and this ram are all part of the same collection. See [[The Masks of Ahmendril]].

## Before Anyone Wears This

- [ ] **Identify** ([[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]])
- [ ] **Detect Magic** -- and note whether it resists, like [[The Goat Head Mask]] does
- [ ] **[[Kane]]'s Divine Sense** -- the same check that flagged the Goat Head Mask as evil
- [ ] Check whether it is **still attuned to [[Samus]]**, and whether Samus's body was left in a collapsing keep that then became [[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate|a portal to hell]]
- [ ] Ask [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] -- she has already examined one artifact helm for this party. **Caveat: she is compromised ([[Silverhand's Missing Memory]]) and doodles an owl mask**
- [ ] **Decide whether carrying it into a devil fight is safe.** A pit fiend has now demanded the masks by name and ordered a search ([[The Devil's Claim]])
- [ ] **Decide whether carrying it is safe.** [[The Goat Head Mask]] already lists "attaches to someone in the party" as a worst case, and this one is a live return anchor for a man [[Kane]] personally helped kill
- [ ] **Find out whether destroying it kills [[Samus]] permanently** -- or releases him

> **Note:** Samus's body went down inside the keep. **The keep collapsed and the ground opened underneath it.** If the helm needed his corpse for anything, his corpse is currently in a hell-pit.

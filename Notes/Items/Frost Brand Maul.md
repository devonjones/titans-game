# Frost Brand Maul

**Type:** Magic weapon (maul) -- **Frost Brand**
**Bearer:** [[Viseli Bar]]
**Source:** Looted from [[Samus]]'s corpse, [[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar|Session 14]]

## How It Was Found

**[[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] looted [[Samus]] and noticed the hammer broadcasts a chill.** [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] asked for it immediately. Confirmed in the session's loot tally as a **Frost Brand maul**, and assigned to her by the end of the session.

**[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] wants to Identify it (and everything else) before it goes into use.**

## What Samus Did With It

He hammered [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] with it for **13, 9, and 13** in a single round -- *after* Rage reduction. She is now swinging the thing that nearly put her down.

## Frost Brand (Standard 5e)

For reference until Identify confirms:
- **+1 to attack and damage** (some versions +3)
- **+1d6 cold damage** on a hit
- **Resistance to fire damage** while attuned
- **Extinguishes non-magical flames within 30 feet**
- **Sheds bright light in freezing temperatures** (10 ft bright / 10 ft dim)
- **Requires attunement**

## Why This Matters for Viseli

She already runs **Rage + [[Cloak of Tranquility]] enlargement (+1d6 per hit at her tier)**. A magic maul with a cold rider stacks straight onto that. It is also her **first confirmed magic weapon on record** in the vault -- previously she was landing hits on things like [[The Assassin]] with no noted enchantment.

**Fire resistance is the sleeper benefit.** The party ate a **36-damage Fireball** in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] and is riding toward a **[[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate|Pit Fiend]]**. Devils are fire. Attuning this before that fight is worth doing deliberately.

## Attunement Note

Frost Brand requires attunement, and [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] already runs the [[Cloak of Tranquility]]. **Confirm her attunement slots are clear** before assuming this is live.

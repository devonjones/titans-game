# The Door of the Dead

**Type:** Artifact -- fragment of a sealed Far Realm gateway
**Origin:** Built by the **[[The Dead Three|Dead Three]]** (Bane, Bhaal, Myrkul) -- **this piece specifically tied to Myrkul** (death; the door / "the way through")
**Current Possessor:** [[Ahmendril]]
**Role in the Gateway:** The **doorway** -- the literal aperture piece
**See also (plot tracking):** [[Notes/Plots/The Door of the Dead]]

## The Myrkul Connection (Working Hypothesis)

The party believes the Door is **Myrkul's** contribution to the three-piece gateway. The case:
- Name: literally **"The Door of the Dead"** -- Myrkul's portfolio (death, the undead)
- Effect: **instant death on touch** -- a Myrkul signature
- Bodies at [[Dragonspear]]: piled corpses -- Myrkul's iconography
- The "way through" / passage / aperture theme matches Myrkul as the gatekeeper of the realm of the dead

If each piece is tied to one of the Dead Three, the other two pieces should track to Bane and Bhaal. See [[Notes/Items/The Stolen Keystone|the Keystone]] and [[Notes/Items/The Key|the Key]] for current per-god speculation.

## Physical Description

We have not directly observed the Door. What we know from [[Bathere]]'s research notes ([[Session 07 - The Door of the Dead|Session 7]]):

- Object you can touch (handled by Bathere in some way without dying -- or perhaps Bathere studied it without touching)
- **Touching it kills you instantly.** The piles of bodies at [[Dragonspear]] are evidence
- Dead Three iconography / symbology
- Resists divination magic

## The Far Realm Gateway

Per [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] briefing:

> The Dead Three made a magical gateway to the **[[The Far Realm|Far Realm]]** -- a place of old gods, raw chaos and power. They got in over their heads, **closed the door, and spread the pieces around** the world to keep it from being reassembled.

The Door is one of those pieces. **Reassembling all pieces opens the gateway.**

## Three-Piece Architecture (Current Theory)

The party currently believes the gateway is a three-piece assembly:

1. **Doorway** -- **THIS** -- the literal aperture. [[Ahmendril]] has it
2. **Keystone** -- [[Notes/Items/The Stolen Keystone|The Stolen Keystone]] -- the structural lock holding the arch together. Taken from [[Waterdeep]] during the [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] chaos. Over 1000 ft from castle when last located
3. **Key** -- [[Notes/Items/The Key|The Key]] -- the unlocking mechanism. Unidentified; strongest candidate is Ahmendril's two gemstone pendants

If this model is correct, Ahmendril is closing in on full assembly:
- He has the Doorway
- The Keystone was just taken (possibly to him)
- He may already have the Key (pendants kept on his person from Session 4)

## How Ahmendril Got It

Ahmendril came to [[Dragonspear]] and **took the Door from [[Bathere]]**. Bathere was researching it but didn't have it as part of an active plan -- Bathere was on his own dracolich track and had collected the Door as a curiosity / artifact study. Ahmendril didn't take [[The Goat Head Mask|the Mask]] (Bathere's phylactery) or anything else of Bathere's at the same time -- he wanted the Door specifically.

**Implication:** Ahmendril knew exactly what the Door was. He came for it, took only it, and left.

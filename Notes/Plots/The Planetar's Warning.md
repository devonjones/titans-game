# The Planetar's Warning

**Type:** Divine intervention / standing directive
**Status:** Active -- the gods have weighed in
**Introduced:** [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms]]

## What Happened

In [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]], [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] cast **Divination** (4th-level, as a ritual) to locate **[[Notes/Items/The Key|the Key]]** -- the missing third piece of the [[The Door of the Dead|Far Realm gateway]].

A **planetar appeared** instead of the usual omen/response.

## The Exchange

> **Planetar:** *"Such questions are forbidden."*
>
> **Celleborn:** *"Two of them have already been acquired by bad guys. They are trying to recreate the gate, and we need to stop them."*
>
> **Planetar:** *"It is important that such things should not be found. Go find the other pieces."*
>
> **Celleborn:** *"OK, where are they?"*
>
> **Planetar:** *"Seek the one who stole them."*
>
> **Celleborn:** *"Where?"*
>
> **Planetar:** *"He moves around a lot."*

## What This Means

### Divine Forces Oppose the Search for the Key
A **planetar** -- a celestial of the angel order, the kind that does NOT show up for routine divinations -- appeared in response to a fourth-level ritual. That's not "the spell worked unusually well." That's **the gods intervening.**

The message: **finding the Key is forbidden, regardless of intent.** Even Celleborn's "we're trying to stop the bad guys" framing didn't move the planetar. The position is absolute.

### Implications

1. **The gods have a stake** in the Key not being found. This goes beyond mortal politics
2. **The Key, if found, is dangerous regardless of who holds it** -- the planetar didn't ask whose side Celleborn was on
3. **The planetar accepts that the party should find the other pieces** -- the Door and the Keystone *can* be retrieved. The Key cannot
4. **The location is being deliberately hidden** by celestial authority -- this isn't "we don't know," it's "we won't tell you"
5. **[[Ahmendril]] "moves around a lot"** -- the only specific intelligence offered. Hint that **the Key is with him**, hence "find the one who stole them" -- but the planetar wouldn't pin a location

### Vaelin's Confirmation
After the planetar departed, [[Vaelin]] cast **Commune** (5th-level cleric; three yes/no questions to his deity). Conclusion: **the gods specifically do not want the Key found.** The planetar's appearance was deliberate divine intervention, not a random celestial passing by.

## Tactical Implications

- **Don't repeat the Divination spell** -- the planetar is on a hair-trigger; pressing again risks worse than a refusal
- **Don't search for the Key directly via magic** -- Locate Object, Locate Creature, Scrying, Find Path are all likely to encounter the same block
- **DO search for [[Ahmendril]]** -- the planetar explicitly said "seek the one who stole them" (the OTHER pieces, plural). The Door and Keystone are on Ahmendril. Following him is sanctioned
- **DO interrogate [[Samus]]** -- mortal intelligence sources are not blocked. Samus probably knows where Ahmendril is or has been
- **If the Key is found and assembled, the gods themselves intervene further?** Unknown. May be a hard stop at a higher level

## The "Don't Ask Why" Implication

The planetar refused to **explain why** the Key must not be found. Possibilities:

1. **The Key is a deity / a piece of one** -- a Dead Three relic embodying a fragment of Bane or Bhaal (whichever isn't the Door)
2. **The Key triggers automatic gate opening on contact** with the other pieces (and the bad guys having it means we have to keep them apart, but mortal hands on the Key risk activating it inadvertently)
3. **The Key drives the bearer mad / corrupts them** -- handling it is unsurvivable
4. **The Key destroys the searcher** if approached by anyone but its current divine custodian
5. **The gods themselves don't know exactly where it is** and don't want mortal divination to find out before they can act
6. **All of the above**

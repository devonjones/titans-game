# The Sword Coast Crisis

**Type:** Regional Instability
**Status:** Ongoing Background
**Introduced:** [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]

## Summary
The entire Sword Coast is experiencing coordinated instability - organized monster tribes, civil war in the [[Dalelands]], increased banditry, and absent patrols. This may be the larger context for the [[Zhentarim]]'s moves on [[Daggerford]].

## Known Threats

### Monster Tribes
- Sword Coast invaded by "organized" monster tribes
- Organization suggests intelligence and coordination
- Not typical monster behavior

### Dalelands Civil War
- Full civil war in the [[Dalelands]]
- [[Zhentarim]] involvement confirmed
- Driving refugees toward the Sword Coast

### Banditry
- Banditry up across the region
- Main roads are unsafe
- Patrols absent from trade routes

### Maritime Threats
- "Trouble at sea" mentioned
- Trade routes disrupted
- Details unknown

## Pattern Analysis
The simultaneous nature of these threats suggests coordination:
- Monster tribes push people from wilderness areas
- Civil war pushes people from the east
- Banditry makes travel dangerous except toward "safe" zones
- All roads lead to [[Zhentarim]]-controlled areas

See: [[The Destabilization Strategy]]

## Regional Powers
- [[Lords' Alliance]] - Regional defense pact, seems absent
- [[Harpers]] - Intelligence network, [[Kane]] has connections
- [[Emerald Enclave]] - Nature protection, [[Cael Stormcrow]]'s faction
- [[Zhentarim]] - Possibly orchestrating everything

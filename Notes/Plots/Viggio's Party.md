# Viggio's Party

**Type:** Social Encounter / Infiltration
**Status:** Party happened — investigation ongoing
**Introduced:** [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]

## Summary
[[Viggio Martel]] invited the party to his manor. We attended, confirmed the missing fingers are on the ground floor, made contact with potential allies, and discovered a hidden passage — but the fingers are still in Viggio's possession, and he slipped away mid-party.

## The Invitation
- [[Viggio Martel]] approached the party at the tavern (Session 1)
- Evaded questions when pressed
- Extended invitation to his party
- [[Kane]]'s assessment: He's part of the corruption

## What Happened (Session 3)

### Before the Party
- [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] sent us to spy; focus on disappearances
- [[Vaelin]] used *Locate Object* — confirmed fingers at Viggio's manor (#23 on the map), guarded by [[Zhentarim]] Grey Wolves under Commander [[Bennet]]
- Narrowing to the dwarf victim's finger: still confirmed at the manor
- Reported to Lady Morwen: she was shaken. **"BRING ME PROOF."** Physical possession of the fingers = proof.

### At the Party (~50 guests, upper class)
- [[Viggio Martel]] approached Stormcrow directly; introduced the party to [[Padon]] (cobbletoner, Builder's Guild)
- **Locate Object pings the ground floor** — the fingers are here, in this building, right now
- [[Vaelin]] made contact with [[Elaine Drago]] — she quietly indicated she opposes Viggio and is aware of the disappearances; positioned herself so Viggio couldn't see her ask
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] and [[Kane]] worked the room; Celleborn identified as associated with "luxury goods"
- Stormcrow chatted up [[Maren Thane]] — probed us about whether we expected trouble; we had to admit Lady Morwen summoned us
- [[Kane]] and [[Margeet Patel]] slipped off to the sitting room

### Viggio Disappears
- Mid-party, Viggio slipped out of the ballroom while deliberately trying not to be seen
- **Where did he go?**

### The Secret Passage
- [[Kane]] noticed dampness and wet footprints in the sitting room with Margeet
- The footprints appear to walk through a wall
- **Hidden passage — recently used**

## What We Know
- The fingers are on the ground floor of Viggio's manor
- Viggio has a Grey Wolf Zhentarim unit ([[Bennet]]) providing personal security
- [[Elaine Drago]] is an active potential ally, being careful
- Viggio vanished mid-party without wanting to be seen
- There is a hidden passage in the sitting room accessed from a locked door

## Open Questions
1. Where did Viggio go when he slipped out?
2. What is through the secret passage — and who used it recently?
3. Where exactly on the ground floor are the fingers?
4. How do we get physical possession of the fingers with ~50 witnesses around?
5. Is Margeet aware of the passage? Is that why she went to the sitting room?
6. What is Viggio's actual plan for the fingers?

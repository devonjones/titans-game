# Lady Morwen's Escape

**Type:** Extraction / Political
**Status:** Contingency Plan
**Introduced:** [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]

## Summary
[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] is considering fleeing to [[Waterdeep]] to escape the [[Zhentarim]] occupation. She has asked the party to potentially serve as her escort.

## Current Situation
- Lady Morwen's power in [[Daggerford]] has been usurped
- Her guards are overwhelmed by Zhent numbers
- Her ally [[Councilman Moore]] was just murdered
- Other unexplained deaths and disappearances
- She's under significant stress (physical reaction when Kane arrived)

## The Request
Lady Morwen wants the party to:
1. First: Investigate Moore's death (current mission)
2. Potentially: Escort her to [[Waterdeep]]

## Complications
- [[Vaelin]] (Baelin?) assessed the journey as "risky"
- Road conditions are dangerous (banditry up, patrols absent)
- [[Zhentarim]] would likely pursue or intercept
- Abandoning [[Daggerford]] may be exactly what the Zhents want
- Could be seen as surrender

## The Pencheska Question
*FR Lore Warning:* If the Pencheska conspiracy is active, there are disturbing possibilities:
- Is the Lady Morwen asking us to escort her the *real* Lady Morwen?
- If she's been replaced, is the real Lady Morwen still alive?
- Would escorting "Lady Morwen" to [[Waterdeep]] actually help the Zhents?

## Strategic Considerations
1. **If she stays:** Risk of assassination, imprisonment, or replacement
2. **If she flees:** Zhents win Daggerford, but she survives
3. **If she fights:** Needs allies, resources, and to break Zhent control

## Open Questions
1. Is fleeing the right choice or a trap?
2. What resources does Lady Morwen have outside Daggerford?
3. Would [[Waterdeep]] actually help her?
4. What happens to Daggerford if she leaves?
5. Can we break Zhent control enough that she doesn't need to flee?

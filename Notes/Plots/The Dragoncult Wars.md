# The Dragoncult Wars

**Type:** Historical event referenced by [[Laeral Silverhand]]
**Status:** Connects directly to [[Ahmendril]]'s current ritual; details unknown to the party
**Introduced:** [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars]]

## Summary

A historical conflict referenced by [[Laeral Silverhand]] in [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]] when [[Vaelin]] briefed her on [[Ahmendril]]'s plan. The current ritual -- two discs, two dragons being revived, an Abyssal control scroll -- is **connected to the Dragoncult Wars**, which Silverhand mentioned as if it were known history.

What the party knows: it happened, it involved dragons, and the current Zanithar's operation is patterned on it. **What we don't know is everything else.**

## What We Need to Find Out

1. **When did the Dragoncult Wars happen?** Years ago? Centuries? Millennia?
2. **Who fought whom?** Was there a dragon cult on one side? Multiple factions?
3. **What dragons were involved?** Were "Rofidian" and "Ravica" (named in [[Session 05 - Signed S|Session 5's scroll]]) participants?
4. **How did the wars end?** Were the dragons defeated? Sealed? Killed?
5. **What role did a [[Zanithar]] play?** Silverhand's framing suggests this isn't the first Zanithar-driven cycle
6. **What's the connection to the [[Harpers]] / [[Lords' Alliance]] / [[Chosen of Mystra]]?** Silverhand knew about it -- it's clearly part of her institutional memory
7. **What ritual or method was used last time** -- is Ahmendril's two-disc setup a known pattern?

## Connections

- **[[Ahmendril]]** -- current Zanithar, running a ritual Silverhand says connects to these wars
- **[[Zanithar]]** -- recurring role; previous Zanithars may have been participants
- **[[The Disk]]** -- the two-disc system may be a Dragoncult War-era ritual
- **The Abyssal scroll** -- dragon-control instructions; possibly an artifact or copy from the wars themselves
- **Rofidian and Ravica** (named in the scroll) -- the likely target dragons; possibly Dragoncult War-era dragons being revived

## Open Questions
- Is the goal the same now as it was then -- revive the dragons -- or has the purpose shifted?
- Did "we" (the side of the angels, broadly) lose last time, or win? If we won, why is it repeating?
- Why do dragon revivals keep being attempted? Is there a finite number of these dragons, or unlimited?
- Could the current ritual be **completion** of an unfinished Dragoncult War ritual, not a fresh attempt?

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 11 - When the World Burns]] -- Dragons Are Rising
**Two dragons attacked [[Waterdeep]] at the end of Session 11.** One breathing fire (giant size), one toward the docks. The Tarrasque emerged from the sea, killed the black one (the dragon toward the docks), and crashed through the wall pursuing the second.

**Strong possibility these are Rofidian and Ravica** -- the dragons [[Ahmendril]] is reviving per [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s Session 10 briefing. If so:
- The ritual has been **completed** (faster than expected)
- Both discs must have been drained
- The control scroll (in [[Vaelin]]'s possession) is now the only known leash, and the dragons are clearly NOT on the leash -- they're attacking Waterdeep on their own
- Ahmendril either hasn't activated the scroll, doesn't have it (Vaelin does), or activated it elsewhere with the dragons fulfilling his orders

The **Dragoncult Wars** historical context is now active and current. [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] should be pressed for the specifics of how the wars ended last time -- the precedent matters now.

The Tarrasque rising at the same time strongly suggests the cosmic immune system has flagged this as a **world-threatening event** -- consistent with the original Dragoncult War threat level.

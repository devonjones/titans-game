# The Stolen Keystone

**Type:** Theft / mystery
**Status:** Active investigation; keystone over 1000 feet from castle when last located
**Introduced:** [[Session 11 - When the World Burns]]

## What Happened

During the dragon attack and Tarrasque rampage in [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]], **someone deliberately removed the keystone arch from the main entrance of the [[Waterdeep|royal castle]]**.

Not collateral damage. Not a casualty of the dragon fight. **Deliberate, surgical theft** -- the rest of the castle is damaged but the arch was specifically targeted and extracted.

## What We Know

- The **main entrance archway's keystone is gone**
- [[Vaelin]] cast **Locate Object** on it: it was already **more than 1000 feet from the castle** when located
- That means the theft happened during the chaos and the thieves moved fast (or had a teleport / flight option)
- The dragon attack may have been **cover** for the heist
- Or the dragon attack may have been **coincidental** -- thieves seized an opportunity

## Why It Matters

The deliberate, targeted nature of the theft suggests:

1. **The keystone has occult / magical value.** Castle architecture sometimes incorporates anchored magic; a keystone is a literal load-bearing piece. Removing it could weaken wards or be a component for something else
2. **It could be another fragment of the [[The Door of the Dead|Far Realm gateway]]**. [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] said the Dead Three "spread the pieces around" -- a castle keystone in Waterdeep would be a reasonable hiding place
3. **It could be a Waterdeep-specific magical anchor** (Ahghairon's wards, Open Lord enchantments, etc.) and the theft is part of weakening the city's defenses
4. **It could be something else entirely** that nobody on the inside knew was important

## Suspects

- **[[The Pencheska Conspiracy|Pencheska]] operatives** -- they hit the Ellenfear Manor the previous night, they had infiltration in place
- **[[Ahmendril]] / [[Zanithar]] agents** -- if the keystone is a gateway piece, this is the obvious purpose
- **Cult of the Dragon** -- referenced in the [[Session 05 - Signed S|Session 5]] scroll; may be running an operation
- **An unknown third party** -- the dragon attack created opportunity; multiple actors could have moved

## What to Do

1. **Track the keystone.** Recast Locate Object periodically; it has a range of ~1000 ft per cast but each ping tells us direction-of-travel
2. **Ask [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]** about the castle's architectural enchantments -- does she know if the keystone was special?
3. **Ask [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]** -- his Dead Three thesis may flag whether keystone-shape components are part of the gateway pattern
4. **Investigate the arch itself** physically -- residual magic, debris pattern, witness reports
5. **Check other Waterdeep landmarks** -- if this is a pattern, what else might have been (or might be) taken in the chaos?

## Open Questions

1. Is the keystone a piece of the [[The Door of the Dead|Far Realm gateway]]?
2. Or is it a Waterdeep-specific anchor?
3. Who actually took it? How did they reach an actively dragon-attacked castle roof?
4. Where is it heading?
5. What else was stolen during the attack that we haven't noticed yet?

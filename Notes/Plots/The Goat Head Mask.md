# The Goat Head Mask

**Type:** Artifact — evil, attuned to a deceased enemy
**Current Possession:** The party (not attuned; unused)
**Introduced:** [[Session 07 - The Door of the Dead]]

## What It Is
The helm that [[Bathere]] wore at [[Dragonspear]]. Recovered from his corpse after the fight. It is an **artifact** (confirmed by [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]) with **non-detection magic** that resists investigation. [[Kane]] used **Divine Sense**: the Mask is **evil**.

It is **still attuned to [[Bathere]]** even though Bathere is dead.

## Why This Is a Problem
- **Still attuned** = whatever it does for its wearer is, in some sense, still live. The link to Bathere's dead body has not dissolved
- **Non-detection** = we can't cast Identify, Legend Lore, or Detect Magic in the usual ways and expect clean results
- **Evil artifact** = it wants things. Possibly to restore its bearer. Possibly worse
- **Artifacts have their own agendas.** Many have resurrection clauses, re-attunement triggers, or dominance effects on new bearers

## Worst Case
- The Mask resurrects [[Bathere]]
- The Mask attaches to someone in the party and tries to dominate them
- The Mask serves as a beacon — [[The Death Tyrant|Ahmendril]] knows where it is and comes for it
- All of the above

## What We Know (And Don't)
**Know:**
- Evil
- Artifact-tier
- Non-detection
- Still attuned to [[Bathere]]
- Related to the goathead aesthetic of [[Bathere]] and possibly to [[Dracolich Research]] or the Dead Three

**Don't Know:**
- What it does for its wearer
- Whether it's part of the dracolich ritual
- Whether it was made by the Dead Three, a devil, or something else
- What happens if [[Bathere]]'s body is destroyed
- Whether it can be safely destroyed

## Options on the Table
1. **Leave it in a warded box with the Duchess.** Her problem for safe storage; we stay mobile
2. **Take it to an expert** (the Emerald Enclave, the Harpers' archivists, Waterdeep scholars)
3. **Keep [[Bathere]]'s body intact and sealed** so the attunement doesn't collapse in an unexpected way
4. **Destroy [[Bathere]]'s body definitively** (disintegrate, burn to ash, scatter) to force the attunement to break — and see what the Mask does next
5. **Try to destroy the Mask** (dangerous; artifacts rarely destroy cleanly)
6. **Attune to it deliberately** with someone expendable or well-protected, and see what it grants/demands

## The Phylactery Problem

If [[The Goat Head Mask|the Mask]] is Bathere's phylactery:
- **Bathere is not gone.** His soul / essence is bound to the Mask and can re-emerge if a viable body is provided -- or the Mask itself may regenerate a host
- **Standard destruction failed.** [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] -- a Chosen of Mystra -- could not destroy it. That puts the bar very high
- **Mount Hotenow volcanic destruction** is the current best plan, but the volcano is also [[Ahmendril]]'s presumed ritual destination. Going there with the Mask risks delivering it to him
- **Sealing in a warded vault** is the conservative fallback -- store it somewhere it can't be retrieved (Waterdeep, the Emerald Enclave, etc.)

If it ISN'T a phylactery, Silverhand's destruction failure still says it's at least artifact-tier and not safely handled

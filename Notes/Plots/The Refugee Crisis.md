# The Refugee Crisis

**Type:** Humanitarian/Political Crisis
**Status:** Ongoing
**Introduced:** [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]

## Summary
Caravans of displaced people are fleeing from the [[Dalelands]] civil war toward [[Daggerford]], overwhelming the town's resources and creating social tension between locals and refugees.

## Current Situation
- [[Daggerford]] overrun with refugees
- Refugees outnumber [[Zhentarim]] patrols
- Locals growing angry about the influx
- Town resources strained
- Social tension rising

## Causes
- Civil war in the [[Dalelands]]
- [[Zhentarim]] involvement in the conflict
- Organized monster tribes on the Sword Coast
- Increased banditry on main roads
- Patrols absent from trade routes

## Witnesses
[[Kane]] and [[Viseli Bar]] traveled from the [[Dalelands]] to [[Daggerford]] and saw:
- Caravans of displaced people on the road
- The scale of the refugee crisis firsthand

## Suspected Conspiracy
See: [[The Destabilization Strategy]]

The [[Zhentarim]] may be deliberately fomenting the chaos driving refugees toward [[Daggerford]] to:
1. Overwhelm local resources
2. Create social division
3. Justify their "security" presence
4. Weaken [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]'s authority

## Open Questions
1. How organized is the migration?
2. Are there Zhent agents among the refugees?
3. What is happening in the [[Dalelands]] specifically?
4. Can the refugee crisis be addressed without playing into Zhent hands?

## The Human Cost (Session 2)
The bandits occupying [[Riverdale]] were themselves war refugees - displaced by the same crisis, turned to crime out of desperation. The [[Roderick]]: *"Being poor brought me here. War put us out of a job."*

This reveals a secondary effect of the crisis: displaced people with no resources are becoming bandits, which further destabilizes the region and justifies the [[Zhentarim]] "security" presence.

# Dracolich Research

**Type:** Research arc -- now understood to be [[Bathere]]'s personal project, not [[Ahmendril]]'s
**Status:** Bathere is dead; the research arc died with him. **NOT** what Ahmendril is actually doing
**Introduced:** [[Session 07 - The Door of the Dead]]
**Recontextualized:** [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars]]

## Session 10 Recontextualization

> ***[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] revealed: the necromancer ([[Bathere]]) did not know [[Ahmendril]].***

This single sentence rewrites the entire Session 7 interpretation:

- **Bathere was NOT preparing the dracolich ritual for Ahmendril.** Their projects were parallel
- **The "ancient ones" in the [[Session 05 - Signed S|Session 5]] scroll do NOT refer to Ahmendril's transformation.** They refer to **two ancient dragons being revived** (per Silverhand's Session 10 briefing on the [[The Dragoncult Wars|Dragoncult Wars]])
- **Ahmendril's actual plan**: revive two ancient dragons using two discs (black + red); the Abyssal scroll is the dragon-control mechanism. See [[The Disk]] and [[Ahmendril|Death Tyrant note]]
- **Ahmendril showed up at [[Dragonspear]] to TAKE [[The Door of the Dead]] from Bathere's collection**, not to direct Bathere's research

## What Bathere Was Actually Doing

[[Bathere]] was pursuing **personal apotheosis** -- become a lich, or build a dracolich, or both, on his own arc. The dead dragon in the cellars, the research notes, [[The Goat Head Mask]], the undead garrison -- all his own project. Ahmendril used Bathere's site (and walked off with the Door) but did not command him. Bathere even told the party he had "no use for [[Viggio Martel|Viggio]]" or the [[Zhentarim]] in Session 6 -- consistent with him being a parallel operator, not part of Ahmendril's command chain.

## What Carried Over to Ahmendril's Actual Plan

Some elements of Bathere's discoveries are still relevant to [[Ahmendril]]'s real ritual:

- **[[The Door of the Dead]]** -- Ahmendril took it. He's using it for SOMETHING in the dragon-revival ritual. Bathere couldn't pry an answer out of it with Legend Lore
- The **dead dragon** in Bathere's cellars -- may have been disc-fodder candidate, or unrelated
- [[The Goat Head Mask]] -- Bathere's; not part of Ahmendril's plan (probably)

## What Got Erased From This Plot

The Session 7 framing of "Ahmendril is becoming a lich/dracolich" was an honest reading of Bathere's notes -- but those notes were Bathere's diary, not a transcript of Ahmendril's plan. We crossed wires. The corrected story:

- **Ahmendril** is reviving two ancient dragons (see [[The Disk]], [[The Dragoncult Wars]]). His own form -- with the death-tyrant head -- is the signature of being a **[[Zanithar]]**, not a transformation in progress
- **Bathere** wanted to be a dracolich. He died before he got there. His arc is closed

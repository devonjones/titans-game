# The Devil's Claim

**Type:** Infernal debt / contract
**Revealed:** [[Session 16 - The Masks I Am Owed|Session 16]]
**Creditor:** The pit fiend at [[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]]
**Collateral demanded:** **The masks**

## The Line

The pit fiend's opening words to the party — **before any attack**:

> ## ***"Have you brought the masks? The masks I am owed?"***

The party answered *"What masks?"*

**He shouted "SEARCH THEM!"** and the Erinyes came forward.

## What This Reorganizes

### 1. Samus's devil contract was paid in masks

[[General Martavis|Martavis]] told [[Kane]] in [[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar|Session 14]] that **[[Samus]] *"made a deal with a devil to secure a brothel to recruit souls."***

That was the **consideration** — what Samus *received*. The brothel, the five succubi, the soul harvest.

**The masks are what he owed in return.**

### 2. Samus's bargain with the party was debt collection

In [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]], [[Samus]] offered to free [[General Martavis|Martavis]] if the party would:

> ***"You have already run into some of Ahmendril's lieutenants. Bring me their helms."***

**Not heads. Helms.** The vault flagged that word choice as deliberate across three sessions. **This is why.**

He was not collecting trophies and he was not merely trying to kill his rivals. **He was sourcing masks to settle an infernal debt**, and he wanted the party to do the collecting. [[Farraday]], [[Celine]], [[Draymon]] — three masks, one payment.

### 3. The chain had two ends

| Creditor | Hold over Samus | What he owed |
|---|---|---|
| **[[Ahmendril]]** | **The mask resurrects him** ([[The Masks of Ahmendril]]) | Service |
| **A devil** | The brothel contract | **Masks** |

[[Kane]] asked him directly: *"Why does [[Ahmendril]] have your chain?"* and got *"We have a mutually beneficial arrangement. It's not your concern."*

**He was leveraged from both directions and disclosed neither.** The deflection covered two arrangements, not one.

### 4. The debt did not die with the debtor

The pit fiend asked **the party** whether *they* had brought the masks, then ordered them searched.

**[[Kane]] is carrying [[Samus's Helm|the ram mask]].** The party took it off Samus's corpse in [[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar|Session 14]].

**[[The Goat Head Mask]] is in storage in [[Waterdeep]]**, unguarded.

> **Open and urgent: does holding a mask make you the debtor?** Killing Samus called the note and opened the gate. The party then picked up his mask and walked toward the creditor.

### 5. It suggests where the masks come from

[[Ahmendril]] **crafts** some masks — the party watched him make the ram in a Legend Lore vision ([[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]]).

But **[[The Goat Head Mask|the goat mask]] was recovered, not made**: hidden in a pool of acid in a dragon's lair, *well-hidden*, older and tougher than his workshop — and **[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]], a [[Chosen of Mystra]], could not destroy it.**

**If a devil holds a claim on these objects, the masks may be infernal in origin.**

That would also account for:
- **Non-spell darkness** on both [[Samus]] and [[The Assassin]] ([[Aura of Darkness]]) — a devil signature
- Why an artifact-tier mask resists a Chosen's destruction
- Why someone hid one in acid in a dragon's lair rather than destroying it

### 6. He expected a delivery

**He opened with a question, not violence.** Someone was supposed to bring this devil masks, and he thought it might be these people.

**[[Samus]] is dead. Who was the courier?**

## Kane's Gambit

With [[Ahmendril]] on the field, **[[Kane]] shouted at the pit fiend: why attack us, when [[Ahmendril]] has all the masks?**

**It is true, and it is the right card.** The mask maker holds the collection. [[Kane]] is attempting to **redirect an infernal debt onto the actual debtor** — the same fracture [[Samus]] tried to sell the party, run from the other side.

**If it lands, a pit fiend and his legion change targets.**

## Open Questions

1. **Who is the devil?** Still unnamed. **Contract devils have names, and names are leverage**
2. **How many masks is he owed, and which?**
3. **Does the debt follow the mask?** [[Kane]] is holding one
4. **Was [[Samus]] the courier, or just the signatory?**
5. **Does [[Ahmendril]] know about this contract?** A subordinate who pledged his master's artifacts to a devil is a subordinate with a second loyalty — **possibly the real reason Samus was moving against the lieutenants**
6. **Are the masks infernal in origin?**
7. **Can the contract be bought out, voided, or turned?** [[Kane]] is already trying
8. **Does the devil know where [[The Goat Head Mask|the goat mask]] is?** It is in [[Waterdeep]], unguarded
9. **Did [[Ahmendril]] come to the field for the masks, for the debt, or for the party?**

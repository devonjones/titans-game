# The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate

**Type:** Infernal breach / open portal
**Location:** [[Troll Claw Hills]] -- where [[Samus]]'s keep stood, on the Trade Way between [[Baldur's Gate]] and [[Daggerford]]
**Status:** **ACTIVE BATTLEFIELD** as of [[Session 16 - The Masks I Am Owed|Session 16]]. The party teleported in with the [[Order of the Fist]]
**Opened:** [[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar|Session 14]], within minutes of [[Samus]]'s death

## What Happened

[[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] killed [[Samus]] with a Lightning Bolt through a closed door. Shortly after:

1. **The ground shook.** Five minute warning
2. The party evacuated the keep. **It collapsed as they ran**
3. **The ground itself began to collapse.** They kept running
4. Where the building had stood: **an orange glow**
5. **A small swarm of small winged creatures flew up out of it**; some flew at the party
6. **[[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] detected a Pit Fiend rising out of the pit. With friends**

[[Vaelin]] named the shape of it before anything came out:

> *Dukes of Hell, or folks of that sort, might show up at times like this -- **looking for payment.***

He then cast **Sending** to [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]:

> ***"Seamus dead, Martavis now in charge of the Zhents. New Oblivion Gate, Duke of Hell may be en route."***

**Silverhand replied that she needs to know more.**

**The party withdrew and took the road to [[Daggerford]].** Nobody is standing on this.

## Session 16: The Assault

**[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] teleported the party in.** Force: the four available PCs, **[[Unthar Froom]] plus five heavily armored veterans** ([[Order of the Fist]]), and **[[Lady Estrell]]**, a wizard. [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] was absent.

### The win condition

**[[Vaelin]]'s read: what's keeping the gate open is most likely ON the pit fiend.**

This corroborates [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s [[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]] line — when [[Vaelin]] asked about identifying an item, she said ***the pit fiend probably has it.*** **Same object.**

> **Kill or banish the pit fiend, take the object, close the gate.** That is the objective. Not a war of attrition against a legion.

### The field

- The primary **pit fiend standing where the castle used to be**, an **Erinyes on each side**
- **"So many devils"**
- **A second pit fiend a few hundred yards back** — [[Kane]] had foreseen this in a vision of pit fiends laying waste to the countryside

### ⚠️ "The masks I am owed"

The pit fiend's **opening line, before any attack**:

> ***"Have you brought the masks? The masks I am owed?"***

**The devil is owed the masks.** [[Samus]]'s brothel contract was paid in masks, and his *"bring me their helms"* bargain was him trying to settle it. See **[[The Devil's Claim]]** — this is the biggest revelation of the campaign so far.

The party said *"What masks?"* He shouted **"SEARCH THEM!"** and the Erinyes came in.

### The fight so far

- **[[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] opened with [[Chain Lightning]]** — one mob down, another bloodied
- **[[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] CRIT an Erinyes for 68**; the paladins finished it
- **The pit fiend looked surprised and shouted "FALL BACK!"** — a CR 20 devil calling a retreat in round one
- **[[Kane]] killed the second Erinyes for 57.** Both discorporated *(they re-form on their home plane — not permanently dead)*
- **[[Lady Estrell]]** wrapped the pit fiend in **tendrils of earth**; he broke free
- **[[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] cast [[Static Cage]]** — 30 damage, with [[Elemental Adept]] rolling no 1s. **Lightning is unresisted here; pit fiends are immune to fire and poison, not lightning**
- **Everyone rolled DEX saves. "Magic leaves."**
- **[[Ahmendril]] APPEARED** — consistent with his **death-tyrant magic suppression**
- **[[Kane]] shouted at the pit fiend: why attack us when [[Ahmendril]] has all the masks?**
- **Fireballs rained on the party**

**Unspent:** [[Vaelin]] holds **his own Banishment and [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s scroll of Banishment** — both unused.

**Combat ongoing at session end.**

## Why It Opened

[[Kane]] asked [[General Martavis|Martavis]] what the implications were. Martavis's answer:

> ***"[[Samus]] had made a deal with a devil to secure a brothel to recruit souls."***

**The gate is the collateral coming due.**

Samus contracted with a devil for the brothel operation -- the pavilion the party saw in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]], staffed by **five succubi** (see [[The Succubi Alliance]]), harvesting souls. The debt was carried by Samus. **The party killed Samus.** The counterparty came to collect, in person, through the ground.

> **This is a consequence of a party decision, not an ambush.** The party attacked at the handshake, killed the debtor, and the note was called. Worth holding onto -- it is the kind of thing a GM sets up to be re-learned later.

## The Session 13 Callback

In [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] a woman watched the parley from the pavilion and [[Kane]] probed it:

- **Kane:** *"What does the lady think?"*
- **Samus:** ***"She doesn't know shit. It's a brothel."***

The session note flagged the speed of that deflection as suspicious. **It was.** The pavilion was the infernal asset, and it was the thing Samus most needed the party not to look at -- more than his lieutenants, more than his chain.

| **[[The Avernus Portal]]** | Beneath [[Dragonspear]] Castle | **Sealed** -- by the fountain-statue relic. **The party owns this castle and this seal** |

**Questions this raises:**
- Does an open breach nearby **stress, weaken, or attract attention to** the Dragonspear seal?

- [[Bathere]]'s spellbook, [[The Apotheosis Codex]], contains **Gate Seal** (4th-level abjuration). The party owns that book. **[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] may be able to close things**

## Session 15 Updates

**The fire is spreading.** From camp the night after, **the party saw fire in the distance, from the direction they had come** — the [[Troll Claw Hills]]. Nobody is watching this and it is not staying put.

**The friends have a name.** [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] identified the pit fiend's companions as **Erinyes** — CR 12 devils, fallen angels, **flying**, longsword and longbow, poison, Parry. A Pit Fiend with an Erinyes escort is a small legion, not a straggler.

**Something the party wants is down there.** [[Vaelin]] asked about sending to a wizard to identify an item; [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s answer was ***the pit fiend probably has it.*** *(Which item is unclear from the session notes — flag for the table.)*

**And [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] can scout it safely.** Her **Commune with Nature** ([[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]]) gives three facts across a **3-mile radius outdoors**, including *powerful fiends* and *influence from other planes of existence*. **That is this gate, read from a safe distance, for a ritual casting.**

**It is one of two options at [[Vaelin]]'s crossroads** — close the gate, or go after [[Farraday]] in [[The Waterdeep Sewers|the sewers]].

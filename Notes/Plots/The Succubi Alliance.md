# The Succubi Alliance

**Type:** Supernatural Threat
**Status:** **CONFIRMED IN THE FLESH** -- [[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar|Session 14]]
**Introduced:** [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]

## Summary
[[Kane]] has intelligence confirming that the [[Zhentarim]] have succubi allies. Given the Pencheska conspiracy from Forgotten Realms lore and the current political situation in [[Daggerford]], this is deeply concerning.

## Known Facts
- [[Zhentarim]] have succubi allies (Kane's intel, [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers|Session 1]])
- **CONFIRMED [[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar|Session 14]]: there were FIVE succubi in [[Samus]]'s camp in the [[Troll Claw Hills]]**
- **They ran the "brothel"** -- the pavilion [[Samus]] deflected on in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] (*"She doesn't know shit. It's a brothel."*)
- **It was a soul-recruitment operation** secured by a devil contract. Per [[General Martavis|Martavis]]: *"Samus had made a deal with a devil to secure a brothel to recruit souls"*
- **ONE IS DEAD. FOUR ESCAPED**

## The Session 14 Kill

[[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] spotted **the pavilion's tent flap moving** and shouted that an invisible person had gone in. From the keep: *"She went invisible!"*

- [[Kane]] was **gifted See Invisibility** by one of [[General Martavis|Martavis]]'s casters
- **[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] looked into the keep and saw an invisible woman**
- He asked Martavis's permission, then **cast Hold Monster. She was paralyzed**
- **[[General Martavis|Martavis]] attacked her. She went down**
- **Invisibility dropped and she dematerialized. She was a fiend**
- Martavis **spat on the floor** where she had been

### What This Teaches

| Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|
| **Hold Monster landed** | They are not immune to control. [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] can lock one down |
| **Detect Invisibility and See Invisibility both found her** | Standard counter-invisibility works |
| **She dematerialized rather than leaving a corpse** | **Killed off her home plane, a fiend re-forms there.** She is not permanently dead, and she knows the party's faces |
| **Martavis regretted "the other four got away"** | He was counting. He knew there were five |

## The Four At Large

**Four shapechangers with charm, telepathy, and dream access walked out of a Zhent command camp that the party is now travelling away from with its former occupants.**

They were embedded with:
- [[General Martavis|Martavis]] -- **who was charmed during the fight** and broken out of it by [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]]'s kiss
- Martavis's surviving ~20 riders
- The surrendered Zhent spellcasters, including [[Eldrick]]
- [[Samus]]'s personal troops -- **all of whom Martavis then executed**

> **This is the strongest available explanation for the [[General Martavis|Martavis]] anomalies** -- the regeneration [[Vaelin]] observed, the fact that he needs no healing, and the hard turn in his behavior. **It is not the only explanation.** See [[General Martavis]] for the full case both ways, and the list of checks nobody has run.

## Countermeasures That Have Actually Worked

- **Hold Monster** -- landed, Session 14
- **Detect Invisibility / See Invisibility** -- found her
- **[[Vaelin]]'s Twilight Sanctuary** -- strips charm at the end of every turn. It ran the whole of Session 14
- **[[Viseli Bar|Viseli]]'s [[Cloak of Tranquility]]** -- advantage on CHA saves plus a Rage-fuelled auto-success. **The party's most charm-resistant member**
- **[[Kane]]'s Divine Sense** -- detects fiends within 60 ft, no save. **Free, and nobody has used it on Martavis**


## Succubus Capabilities
- **Shapechanging** - Can assume any humanoid form
- **Charm** - Supernatural ability to influence and control
- **Dream Manipulation** - Can invade dreams
- **Life Drain** - Feed on life force through physical contact
- **Telepathy** - Can read surface thoughts
- **Ethereal Travel** - Can move between planes

## The Pencheska Conspiracy
*Forgotten Realms Canon:* A succubus named Pencheska impersonated [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] as part of a [[Zhentarim]] plot. The real Lady Morwen was held prisoner while Pencheska ruled in her place, furthering Zhent interests.

### Signs to Watch For
- Behavioral changes in key figures
- Decisions that benefit the [[Zhentarim]] from unexpected sources
- People acting against their established interests
- Unexplained influence over powerful individuals

## Potential Infiltration Targets
- [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] - Ruler of Daggerford (canon target)
- [[Daggerford Council]] members
- [[Captain Hidar]] - Lady Morwen's trusted officer
- Key military figures
- The party itself?

## Countermeasures
- True Seeing reveals true form
- Protection from Evil prevents charm
- Dispel Magic/Dispel Evil might force form change
- Direct confrontation in private is dangerous
- Public revelation could cause panic or be denied

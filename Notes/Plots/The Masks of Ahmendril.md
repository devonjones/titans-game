# The Masks of Ahmendril

**Type:** Artifact class / resurrection mechanism / command structure
**Maker:** [[Ahmendril]] -- **an expert mask maker**
**Revealed:** [[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]], via two **Legend Lore** castings

## ⚠️ A DEVIL IS OWED THEM (Session 16)

The pit fiend at [[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]] opened on the party with:

> ## ***"Have you brought the masks? The masks I am owed?"***

**See [[The Devil's Claim]].** Short version:

- **[[Samus]]'s devil contract was paid in masks.** The brothel and its five succubi were what he *received*; the masks were what he *owed*
- **His [[Samus's Bargain|*"bring me their helms"*]] offer was debt collection** — he wanted the party to source masks for him to hand over
- **The chain had two ends:** [[Ahmendril]] held the resurrection, a devil held a claim on the objects
- **The debt may follow the mask.** He asked the *party* whether they had brought them, then **ordered them searched** — and [[Kane]] is carrying [[Samus's Helm|the ram]]
- **It suggests the masks may be infernal in origin**, which would explain why [[The Goat Head Mask|the goat mask]] was *recovered rather than crafted*, why a [[Chosen of Mystra]] couldn't destroy it, and the **non-spell darkness** shared by [[Samus]] and [[The Assassin]]

**[[Ahmendril]] then appeared in person.** The mask maker turned up to a fight about masks.

## The Mechanism

**Two visions. Two lieutenants. Two eras. The same three beats:**

| | [[Samus]] (Ram) | [[Farraday]] (Raven) |
|---|---|---|
| **Recruited** | Defeated in single combat; **surrender accepted**; negotiation; mask crafted | **Killed a [[Zanithar]]**; [[Ahmendril]] came to her house for **tea**; agreement; mask crafted |
| **Killed** | Paladin stabs him in the chest, **angel in the distance** | Stabbed in the chest by a **drow paladin, white hair, blue fist on her shield** |
| **Returned** | Fade to black -- then **kneeling before [[Ahmendril]], wearing the mask** | **The era changed** -- then **kneeling before [[Ahmendril]]** |

**The mask is the anchor. Kill the wearer and they come back through it.**

### The interval

[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] dated the masked-cabal battle to **about 100 years ago**, with **a gap of about 80 years** before [[Farraday]] reappears.

**Leading read: resurrection by mask takes roughly eighty years.**

Consequences if that holds:
- **[[Samus]] is not coming back this campaign.** He died in [[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar|Session 14]]; the party's regret about not dismembering the body was misplaced
- **[[Farraday]] has only been back about twenty years**
- **[[Bathere]]** died in [[Session 07 - The Door of the Dead|Session 7]]. Decades out -- **unless [[The Goat Head Mask|the goat mask]], which is older and stranger than the others, runs on different rules**

*(Ambiguity: "the battle" could instead be the childhood battle that orphaned Farraday. Worth pinning down at the table.)*

## He Does Not Make Them All

**[[Farraday]] recovered the goat mask.** Her crew scaled a mountain, waited for **a dragon to leave**, entered the lair, and found the mask **settled in a pool of acid** -- *"well-hidden."* They brought it back to [[Ahmendril]].

**Someone concealed that mask deliberately.** In acid. In a dragon's lair. On a mountain.

**So there is a collection program.** Ahmendril crafts some masks and **sends lieutenants to recover others.** Which reframes the other two:

- **[[Celine]]** -- Astral Plane
- **[[Draymon]]** -- [[Chult]]

Those look less like garrison postings and **more like search assignments.**

**Acid pool means black dragon.** The campaign has one: **[[Ravica]]** -- ancient, black, from the [[The Dragoncult Wars|Dragoncult Wars]] era, revived by Ahmendril, killed by the [[The Tarrasque|Tarrasque]], corpse harvested and under Gentle Repose in [[Waterdeep]]. **Did Ahmendril later resurrect the dragon whose lair his lieutenant had robbed?**

## Recruitment: He Buys The People Who Beat Him

He defeated [[Samus]] and **accepted his surrender** rather than killing him. He came to [[Farraday]]'s home **for tea** after she killed a [[Zanithar]] -- one of his own predecessors.

**He does not command loyalty. He purchases it with resurrection.**

These are **contractors on a very long contract**, not fanatics. Which is exactly why [[Samus]] spent [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] trying to break his -- *"[[Ahmendril]] has his chain."*

> **THE CHAIN IS THE MASK.** [[Kane]] asked Samus directly why Ahmendril held his chain and got a deflection. The answer was hanging on Samus's own face.

## Why "Bring Me Their Helms" Was Literal

[[Samus]] priced [[Samus's Bargain|his bargain]] in **helms**, not heads. Not a euphemism.

**If the mask is the return anchor, then taking a lieutenant's mask is the only way to kill one permanently** -- and it is precisely what a chained man would ask for if he wanted his colleagues gone for good rather than gone for eighty years.

**[[Kane]] is carrying [[Samus's Helm|Samus's mask]] right now.**

## Practical Notes

- **Non-detection is in the masks.** [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]]'s locate-the-mask plan is dead; so is Scrying on the object. [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] declined the alternative of targeting items from Farraday's house
- **Legend Lore works on them.** The ram gave a full biography on the first cast. **[[Notes/Items/The Door of the Dead|The Door]] is warded against Legend Lore** per [[The Apotheosis Codex|Bathere's book]] -- **the masks are not.** Cast on the rest before the enemy closes that gap
- **Next target: [[The Goat Head Mask]].** It is in [[Waterdeep]] and the party is in Waterdeep
- **He is a master craftsman.** That is a findable trade. Materials, reputation, buyers -- **[[Neverwinter]], [[Baldur's Gate]], [[Waterdeep]]**. Masks are traditional at **Midsummer**

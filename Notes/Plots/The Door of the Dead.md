# The Door of the Dead

**Type:** Fragment of a sealed gateway to the **Far Realm**
**Status:** In the possession of [[Ahmendril]]
**Origin:** Constructed by the **Dead Three** (Bane, Bhaal, Myrkul)
**Introduced:** [[Session 07 - The Door of the Dead]]
**Reframed:** [[Session 11 - When the World Burns]]

## What It Is (Session 11 Reveal)

Per [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s briefing in [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]]:

> *The Dead Three made a magical gateway to the **Far Realm** -- a place of the **old gods**, a place of **raw chaos and power**. They got in over their heads, **closed the door, and spread the pieces around** the world. The Door of the Dead is **one of those pieces**. [[Ahmendril]] holding it should be investigated further.*

**The Door of the Dead is not a Dead Three murder-artifact.** It is a **fragment of a sealed Far Realm gateway** that the Dead Three themselves built and then deliberately broke up because they couldn't control what they had opened.

## What This Means

- **Reassembling the pieces opens the door.** Whoever puts them back together has direct access to the Far Realm
- **Ahmendril has one piece.** How many more pieces are there? Where? Who has them?
- **The Dead Three failed to control what they made.** If they couldn't, the chances Ahmendril can are negligible -- but he may not be trying to *control* it. He may be trying to use it as a power conduit, or simply open it as an act
- **The Far Realm = old gods.** This is a cosmic-tier threat, not a Sword Coast-tier political plot. Worldbuilding implication: the campaign's stakes are now apocalyptic

## Known Facts About the Door (Carried Over)
- **Touch it and you die instantly.** The pile of bodies at [[Dragonspear]] is its history
- **Legend Lore failed on it** (per [[Bathere]]'s notes) -- it actively resists divination
- Bathere correctly identified it as Dead Three -- but didn't know it was a gateway piece
- Ahmendril took it from Dragonspear before the party arrived

## Connection to Ahmendril's Other Operations

Ahmendril's known activities now stack up differently:
- **Reviving Rofidian and Ravica** (ancient dragons) -- per [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]] Silverhand briefing
- **Holding a Far Realm gateway piece** (the Door) -- per Session 11 Silverhand briefing
- **Bearing the [[Zanithar]] mantle** -- a recurring role across history
- **Two dragons attacked [[Waterdeep]] at the end of Session 11** -- possibly Rofidian and Ravica revived

The dragon revival may be a **distraction, a power source, or a delivery mechanism** for the Far Realm gateway. Or all three. Or the gateway may be the means and the dragons the actual goal. Either way, **the Door is the higher-priority artifact** -- Silverhand explicitly said so when comparing it to [[The Goat Head Mask]].

## Risks
- **Do not touch it.** Instant death is literal
- **Other pieces likely exist.** Track them. The Pencheska / Zhentarim operations may be partly about acquiring more
- **The Tarrasque rose at the end of Session 11** -- whatever the world-threatening event was, the Door + dragons + ritual situation likely qualifies. The cosmic immune system is responding

6. **Who else knows about the gateway?** [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] does. [[Harpers]] presumably. The Dead Three's priesthoods?

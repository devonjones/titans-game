# Samus's Bargain

**Type:** Negotiation / betrayal
**Status:** **DEAD.** Agreed to, broken at the handshake, and then **[[Samus]] was killed in [[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar|Session 14]].** There is nobody to bring helms to
**Introduced:** [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]]

## The Offer

At the [[Troll Claw Hills]] camp in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]], with [[General Martavis|Martavis]] beaten and the party riding in, **[[Samus]] proposed an alliance**:

> ***"We have mutual problems. Work with me."***
>
> ***"You have already run into some of Ahmendril's lieutenants. Bring me their helms, and I will let Mark go."***

He then **named three of [[Ahmendril]]'s lieutenants unprompted**:

| Lieutenant | Mark | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **[[Farraday]]** | Raven's head mask | [[Waterdeep]] |
| **[[Celine]]** | -- | Astral Plane |
| **[[Draymon]]** | -- | [[Chult]] |

## The Negotiation

[[Kane]] pushed for [[General Martavis|Martavis]]'s immediate release:

- **Kane:** *"We are concerned that your offer did not include releasing Martavis and his troops now."*
- **Samus:** *"We can negotiate that."*
- **Kane:** *"We're going to kill your enemies anyway."*
- **Samus:** *"Bring me the first helmet and I'll release them."*
- **Kane:** ***"No."***

Samus conferred with someone at his side, then **conceded almost everything**:

> ***"You ride out of my camp and I'll send Mark behind you. I don't care which one you kill first, but I recommend Farraday first."***

**Final terms: the party rides out, Martavis follows, no helms required up front.** Samus gave up his hostage leverage for nothing but the party leaving.

## The Betrayal

[[Kane]]: *"Fine. You have a deal."* He walked over and **shook Samus's hand**.

**[[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] then cast [[Synaptic Static]] on Samus.**

Samus rolled a natural 20 on the INT save and *"chose to succeed"* (see [[Samus]] -- possible Legendary Resistance). [[Kane]] grappled him, Hunter's Marked, and went into a smite. The camp erupted.

> ***"Doesn't appear I can count on any honor in here."*** -- [[Samus]], after his attack took out Kane's mirror images

## ⚠️ IT WAS DEBT COLLECTION (Session 16)

**[[Samus]] owed a devil the masks.**

The pit fiend at [[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]] opened on the party with ***"Have you brought the masks? The masks I am owed?"*** — see [[The Devil's Claim]].

**That is what this bargain was for.** Not trophies. Not even primarily killing his rivals. [[Samus]] needed **masks to hand to a devil**, and he wanted the party to be the ones who collected them.

Which finally explains the word everyone kept circling:

> ***"Bring me their helms."***

**Not heads. Helms.** Three lieutenants, three masks, one payment.

And it explains the deflection. [[Kane]] asked *"Why does [[Ahmendril]] have your chain?"* and got *"We have a mutually beneficial arrangement."* **He had two creditors** — [[Ahmendril]] held his resurrection, a devil held a claim on the objects — **and the word "arrangement" covered both.**

## Why Samus Wants This

**[[Ahmendril]] holds [[Samus]]'s chain.** Kane asked directly:

- **Kane:** *"Why does Ahmendril have your chain?"*
- **Samus:** *"We have a mutually beneficial arrangement. It's not your concern."*

He didn't deny the chain. He deflected. Read together with him **volunteering the names and locations of his own ally's command structure**, the picture is clear: **Samus is trying to decapitate Ahmendril's lieutenants using the party as the instrument**, without being seen to move against Ahmendril himself.

This is the first hard evidence that the **Zhentarim / Death Tyrant alliance is not solid**. It is a leash, and Samus resents it.

## What the Party Gained Anyway

Even with the deal broken, the intelligence is **kept**:
- Three named lieutenants with locations -- the best actionable intel on [[Ahmendril]]'s organization the party has ever had
- Confirmation that Ahmendril **has** a lieutenant structure
- Confirmation that Ahmendril **controls** Samus rather than partnering with him
- [[Farraday]] identified as the [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]] raven-masked attacker

## What the Party Lost

- **The deal.** [[General Martavis|Martavis]]'s release was agreed and is now void
- **Credibility.** Samus's *"no honor in here"* line was delivered in front of Martavis, Martavis's men, and the whole Zhent camp
- **A potential defector.** Samus was actively trying to work against Ahmendril. That door is now much harder to reopen
- **The initiative.** The party attacked into a fortified camp with "many casters" inside ([[Vaelin]]'s read) and immediately lost the exchange

## Void (Session 14)

**[[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] killed [[Samus]]** the session after the handshake -- Lightning Bolt through a closed door, decapitation.

**The contract cannot be performed.** No counterparty, no payment, no release of [[General Martavis|Martavis]] to negotiate for (Martavis freed himself when Samus died and claimed the man's army in the same breath).

### What survives

**All of the intelligence.** Samus gave up the names and locations of [[Ahmendril]]'s command structure for free, and dying doesn't take it back:

- **[[Farraday]]** -- raven mask, [[Waterdeep]]. **The party is heading to Waterdeep**
- **[[Celine]]** -- Astral Plane
- **[[Draymon]]** -- [[Chult]]

The party pursues these three now **on its own terms rather than under contract** -- which is arguably a better position, minus the payment.

### What died with him

- **The fracture.** Samus *was* the crack in the [[Ahmendril]] coalition. Killing him welded it shut. This was strategy #7 on [[The Gate of the Far Realms]] and it is gone
- **The chain.** Nobody now living has told the party what [[Ahmendril]] holds over his subordinates
- **[[Ahmendril]]'s location.** Samus was the only mortal source who plausibly knew where a man who *"moves around a lot"* actually is
- **The intelligence source generally.** [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]] framing -- *if captured alive, Samus is the next intelligence source on Ahmendril* -- is now moot

### And the thing nobody priced in

**Samus had a second creditor.** He wanted the party to kill Ahmendril's lieutenants because Ahmendril held his chain -- but he **also had a devil contract** on the brothel, and *that* is what killed the camp. **He was leveraged twice**, and the party only ever knew about one of the loans. See [[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]].

### The helms, revisited

He asked for **helms**, not heads. [[Kane]] then took **[[Samus's Helm|Samus's own helm]]** off his corpse after Samus **refused to say whether it was cursed** -- and felt foreboding. The word choice may have been literal. See [[Samus's Helm]].

# The Time of Troubles

**Type:** Historical event (background lore)
**First Referenced:** [[Session 11 - When the World Burns]] ([[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s Dead Three briefing)

## What It Was

A cataclysmic event in Forgotten Realms history -- the **gods were cast out of the heavens and forced to walk the earth as mortal Avatars**. Many gods died during this period; others were elevated; the cosmology was rewritten. Sometimes called **the Avatar Crisis** or **the Godswar**.

[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] referenced this event in her [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] Dead Three briefing -- the Dead Three's history involves it (Bane, Bhaal, and Myrkul all **died at the Time of Troubles**, with various replacements and eventual returns).

## Relevance to the Campaign

- **The [[The Dead Three|Dead Three]] died and returned** as part of the Time of Troubles -- the institutional knowledge that exists about their cults includes the post-Troubles reconstitution
- **Mystra died** at the Time of Troubles, was replaced/reincarnated, then died again in later events (the **current campaign timeline has Mystra dead** -- per Session 5 GM note)
- **Cyric** ascended during the Time of Troubles, briefly held Myrkul's portfolio of death, and is responsible for some of the chaos around the Dead Three's apparent "return" arc
- **The Chosen of Mystra** were created/empowered around this period -- [[Laeral Silverhand]]'s history runs through it

## Why Cael Cares (Possibly)

- The Dead Three's **[[The Far Realm|Far Realm gateway]]** may date from before, during, or after the Time of Troubles. Knowing which would constrain who might have records
- The current [[Zanithar]] cycle -- if it pre-dates the Time of Troubles, it survived a cosmic reset, which would tell us something about its mechanism
- **[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s thesis** on the Dead Three almost certainly covers the Time of Troubles aftermath in detail. He is the in-character expert
- The **Cult of the Dragon** (per FR canon) was founded around this era too, by Sammaster

## Forgotten Realms Lore (Brief)

The **Time of Troubles** (also "Avatar Crisis") was a campaign-shaping event in 1358 DR in canonical Forgotten Realms. **Lord Ao**, the overgod, stripped the gods of their divine power and cast them down as mortal Avatars after Bane and Myrkul stole the Tablets of Fate. Cyric and Midnight (the future Mystra reborn) emerged as new powers during the ensuing chaos. The Time of Troubles ended with major shake-ups in the pantheon: Bane, Bhaal, and Myrkul all dying (only to return centuries later in some forms), Mystra dying and being replaced by the mortal Midnight, Cyric stealing portfolios, and Helm killing Mystra at Ao's command.

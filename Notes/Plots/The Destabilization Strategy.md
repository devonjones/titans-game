# The Destabilization Strategy

**Type:** Conspiracy Theory
**Status:** Unconfirmed
**Introduced:** [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]

## The Theory
The [[Zhentarim]] are not merely responding to regional chaos - they are *creating* it. The organized monster tribes, [[Dalelands]] civil war, and increased banditry are coordinated efforts to destabilize the Sword Coast and position the Zhents as the solution.

## Evidence

### Suspicious Coordination
- Monster tribes are described as "organized" - unusual behavior
- [[Zhentarim]] happened to have hundreds of soldiers ready for deployment
- Banditry up precisely on routes that would justify Zhent patrols
- Patrols absent from main roads before Zhents arrived

### Strategic Benefits
If the [[Zhentarim]] are behind the chaos, they gain:

1. **Overwhelmed Resources** - [[Daggerford]] can't handle the refugee influx
2. **Social Division** - Locals angry at migrants, population fighting itself
3. **Justified Presence** - Security contract seems necessary, even welcome
4. **Weakened Leadership** - [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] can't solve the crisis
5. **Economic Control** - Taxes "stolen by bandits," trade routes unsafe

### The Succubi Connection
[[Kane]]'s intelligence confirms [[Zhentarim]] have succubi allies. Succubi excel at:
- Infiltration and impersonation
- Manipulation of powerful figures
- Creating conflict and division
- Long-term destabilization

*Note: The Pencheska Conspiracy from FR lore involves a succubus impersonating [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] for the [[Zhentarim]].*

## The Play
1. Create chaos across the region
2. Drive refugees toward target location
3. Offer "security" to overwhelmed authorities
4. Eliminate opposition ([[Councilman Moore]])
5. Control the council through [[Viggio Martel]] and others
6. Complete takeover of [[Daggerford]]

## Session 2 Evidence
- [[Merron]]'s bandits were war refugees turned criminals - the crisis is creating its own secondary problems
- [[Zhentarim]] arrived at the bandit camp with 100 cavalry *before* the party - suspiciously fast response
- [[General Martavis]] tried to claim the recovered taxes - Zhents positioning themselves as essential
- Zhents running soup kitchens and maintaining order - winning hearts and minds while consolidating control
- The [[Daggerford Council]] (who hired the Zhents) likely directed the tax recovery operation

## Session 3 Evidence
- The missing fingers may be spell components — if so, the murders are part of a larger magical operation, not purely political assassination
- Viggio keeping the fingers at his manor during the party suggests active use, not just evidence disposal
- A hidden passage discovered in Viggio's sitting room — the manor has secret infrastructure. Pre-planned or long-standing?

# The Avernus Portal

**Type:** Sealed Infernal Portal / Strategic Liability
**Location:** Beneath [[Dragonspear]] Castle (cellars)
**Status:** **Sealed** — held closed by a relic in the fountain statue out front
**Introduced:** [[Session 07 - The Door of the Dead]]

## What It Is
The historic portal to **Avernus** (first layer of the Nine Hells) beneath Dragonspear Castle. This is the portal that made Dragonspear one of the most dangerous places on the Sword Coast for centuries. It has been opened and closed multiple times through Faerûnian history.

**Currently sealed.** But the seal is fragile in the sense that it depends on a specific object, not on an active spell or ward.

## How It's Sealed
- A **statue in the fountain** outside the castle is a **relic** that keeps the portal closed
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] and [[Vaelin]]'s History checks confirmed: one of the historic wars fought at Dragonspear involved this portal being opened. It was eventually closed and bound by this relic
- As long as the fountain statue remains intact and in place, the portal stays shut
- **[[The Apotheosis Codex|Bathere's spellbook]] contains Gate Seal** (4th-level abjuration) — a spell that literally seals portals. It's possible Bathere was reinforcing the fountain's relic with Gate Seal as a backup, or preparing to seal/unseal the portal on his own terms. Either way, the fact that he had this spell in his book is not a coincidence

## Why This Matters to Us
- **[[Bathere]] may have been guarding this portal** as part of his role at Dragonspear — possibly for infernal patrons or as a contingency for his [[Dracolich Research|ritual]]
- **The castle is now ours,** which means the relic is now our responsibility
- **Anyone who breaks the fountain opens hell.** This is not a fast seal to replace
- The political implications are real — [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|Lady Morwen]] and the broader region have a strong interest in Dragonspear remaining sealed

## Risks
- **Don't disturb the fountain.** Ever. Under any circumstances
- **Someone else might know about the relic.** If our enemies learn Dragonspear is lightly held, they may try to break the seal
- **Avernus has its own agents** — devils, cultists, diabolists — who would benefit from this portal reopening

## Open Questions

3. Should we move the relic, or reinforce it in place?
4. Should we tell the Duchess, the Harpers, the Emerald Enclave, or all three?

## ⚠️ A Second Door Opened Nearby (Session 14)

**[[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]]** opened in the [[Troll Claw Hills]] when [[Samus]] died -- an infernal breach with a **Pit Fiend** climbing out of it, **unwatched**, on the Trade Way. The party rode past it and arrived here within a day.

This changes the risk profile on the fountain relic:

- **Devils now have a foothold one day's ride from Dragonspear.** The "someone else might know about the relic" risk stops being hypothetical
- Reopening Dragonspear has historically been an *objective* for infernal forces, not an accident
- The party's own note above says it plainly: **"Anyone who breaks the fountain opens hell."** Someone with a reason to want that is now nearby and numerous

**[[The Apotheosis Codex|Bathere's spellbook]] contains Gate Seal** (4th-level abjuration). It was already flagged as suspicious that Bathere had it. With two hell-doors in the region and the party holding the book, **it is now a tool worth actually evaluating** -- both for reinforcing this seal and for the new gate.

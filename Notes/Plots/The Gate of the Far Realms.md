# The Gate of the Far Realms

**Type:** Composite artifact -- three-piece sealed Far Realm gateway built by the [[The Dead Three|Dead Three]]
**Working name:** "The Gate of the Far Realms" (tentative; per [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]] briefing)
**Status:** **[[Ahmendril]] has 2 of 3 pieces.** Only the Key remains uncollected (and is **divinely protected** from being located -- see [[The Planetar's Warning]])

## The Three Pieces

| Piece | God | Status | Location |
|-------|-----|--------|----------|
| **[[Notes/Items/The Door of the Dead\|Door]]** | **Bhaal or Myrkul** (best guess Myrkul -- death effect) | In [[Ahmendril]]'s possession | Wherever Ahmendril is ("moves around a lot") |
| **[[Notes/Items/The Stolen Keystone\|Keystone]]** | Bhaal or Myrkul (whichever isn't the Door); possibly Bane (load-bearing authority) | In [[Ahmendril]]'s possession (Session 11 theft, Session 12 confirmation) | With Ahmendril |
| **[[Notes/Items/The Key\|Key]]** | Bhaal, Myrkul, or Bane (whichever isn't taken by the other two) | **Unfound; divinely hidden** | Unknown; planetar refuses to disclose |

[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]] briefing:

> *"The bad guy already has two of the three pieces. The only part missing is the Key."*

She also stated each piece is **connected to one of the Dead Three** -- and **the Door is NOT Bane** (Tyranny). So Bane is either the Keystone or the Key.

## What It Does

Per [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] briefing: it is a **gateway to the [[The Far Realm|Far Realm]]** -- "a place of the old gods, raw chaos and power."

The Dead Three built it, **got in over their heads**, closed it, and **scattered the pieces** to prevent reassembly. Reassembling all three opens the Gate.

## Divine Opposition

In [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]], a **planetar appeared** in response to [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s 4th-level Divination ritual to locate the Key. The planetar explicitly **forbade the search**.

See [[The Planetar's Warning]] for the full exchange. Bottom line:
- **Find the Door and Keystone** (sanctioned -- "seek the one who stole them")
- **DO NOT search for the Key directly** (forbidden by divine authority)
- The implication: **the Key may not be intended for mortal handling at all**

## Who's Trying to Reassemble It

- **[[Ahmendril]]** (current [[Zanithar]]) -- has the Door and Keystone; presumably working to acquire the Key, or believing he already has it
- **[[Samus]]** -- runs Ahmendril's mortal operations; the party is en route to him as of session end
- **The [[Cult of the Dragon]]** -- resurrected [[Rofidian]] and [[Ravica]]; presumably allied or in coordination
- **The [[The Pencheska Conspiracy|Pencheska Conspiracy]]** -- likely the keystone-theft operatives in [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]]

## How To Stop It

The strategic options:

1. **Take the pieces from Ahmendril** -- recover the Door and Keystone. With the Key divinely hidden, removing two of three prevents the assembly. But Ahmendril has antimagic via his eye and "moves around a lot"
2. **Kill the [[Zanithar]] mantle** -- but a new Zanithar takes over. Doesn't end the threat
3. **Find and secure the Key first** -- explicitly forbidden by divine authority. Off the table unless we accept the cost
4. **Destroy individual pieces** -- requires understanding the artifact-tier destruction problem ([[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] couldn't destroy [[The Goat Head Mask]] -- comparable difficulty likely)
5. **Petition the gods directly** for active intervention -- the planetar suggests divine forces are willing to gatekeep but not (yet) to act offensively
6. **Stop the activation site** -- if assembly + ritual is required at a specific place ([[Mount Hotenow]]?), holding that site denies the completion even if the pieces are assembled

# Martavis's Emblem

**Type:** Mystery / Identity
**Status:** Unresolved
**Introduced:** [[Session 02 - Riverdale]]

## Summary
[[Kane]] noticed a distinctive emblem on [[General Martavis]]'s jacket, repeated on all his soldiers' jackets. Kane recognizes these soldiers from the Thayan civil war - they were nominally [[Zhentarim]], hired by the traditional [[Red Wizards of Thay]]. But while Kane remembers the Zhents showing up for the actual battle, he doesn't recall the soldiers wearing *this particular emblem* being present for the real fighting.

## What Kane Knows
- The emblem is on Martavis and all his soldiers
- The [[Zhentarim]] were hired by the traditional [[Red Wizards of Thay]] during the civil war
- Kane was there as a war-slave with [[The Ashen Spears]]
- The Zhents showed up for the battle
- But the soldiers with this emblem were **not at the real fight**

## Implications
This raises several disturbing possibilities:
1. **Martavis's unit is a splinter faction** - Not mainstream Zhentarim, but something else wearing the brand
2. **They were doing something else during the war** - If not fighting, what were they doing in [[Thay]]?
3. **They're connected to the Red Wizards differently** - Perhaps allied with a specific faction within Thay
4. **The emblem marks a specific operation or sub-group** - One with a different purpose than frontline combat
5. **They may not be "real" Zhentarim** - Using the name for cover

## What Were They Doing Instead?
If they weren't at the battle, possible activities:
- Intelligence gathering
- Infiltration operations
- Slave trading or trafficking
- Necromantic research with Red Wizard allies
- Establishing trade routes or enclaves
- Something worse

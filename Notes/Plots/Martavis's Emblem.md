# Martavis's Emblem

**Type:** Mystery / Identity
**Status:** Resolved (Session 9)
**Introduced:** [[Session 02 - Riverdale]]

## Summary
[[Kane]] noticed a distinctive emblem on [[General Martavis]]'s jacket, repeated on all his soldiers' jackets. Kane recognized these soldiers from the Thayan civil war -- they were nominally [[Zhentarim]], but were NOT present for the real fighting.

## Resolution
**The emblem marks a Harper infiltration team**, not a Zhentarim command unit. Martavis and his soldiers were embedded within the Zhentarim as deep-cover [[Harpers|Harper]] agents during the Thayan war. That's why Kane didn't see them at the actual fighting -- they weren't there to fight for the necromancers. They were there to spy on them.

Kane's identification was correct (he recognized the emblem from Thay), but his interpretation was wrong. In Session 8, he believed Martavis was a necromancer commander. In Session 9, Martavis revealed his Harper pin, resolving the mystery entirely.

## What Kane Knew (and Misread)
- The emblem is on Martavis and all his soldiers -- **correct** (they're all Phoenix/Harper)
- The soldiers with this emblem were NOT at the real fight -- **correct** (they were infiltrators, not combatants)
- Kane assumed they were command/logistics for the necromancers -- **wrong** (they were Harper agents embedded behind enemy lines)

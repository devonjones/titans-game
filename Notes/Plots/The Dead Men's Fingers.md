# The Dead Men's Fingers

**Type:** Murder Mystery
**Status:** Closed — fingers recovered, evidence presented, murder ordered by "S" (Samus)
**Introduced:** [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]
**Closed:** [[Session 05 - Signed S]]

## Summary
Multiple bodies in [[Daggerford]]'s morgue are missing their right index fingers, cut off post-mortem. The most recent victim is [[Councilman Moore]], an ally of [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]].

## Key Facts
- [[Councilman Moore]] killed by a single stab wound in the back
- Front chest wounds show "stabby" overkill - suggests passion or message
- Right index finger removed post-mortem
- Moore believed [[Viggio Martel]] was responsible (Speak with Dead)
- Moore did not see his killer
- Moore claims nothing was on the finger

## Evidence
- Multiple other bodies also missing index fingers (pattern)
- Morgue dwarf asked if [[Vaelin]] could locate objects (fingers? rings?)
- **Locate Object (Session 3):** Confirmed the fingers are inside [[Viggio Martel]]'s manor. Narrowing to the dwarf victim's finger specifically still pings. At [[Viggio's Party|Viggio's party]], the spell pinged on the **ground floor** — the fingers were present during the party itself.
- **GM hint:** The fingers may be part of a spell. Possibly related to bounties.

## Suspects
- **[[Viggio Martel]]** - Moore named him as his enemy and suspected killer
- Unknown assassin(s) working for Viggio?

## Other Victims (Session 2)
[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] revealed the other victims were:
- A seamstress
- A worker

Nothing in common with [[Councilman Moore]] - no obvious pattern connecting the victims by profession or status.

## Resolution (Session 4)
- **Fingers physically recovered** from a locked chest in Viggio's secret room — satisfies [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|Lady Morwen]]'s demand for proof
- Chest also contained **other unidentified items**
- [[Viggio Martel]] formally accused of [[Councilman Moore]]'s murder; [[Captain Hidar]] notified
- **But the deeper question remains:** what were the fingers FOR? The [[The Dead Three Temple|Dead Three temple]] below Viggio's manor suggests they were ritual components. The case is closed on "who" — but not on "why" or "what was accomplished."

## Open Questions
1. Why take the fingers? Trophy? Key? Magical component? Bounty proof?
2. What do the victims have in common (if not profession)?
3. Is there a pattern to the killings beyond the finger removal?
4. What is the significance of the right index finger specifically?
5. Could the non-Council victims be cover for the political assassination?
6. Why keep the fingers at the party? Is a ritual happening soon?
7. What is the spell (if any) that requires right index fingers?

## Connections
- [[Viggio Martel]] - Primary suspect
- [[Councilman Moore]] - Latest victim, Lady Morwen's ally
- [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] - Commissioned the investigation
- [[Captain Hidar]] - Party reports to him

## Session Appearances
- [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]] - Investigation begun, Speak with Dead performed
- [[Session 02 - Riverdale]] - Lady Morwen revealed other victims (seamstress, worker); no obvious pattern
- [[Session 03 - Through the Wall]] - Locate Object confirmed fingers at Viggio's manor; ground floor ping during the party; Lady Morwen demands physical proof
- [[Session 04 - Good Timing]] - **Fingers recovered.** Viggio accused and fled. Dead Three temple discovered below — fingers were almost certainly ritual components

Case closed on the physical evidence.

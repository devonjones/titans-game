# The Pencheska Conspiracy

**Type:** Replacement conspiracy -- targeting nobles for substitution
**Status:** Active in [[Waterdeep]] (confirmed [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]])
**Introduced (named):** [[Session 08 - Running the Gauntlet]] (Pencheska angle inferred)

## What It Is

A conspiracy to **replace ruling nobles** with substitutes -- likely shapechangers, doppelgangers, mind-controlled bodies, or simply puppet successors. Discovered via:

- **[[Viggio Martel|Viggio]]'s true mission was to replace [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|the Duchess]]** ([[General Martavis|Martavis]] revealed this in [[Session 09 - The Wolf and the Phoenix|Session 9]] after killing Viggio)
- This matches the canon Forgotten Realms "Pencheska" pattern -- nobles being swapped out for conspiracy assets

## Confirmed Operations

### Daggerford
- [[Viggio Martel|Viggio]] was being positioned to **replace [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|Lady Morwen]]**
- The [[Zhentarim]] occupation was the cover -- create a security crisis, then have the council "agree" to alternative leadership
- Killed by [[General Martavis|Martavis]] before he could execute -- the replacement was preempted

### Waterdeep (Session 10)
- **Night attack on the Ellenfear estate** -- assassins/operatives in raven masks, leather, organized
- Hit while the party was unprotected, sleeping
- Targeted [[Cael Stormcrow|Cael]] specifically (he went down)
- Used poison, force damage, magical mobility
- Withdrew when the fight turned -- not a suicide mission, an extraction or kill-and-retreat
- **[[Vaelin]]'s Divination** earlier warned that staying in his castle quarters tonight would be "a terrible idea" -- the attackers may have hit there too, or chose targets the party would have been split from

## Methods (Observed and Inferred)
- **Raven masks** -- a visual signifier (Pencheska iconography? Or generic anonymity?)
- **Light, fast, lethal** -- assassins with magical support
- **Coordination** -- multiple operatives, multiple rooms, simultaneous entry
- **Magic** -- one used force damage; [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] had to counterspell
- **Withdrawal under pressure** -- they extracted via window/banister rather than dying in place
- **Pre-positioning intel** -- they knew where the party would be sleeping (despite Vaelin's last-minute change of plan)

## Connections

- **[[Zhentarim]]** -- [[Viggio Martel|Viggio]] was a Zhent operative AND a Pencheska replacement target. Are the two operations the same, or did Pencheska piggyback on Zhent infrastructure?
- **[[Ahmendril]]** -- Commands [[Samus]] who ran the Daggerford op. Is Pencheska under Ahmendril's umbrella, or a separate organization riding the same wave?
- **[[Waterdeep]]** -- Active here now; [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] may or may not be a target / replacement risk
- **[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s family** -- the attack happened at the [[Ellenfear Manor|Ellenfear estate]]; was the family targeted, or just the party as guests?

## Open Questions
- **Is there a Pencheska in the [[Waterdeep]] council right now?** [[Dagult Neverember|Neverember]] and [[Thrun]] are unverified -- one of them could be a replacement already
- **Is the household guard at the Ellenfear estate compromised?** The attackers got past them
- **What's the relationship between Pencheska and the [[Zanithar]] / [[Ahmendril]] arc?** Top-down? Coincidental? Co-conspirators?
- **Are the raven masks meaningful?** Iconography of an actual organization, or operational anonymity?
- **Who runs Pencheska?** Has it been identified or is it still a shadow?

## Priority

**High.** The party is now a target. Sleeping arrangements, household security, divination defenses, and counter-intelligence all need to be considered going forward. The Duchess in particular is the highest-value target -- she escaped Daggerford but is now in a city where the conspiracy has demonstrated reach.

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 11 - When the World Burns]] -- Aftermath Review
The Session 10 attackers withdrew successfully. Post-attack analysis at [[Ellenfear Manor]]:
- **Celleborn's parents were not the target** -- they emerged after the fight, unharmed
- **The household guards were killed**
- **The attacker who jumped over the bannister flew away** (suggests magical flight; raven mask iconography may be literal)
- Celleborn suspects other houses in Waterdeep were attacked the same night
- [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s reply to the attack report: just *"meh"* -- she's not surprised
- City guard claim no other reports of attacks ("not that I'm aware of")

The party evacuated to the royal castle for the rest of the long rest. **Tiny Hut becomes standard going forward** (Vaelin's recommendation).

**Possible follow-on:** during the dragon attack, **someone deliberately stole the castle's keystone arch** (see [[The Stolen Keystone]]). The dragon attack may have been cover, the Pencheska may have been the thieves, or both. Or neither.

### [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms]]
**The keystone theft is now strongly linked to [[Ahmendril]]'s operation** -- [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] confirmed Ahmendril holds the keystone. The Pencheska operatives who hit [[Ellenfear Manor]] in Session 10 and the keystone thieves in Session 11 are likely the same operation -- both feeding Ahmendril's [[The Gate of the Far Realms|Far Realm gateway]] assembly. **An arch-wizard** is involved -- the teleportation runes left at the theft site were above [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s reading level. The Pencheska / Ahmendril coalition has wizardly support at the highest tier.

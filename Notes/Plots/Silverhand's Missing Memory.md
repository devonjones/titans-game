# Silverhand's Missing Memory

**Subject:** [[Laeral Silverhand]] -- Open Lord of [[Waterdeep]], **[[Chosen of Mystra]]**, one of the Seven Sisters
**Discovered:** [[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]]
**Status:** **Unresolved.** [[Vaelin]]'s Divine Intervention failed. She named **Wish** as the fix

## What Happened

In conversation at the [[Waterdeep]] castle, it became apparent that **[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] may have been charmed into forgetting something important.**

**[[Vaelin]] had her doodling.**

**She drew an owl mask.**

> ***"What is the thing that you haven't asked for, that might help?"***

The party floated **Greater Restoration** and **Heal**.

**She suggested Wish.**

**[[Vaelin]] attempted Divine Intervention to restore her memory. It failed.**

## Why This Is The Biggest Problem In The Campaign

**[[Ahmendril]] wears an owl mask** ([[The Masks of Ahmendril]], confirmed the same session).

Her own hand draws his face when she isn't thinking about it. **She has been got at, by him or his, and she does not know what she lost.**

She is:
- The **Open Lord of [[Waterdeep]]** -- head of the city's ruling council
- A **[[Chosen of Mystra]]**, in a timeline where **Mystra is dead**
- A **[[Harpers|Harper]]**, at the top of the [[Lords' Alliance]]
- **The party's single source** for the [[Zanithar]] cycle, the [[The Dragoncult Wars|Dragoncult Wars]], the two discs, [[The Gate of the Far Realms]], and the three-piece architecture

**Everything she has told the party is now suspect by omission.** Not lies -- **gaps.** And the gaps are shaped like whatever [[Ahmendril]] needed her not to say.

## The Fix Is Out Of Reach

| Option | Status |
|---|---|
| **Greater Restoration** (5th) | Floated. Removes charm -- **but not memory alteration** (that's Modify Memory's remedy, and GR doesn't cover it) |
| **Heal** (6th) | Floated. No memory effect |
| **Divine Intervention** | **[[Vaelin]] tried. FAILED.** Retry is once per long rest at his tier |
| **Dispel Magic / Remove Curse** | Not attempted. Worth trying -- cheap |
| **Wish** (9th) | **Her own recommendation.** Nobody in the party is close |
| **Another Chosen / a god** | Her sisters. Storm Silverhand, Alustriel. **Unexplored** |

**She named the 9th-level spell herself.** A Chosen of Mystra assessing her own condition and concluding it takes a Wish is the strongest possible statement about what was done to her.

## What To Actually Do

- [ ] **Retry Divine Intervention.** [[Vaelin]] gets another attempt after a long rest, and it costs nothing to ask
- [ ] **Try the cheap spells anyway** -- Dispel Magic, Remove Curse, Detect Magic on her. Nobody appears to have cast anything *at* her yet
- [ ] **Keep her doodling.** [[Vaelin]]'s trick produced the owl mask. **It is a working channel into whatever is still in there** -- ask again, with paper, about other subjects
- [ ] **Ask her sisters.** Storm Silverhand and Alustriel are Chosen too. This is a family matter and she has not been asked
- [ ] **Go around her.** The **[[Order of the Fist]]** fought [[Ahmendril]]'s masked cabal a century ago ([[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]] vision: a drow paladin with a blue fist shield killed the ram-masked figure). **Silverhand is the reason [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] withheld intel from [[Unthar Froom]] in [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]].** That exclusion was made by a compromised mind -- **re-evaluate it**
- [ ] **Audit what she has told us** against independent sources, especially anything about [[Ahmendril]] personally

## The Exclusion Problem

This is the practical sting.

In [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]], [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] **politely declined to share full intelligence with [[Unthar Froom]]** -- head of the [[Order of the Fist]], a paladin order whose **stated mission is hunting the [[The Dead Three|Dead Three]]** -- **because Silverhand had excluded him from the earlier briefing.**

She never explained why. It went on the record as a trust boundary.

**A century ago, the Order of the Fist was the faction that beat Ahmendril's cabal in open battle.**

If Ahmendril edited the Open Lord's memory, **isolating the party from the one organization with institutional memory of him is exactly the shape of edit you would make.**

## Open Questions

1. **What did she forget?** The only clue is the owl mask
2. **When was it taken?** Before the party ever met her? Between briefings? During the [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] dragon attack, when she flew off alone and her actions were unobserved?
3. **Is it Modify Memory, a charm, a geas, or something a [[Zanithar]] does?** A Chosen of Mystra should be extremely hard to affect
4. **Did she meet [[Ahmendril]] in person?** The doodle suggests she has seen that mask
5. **Why did she exclude [[Unthar Froom]]?** Her own reason, or an implanted one?
6. **Is anything else in her missing?** Memory is not the only thing that can be edited
7. **Is she still being influenced**, or was this a one-time extraction?
8. **Does she know about the masks?** She held [[The Goat Head Mask]] in her hands in [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]], said *"might be a phylactery,"* and **tried to destroy it and failed.** Did she once know exactly what it was?
9. **Where does the party get a Wish?**

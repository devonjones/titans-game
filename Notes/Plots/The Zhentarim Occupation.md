# The Zhentarim Occupation

**Type:** Political Takeover
**Status:** Ongoing
**Introduced:** [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]

## Summary
The [[Zhentarim]] have established a significant military presence in [[Daggerford]] under the guise of a security contract. [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]'s power has been effectively usurped, with hundreds of Zhent soldiers patrolling the streets.

## Current Situation
- Hundreds of [[Zhentarim]] soldiers in half-plate patrol [[Daggerford]]
- [[Daggerford Council]] contracted Zhents for "security"
- [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]'s guards are overwhelmed
- [[General Martavis]] commands the Zhent forces
- Zhents have succubi allies (per [[Kane]]'s intel)

## Key Events
- Council awarded security contract to [[Zhentarim]]
- Town taxes stolen by bandits (convenient timing)
- [[Councilman Moore]] (Lady Morwen's ally) killed
- [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] considering fleeing to [[Waterdeep]]
- Argument witnessed between Lady Morwen and [[General Martavis]]
- Zhents deployed 100 cavalry to recover taxes from [[Riverdale]] bandit camp (Session 2)
- [[General Martavis]] disputed ownership of recovered taxes (Session 2)
- Zhents maintaining order effectively - soup kitchens, patrols (Session 2)

## Chain of Command (Session 2 Theory)
We believe [[General Martavis]]'s orders come from the [[Daggerford Council]] - the people who hired the [[Zhentarim]]. The corrupt council members (especially [[Viggio Martel]]) are directing Zhent operations through the security contract.

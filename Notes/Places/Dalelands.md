# The Dalelands

**Type:** Region (collection of independent dales)
**Region:** North Faerûn, around Cormanthor forest
**Status:** Civil war

## Overview
A region of dense forests and bountiful farmlands comprising loosely-organized independent countries called dales. Currently engulfed in civil war, with [[Zhentarim]] involvement driving refugees toward [[Daggerford]].

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dalelands).*

### Geography
Located around and partially within the Cormanthor forest, west of the Sea of Fallen Stars. Bordered by Cormyr to the southwest, Sembia to the south, and the Desertsmouth Mountains to the northwest.

### The Dales
Includes Archendale, Battledale, Daggerdale, Deepingdale, Featherdale, Harrowdale, The High Dale, Mistledale, Scardale, Shadowdale, and others. Each dale is independent with its own government.

### Government
Not a unified kingdom. Each year at Midwinter, dales send delegates to the Dales Council to debate issues like trade routes, defense pacts against Zhent or Sembian aggression, and border disputes.

### Culture
Dalesfolk seem close-mouthed, suspicious, and reserved to outsiders. Stubborn, backwoods farmers and craftsfolk who maintain control of coveted lands surrounding the great elven forest against all odds.

### Demographics
Population ~602,640 (80% human, 6% drow, 5% half-elves, 4% elves, 2% halflings, 1% gnomes, 1% dwarves).

### Historical Conflicts
Two attempts to unify the Dales failed - Aencar the Mantled King (slain 1044 DR) and Lord Lashan of Scardale (defeated 1356 DR). The region has long been targeted by the [[Zhentarim]].

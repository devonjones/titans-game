# Chult

**Type:** Jungle peninsula, far south of the Sword Coast
**Status:** Unvisited. Known location of **[[Draymon]]**, a lieutenant of [[Ahmendril]]
**Introduced:** [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]]

## Campaign Relevance

Named by [[Samus]] in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] as the location of **[[Draymon]]**, one of [[Ahmendril]]'s three lieutenants. Samus offered to release [[General Martavis|Martavis]] in exchange for the lieutenants' helms.

It is **the most distant of the three lieutenant locations** on the Material Plane (only [[Celine]]'s Astral Plane posting is harder to reach).

## Why an Ahmendril Lieutenant Would Be Here

Chult is **death-magic country** in Forgotten Realms canon. That is not incidental for a lieutenant of a [[Zanithar]] -- a recurring death-tyrant figure assembling a [[The Gate of the Far Realms|Far Realm gateway]] built by the [[The Dead Three|Dead Three]].

Worth investigating whether Draymon is in Chult to **acquire, guard, or research** something rather than simply to hide.

## ⚠️ Chultans Are In Waterdeep (Session 16)

In [[Session 16 - The Masks I Am Owed|Session 16]], **[[Vaelin]] noticed people on the streets of [[Waterdeep]] who were disguised or illusory**, appearing to be tourists. Casually touching one, **he felt SCALES.**

**They said they were visitors from Chult, trying not to cause offense with their appearance.**

**Nobody cast anything on them. Nobody got a name.**

Their stated reason is entirely plausible -- a scaled people disguising themselves in a city that has just been attacked by dragons. But **Chult is [[Draymon]]'s posting**, and [[Waterdeep]] is where [[Farraday]] runs [[Ahmendril]]'s operations from [[The Waterdeep Sewers|the sewers]].

**If they are yuan-ti**, that is Dendar, the Soulmonger, and Chultan death-cult infrastructure -- and yuan-ti malison and abomination both have **Disguise Self at will**, matching exactly what Vaelin saw.

See **[[The Chultan Visitors]]**.

## Getting There

- **[[Lords' Alliance]] teleport circle?** The party took the oath in [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]] and has network access. Port Nyanzaru is a major trade port, so a circle is plausible. **Ask [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]** -- this is the cheapest possible answer
- **Sea voyage** -- weeks each way. [[Cael Stormcrow|Cael]] is a former navigator and the party has maritime experience, but the time cost is severe with [[Ahmendril]] holding two of three Gate pieces
- **Teleport** -- requires the spell plus a known destination

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Chult).*

Chult is a tropical peninsula in southwest Faerûn, dominated by dense jungle, dinosaurs, undead, and volcanic terrain. Its major settlement is **Port Nyanzaru**, a free trade city on the northern coast.

Chult is strongly associated with **death magic**: it is the setting of *Tomb of Annihilation*, where the archlich **Acererak**'s Soulmonger drained souls across Faerûn from a tomb beneath the jungle. Chultan religion includes the worship of death and the afterlife alongside a pantheon of nature and trickster deities.

**Relevance:** any death-tyrant operation with a presence in Chult is worth taking seriously. The precedent for continent-spanning soul-draining artifacts hidden in that jungle is canon.

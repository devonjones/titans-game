# Delimbiyr River (The Shining River)

**Type:** River
**Region:** Delimbiyr Vale, flows through [[Daggerford]]

## Overview
A major river that flows through [[Daggerford]], giving the town its name (Tyndal found a ford here). The site of the Daggerford Cleansing where [[Cael Stormcrow]] first proved himself to [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]].

## Connections

### People
- **[[Cael Stormcrow]]:** Stood in the river calling lightning for 6 hours during the Daggerford Cleansing
- **[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]:** Watched from the walls; began trusting Cael afterward

### Organizations
- **[[Emerald Enclave]]:** Druids worked alongside Cael during the cleansing

## Historical Events

### The Daggerford Cleansing (2 years ago)
Aberrant corruption emerged from the river - twisted things trying to use water and weather for unnatural purposes. [[Cael Stormcrow]] walked into the river and called the storm down on himself for six hours straight while [[Emerald Enclave]] druids and [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]'s soldiers dealt with the corruption.

Locals say he stood in a pillar of lightning and never moved.

Afterward, Lady Morwen approached him: *"Are you in control of that, or is it in control of you?"* His answer: *"Yes."*

## Notes
- The river is too shallow at [[Daggerford]] for ships to continue upriver
- Goods transfer from river ships to caravans here
- Named "Shining River" for its appearance
- [[Riverdale]] sits further east up the river

# Ellenfear Manor

**Type:** Noble estate (family seat in [[Waterdeep]])
**Owner:** Ellenfear family ([[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s family)
**Status:** Attacked overnight in [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]] -- security compromised

## Overview

The Waterdeep family estate of the Ellenfears -- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s noble family. The party stayed here on the night of [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]] after delivering [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|the Duchess]] to [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]].

## Layout (Observed)
- Household guard on site
- **Three bedrooms** (made available to the party)
- Multiple entry points (window, bannister, hallway)
- Celleborn's father lives here

## People

- **[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s father** -- present at the estate; warned the household guard at Celleborn's request before the night attack
- Household guards (unnamed)
- Domestic staff (unnamed)

Celleborn previously sent **Icarus** (his owl familiar) to notify the family the party was coming. The reply Icarus brought back:

> ***"Relieved to see Icarus. Glad to know you're safe. We will send an escort."***

The escort met the party at the closer shore (Smuggler's Bay/Dock skipped due to choppy sea) and brought them to the estate after the council meeting.

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars]]
- Party stayed overnight after meeting [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] cast **Alarm** on the bedroom doors and asked his father to warn the guards
- **Night attack** at the estate by Pencheska operatives:
  - Multiple masked, leather-clad assassins
  - Hit at least two bedrooms simultaneously
  - Used poison and force damage
  - **[[Cael Stormcrow|Cael]] went down**; saved by [[Kane]]'s heal
  - Attackers withdrew via window and bannister
  - One mob killed (by Celleborn's summoned fey); rest escaped
- Vaelin's plant **knocked out the window** during the chaos

### [[Session 11 - When the World Burns]] (post-attack)
**After the attack:** Celleborn's parents emerged safely -- they were not the target. **The household guards are dead.** The attacker who jumped over the bannister last session **flew away** (magical mobility). Celleborn's familiar circuited the building; no remaining threats found.

The parents advised the party to sleep in the royal castle instead. **The manor is no longer the party's billet** -- they relocated. Future use uncertain; the household needs to rebuild its security and the estate itself isn't compromised structurally but has lost personnel.

## Security Concerns
- The household guard did not stop the attackers from reaching the bedrooms
- Either the guards were bypassed, distracted, or compromised
- Pencheska had enough intel to know the party was sleeping here, despite the late decision
- Either the family is being watched, or one or more household members are leaking

## Future Use
- Probably still the party's safest known billet in Waterdeep, given the alternative was Vaelin's "terrible idea" castle quarters
- Should investigate the household guard for plants
- Celleborn's father is now a stakeholder in whatever the party does next

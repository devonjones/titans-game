# The Far Realm

**Type:** Cosmic plane / dimension outside reality
**Status:** Sealed by the [[The Dead Three|Dead Three]]; gateway pieces scattered
**Introduced:** [[Session 11 - When the World Burns]]

## What It Is

A plane outside the normal cosmology -- a place of the **old gods**, **raw chaos**, and **raw power**. In Forgotten Realms / D&D cosmology, the Far Realm is associated with **alien intelligences**, **aberrations**, and **incomprehensible horrors** -- the source of mind flayers, beholders, and other "wrong" creatures. Contact with the Far Realm tends to drive mortals mad and warp reality.

In this campaign, [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] revealed that the **[[The Dead Three|Dead Three]] built a magical gateway to the Far Realm** -- and **got in over their heads.** They closed the door and **scattered the pieces** to prevent reassembly.

[[The Door of the Dead]] is one of those pieces. [[Ahmendril]] has it.

## Why This Matters

- **The Dead Three themselves couldn't control what they accessed.** That sets the bar
- **Whoever reassembles the gateway opens it.** The threat is not "ancient evil escapes" but "a place outside reality becomes accessible"
- **Ahmendril holding a piece** is the campaign's new highest stake -- bigger than the [[The Zhentarim Occupation|Zhent occupation]], bigger than the [[The Disk|two-disc dragon revival]], bigger than the [[Zanithar]] cycle (or these may all be steps toward the Far Realm gateway, not separate plans)
- **The Tarrasque rose at the same time** the dragons attacked -- consistent with the world's "immune system" responding to a Far-Realm-tier threat coming online

## Pieces of the Gateway

## Forgotten Realms Lore (Brief)

The **Far Realm** in D&D cosmology is the home of alien, incomprehensible entities -- the great old ones, the elder evils, the consciousness behind aberrations. Contact warps reality and minds. It is **not** a hell, an abyss, or an elemental plane -- it's something stranger, outside the standard wheel of the planes entirely.

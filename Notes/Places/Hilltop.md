# Hilltop

**Type:** Farming Settlement
**Status:** Defended (8 months ago)

## Overview
A farming settlement that was threatened by a hundred-strong gnoll war band eight months ago. The site where [[Kane]] and [[Cael Stormcrow]] first fought together, earning the name "storm and shadow."

## Connections

### People
- **[[Kane]]:** Led [[The Ashen Spears]] at the breach
- **[[Cael Stormcrow]]:** Held the eastern approach with lightning

### Organizations
- **[[The Ashen Spears]]:** Mercenary company that helped defend the settlement

## The Hilltop Defense (8 months ago)
A hundred-strong gnoll war band approached the farming settlement. [[Cael Stormcrow]] called lightning from a clear sky to ignite a warning beacon.

Through the night:
- Cael held the eastern approach with lightning strikes
- [[Kane]] dissolved into ravens and put down eight gnolls in seconds at the breach
- [[The Ashen Spears]] held a spear-wall alongside them

The assault was broken with combined lightning strikes and spear-wall tactics.

## Significance
This was the first time in five years Cael had backup that didn't need explaining or protecting - just coordination. The Spears called them "storm and shadow" when they saw them fight together.

For Cael: Finally had someone who could match his intensity in combat.
For Kane: Found someone who understood being the most dangerous thing on the field.

The name stuck with both of them.

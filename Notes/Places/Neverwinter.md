# Neverwinter

**Type:** Major City
**Region:** Sword Coast (far north of [[Daggerford]])

## Overview
A major city on the northern Sword Coast. Relevant to the campaign because **Mount Hotenow** — the destination for the ritual to raise "the ancient ones" — is located east of Neverwinter.

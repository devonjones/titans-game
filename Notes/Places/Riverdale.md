# Riverdale

**Type:** Town
**Region:** East of [[Daggerford]], up the [[Delimbiyr River]]
**Status:** Recently liberated (was occupied by bandits)

## Overview
A town east of [[Daggerford]] along the [[Delimbiyr River]]. Recently occupied by bandits under a leader named [[Merron]], who stole [[Daggerford]]'s tax revenue. The [[Zhentarim]] swept the bandit camp with 100 cavalry and recovered the taxes.

## Connections

### People
- **[[Merron]]:** Bandit leader, camp a mile north of town
- **[[Talkative Bandit]]:** One of the bandits captured here
- **[[General Martavis]]:** Led the Zhentarim force that cleared the bandit camp

### Organizations
- **[[Zhentarim]]:** Deployed 100 cavalry to recover the taxes
- **[[Daggerford Council]]:** Tax revenue stolen from here

## Key Events

### The Bandit Occupation
Bandits under [[Merron]] took over Riverdale and stole [[Daggerford]]'s tax shipment. A villager escaped and reported to [[Captain Hidar]]. The bandits killed the local smith and terrorized the population.

### The Party's Investigation (Session 2)
The party cleared bandit guards from the bridge and tavern, captured and interrogated prisoners, and learned the location of [[Merron]]'s camp to the north.

### The Zhentarim Recovery
Before the party could reach the camp, [[General Martavis]] arrived with 100 [[Zhentarim]] cavalry. They took no prisoners and recovered a chest believed to contain the tax revenue. The speed and scale of the response raises questions about how the Zhents knew the camp's location.

## Notable Locations
- **The Bridge** - Entry into town, was guarded by bandits
- **The Tavern** - Where the party captured and interrogated bandits
- **The Smith's House** - Family killed by bandits; widow and son remained

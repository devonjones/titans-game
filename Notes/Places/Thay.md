# Thay

**Type:** Nation (Magocracy)
**Region:** East Faerûn
**Status:** Enemy territory

## Overview
A nation ruled by the [[Red Wizards of Thay]], notorious for slavery, necromancy, and magical experimentation. [[Kane]] spent six brutal years here as a war-slave before escaping with [[The Ashen Spears]].

## Connections

### People
- **[[Kane]]:** Conscripted and enslaved here for 6 years (18-12 years ago)
- **[[Margeet Patel]]:** Has ties to the [[Red Wizards of Thay]]

### Organizations
- **[[Red Wizards of Thay]]:** Ruling class
- **[[The Ashen Spears]]:** Formed here as conscripted soldiers, escaped together
- **[[Moonwhisper Caravan]]:** 31 of their men were conscripted here

## Historical Events

### Kane's Conscription (18 years ago)
The [[Moonwhisper Caravan]] took a contract near the Thayan border. [[Red Wizards of Thay|Red Wizards]] intercepted them and demanded all males of fighting age be conscripted. Kane and 30 others volunteered to save the caravan. Only 8 survived (Kane and [[The Ashen Spears]]).

### The Pact (13 years ago)
During a battle against undead, Kane made his pact with the Raven Queen, gaining the power to fight back against the necromancers.

### The Escape (12 years ago)
[[The Ashen Spears]] broke formation during a chaotic battle and fled across the border. They provided intelligence to the [[Lords' Alliance]] and fought for the revolution that overthrew the necromancer regime.

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Wizards_of_Thay).*

### Government
A magocracy ruled by the eight **zulkirs**, each specialized in one school of magic. Chief among them is **Szass Tam**, an undead archmage (lich). Tharchions serve as regional governors.

### Society
- Slavery is fundamental
- Necromancy practiced openly
- Demonic pacts common
- Magical experimentation on living subjects

### Enemies
Constantly at war with Rashemen (thwarted by the Witches), Aglarond (opposed by the Simbul), and Mulhorand (their country of origin).

### Szass Tam's Civil War
Szass Tam's rise as sole ruler sparked civil war. He used the Spellplague to devastate the land and end the conflict through catastrophe.

### Thayan Enclaves
After failed military conquests, Red Wizards pivoted to economic infiltration through enclaves in foreign cities - publicly selling magic items while quietly trafficking in darker goods.

# Smuggler's Dock

**Type:** Waterdeep dockside location
**Status:** Known; party has not used it

## Overview

A docking point in [[Waterdeep]] associated with smuggling traffic -- the obvious approach for parties trying to enter the city quietly. Mentioned by the party as a candidate landing site in [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]] but skipped due to **choppy sea conditions**; landed at a closer shore instead and were met by an Ellenfear escort.

## Known Connections
- Likely the surface-level connection toward **Skullport** (the secret undercity beneath [[Waterdeep]] via Undermountain)
- Standard route for goods avoiding official inspection
- Useful approach for the party in future Waterdeep operations if dockside access is needed

## Session Appearances
- [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars]] -- **Skipped.** Sea too choppy; party landed at a closer shore instead
- [[Session 11 - When the World Burns]] -- The docks were where [[Ravica]] was killed by the [[The Tarrasque|Tarrasque]] -- proximity to Smuggler's Dock unknown but the dockside district as a whole was devastated

# Baldur's Gate

**Type:** Major City
**Region:** Sword Coast (south of [[Daggerford]])

## Overview
A major city on the Sword Coast, south of [[Daggerford]] along the Trade Way. The destination of [[The Moonrunner]]'s merchant run from the [[Moonshae Isles]].

## Connections

### People
- **[[Cael Stormcrow]]:** Worked the merchant run from Callidyrr to here aboard [[The Moonrunner]]
- **[[Kane]]:** Dismantled three slaver rings here while working for the [[Harpers]]

### Organizations
- **[[The Moonrunner]]:** Made regular merchant runs here
- **[[Harpers]]:** Kane conducted operations here
- **[[Moonwhisper Caravan]]:** Travels between here and [[Waterdeep]] along the Trade Way

## Historical Events

### Kane's Harper Work
Over his nine years with the [[Harpers]], Kane dismantled three slaver rings in Baldur's Gate using his caravan-folk connections.

### The Moonrunner's Route
[[The Moonrunner]] worked the merchant run from Caer Callidyrr ([[Moonshae Isles]]) to Baldur's Gate. This was [[Cael Stormcrow]]'s life before the transformation.

## Notes
- Southern terminus of the Trade Way from [[Waterdeep]]
- Major port city and trading hub
- [[Daggerford]] sits between here and [[Waterdeep]]

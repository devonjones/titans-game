# Daggerford

**Type:** Walled Town
**Region:** Sword Coast, Delimbiyr Vale
**Status:** Under Zhentarim occupation (de facto)

## Overview
A small but consequential walled town on the Trade Way between Waterdeep and Baldur's Gate. Currently overrun with refugees and effectively occupied by [[Zhentarim]] forces under the guise of "security."

## Key Locations
- **Castle Daggerford** - [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]'s manor
- **The Morgue** - Adjacent to the cemetery; where [[Councilman Moore]]'s body was examined
- **The Tavern** - Where [[Viggio Martel]] approached the party

- Town overrun with refugees fleeing regional conflicts
- Locals growing angry about the migrant influx
- [[Zhentarim]] patrols everywhere (half-plate, well-equipped)

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Daggerford).*

### Origins
Named for Tyndal, a boy who killed six lizardfolk with only a dagger while finding a ford across the Delimbiyr (Shining River) some 400 years ago. The dukes of Daggerford claim descent from Tyndal; their arms display a bloody silver dagger on a deep blue field.

### Location & Trade
Sits on the Trade Way between [[Waterdeep]] and Baldur's Gate. Vital mercantile hub where goods from river ships transfer to caravans. The waterways of the Delimbiyr at Daggerford are too shallow for ships to continue upriver.

### Government
Founded as part of the Kingdom of Phalorm around 523 DR. Governed by Dukes through Tyndal's family line. Around 1280 DR, Duke Conan granted a town charter creating the [[Daggerford Council|Council of Guilds]], modeled after the Masked Lords of [[Waterdeep]].

### Character
Despite its modest size, Daggerford is resilient - a relic from the old kingdom of Delimbiyr, too stubborn to fall even when wars and crusades threatened the Sword Coast. Has ambitions to be seen as an alternative to [[Waterdeep]].

### The Pencheska Conspiracy
In canonical lore, the succubus Pencheska captured [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] and impersonated her, brokering a deal with the [[Zhentarim]] for the town's "safety." The current situation matches this storyline.

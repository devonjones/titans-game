## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background. Campaign may diverge.*

Dragonspear Castle sits on the Trade Way between [[Daggerford]] and Baldur's Gate, roughly a day's travel south of Daggerford. It is one of the most contested and dangerous locations on the Sword Coast.

### History
Built by the merchant Daeros Dragonspear and his adventuring company, the castle was intended as a waypoint on the Trade Way. However, a portal to Avernus (the first layer of the Nine Hells) was discovered beneath it, turning it into a permanent battleground between mortal forces and fiends pouring through.

The castle has changed hands many times. Hellriders of Elturel once mounted major assaults to seal it. The portal has been opened and closed multiple times. The site has never been truly safe.

### Baelrus's Ghost
A spectral presence (reportedly Daeros Dragonspear himself) is said to haunt the ruins.

### Strategic Importance
- Controls a critical section of the Trade Way
- The portal to Avernus makes it a target for fiend-worshippers and those seeking demonic power
- Its ruins are extensive — underground complexes, dungeons, old temple spaces

### Current Occupant
**The party.** [[Bathere]] was killed in [[Session 07 - The Door of the Dead|Session 7]]. The castle is now held by the party pending a report to [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]].

### Dead Three Connection
Given that the Dead Three (Bane, Bhaal, Myrkul) have connections to death, tyranny, and infernal power, the portal to Avernus beneath Dragonspear makes it a natural site for their operations.

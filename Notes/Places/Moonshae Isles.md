# Moonshae Isles

**Type:** Island Archipelago
**Region:** Sea of Swords (west of Sword Coast)
**Significance:** [[Cael Stormcrow]]'s homeland

## Overview
An archipelago in the Sea of Swords, home to the Ffolk - a druidic people who worship the Earthmother. Cael was born here in Caer Callidyrr on the island of Alaron, and worked the merchant run to Baldur's Gate aboard [[The Moonrunner]].

## Key Locations
- **Caer Callidyrr** - Capital of Alaron, Cael's birthplace
- **Alaron** - Largest island, where Cael grew up

## Connections

### People
- **[[Cael Stormcrow]]:** Born and raised here; left after [[The Moonrunner]] was destroyed

### Organizations
- **[[The Moonrunner]]:** Worked the merchant run from Callidyrr to Baldur's Gate

## Relevance to Campaign
Cael hasn't returned since his transformation. He's not sure he can go back, or what would happen if he did. Some Moonshae expatriates on the Sword Coast recognize him and know the *Moonrunner* story.

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Moonshae_Isles).*

### The Ffolk
The first humans of the Moonshae Isles, sharing the islands with their longtime enemies, the Northlanders. The Ffolk traditionally inhabited the southern parts of the Moonshaes including Alaron.

### Origins
The first Ffolk were Talfiric refugees who settled the Moonshaes. Modern Ffolk are almost entirely of Tethyrian origin, following a large immigration in 467 DR. In 140 DR, refugees fleeing the Shadowking crossed the Sea of Swords and settled on Gwynneth.

### Religion & Culture
The Ffolk are a rare monotheistic culture in the Realms, united under belief in the Earthmother (an aspect of Chauntea). Led by druids rather than clerics. They revere bards but shun and fear wizards. Fiercely independent, valuing self-reliance and finding one's own way.

### The Northlander Conflict
In 256 DR, Illuskan sailors from Tuern and Gundarlun arrived, beginning centuries of raids against the Ffolk. Conflicts frequently erupt as Northmen expand their holdings at Ffolk expense.

### Maritime Tradition
The Moonshaes have a strong seafaring culture. Navigation techniques include carrying ravens or crows in the crow's nest - if released and they fly away, follow their heading to land. This is the origin of [[Cael Stormcrow]]'s name.

# The Waterdeep Sewers

**Type:** Subterranean tunnel network beneath [[Waterdeep]]
**Significance:** **[[Farraday]] lives here.** She has for roughly a century
**Location known:** [[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]] -- [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] gave the party a location for the home seen in the Legend Lore vision

## What The Visions Showed

From [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s Legend Lore on [[Farraday]] ([[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]]):

- She **slips down an alley into the sewer system** from the streets of [[Waterdeep]]
- She is **joined by her friends who dwell in the shadows** -- a crew, not a solo operator
- They move through **complex tunnels**
- They find and destroy **a meeting of underground criminals** -- taking shelter in the shadows, surrounding them, killing them all. *"A long time ago"*
- They continue, **killing along the way**
- Until they reach **a Beholder -- a [[Zanithar]] -- and kill him**

And later, after her return:

- **She goes back into the sewers and sets up a home there. Keeps a low profile**

## ⚠️ Do Not Fight Her Here

**This is the single most defended position [[Farraday]] has.**

- She **cleared these tunnels of an entire criminal syndicate**
- She **killed a beholder [[Zanithar]]** in them -- surrounding it from all angles so its antimagic cone couldn't cover the room
- She has **lived here on and off for about a century**
- Her crew are **shadow-dwellers** and the tunnels are **complex**
- She is a **warlock** with flight, invisibility, poison, force damage, and elemental summoning

**She chose this ground on purpose and she has already won a fight in it against something worse than the party.**

## The Detection Problem

| Tool | Works? |
|---|---|
| **[[Viseli Bar\|Viseli]]'s Commune with Nature** | **No.** *"Doesn't function where nature has been replaced by construction, such as in dungeons and towns"* |
| **Locate Object** on the raven mask | **No.** [[The Masks of Ahmendril\|The masks carry non-detection]] |
| **Scrying** on an item from her house | **[[Laeral Silverhand\|Silverhand]] said no** |
| **Locate Creature** (*"detect person"*) | **Silverhand asked if the party had it. They do.** Blocked if the non-detection is on *her* rather than only the mask |
| **[[Laeral Silverhand\|Silverhand]]'s location** | **Yes -- this is what the party actually has.** A place, from the vision |

**The party is going in on an address, not a ping.**

## Undermountain

Waterdeep's sewers are shallow, but **[[Waterdeep]] sits on Undermountain** -- a dungeon complex descending far past any 1,000-foot detection radius, with **Skullport** on its third level.

If [[Farraday]] goes down instead of out, nothing surface-side reaches her. **Consider what happens if she runs deeper rather than to the street.**

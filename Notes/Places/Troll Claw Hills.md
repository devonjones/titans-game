# Troll Claw Hills

**Type:** Hill region
**Location:** Between [[Baldur's Gate]] and [[Daggerford]], along the Trade Way
**Status:** Zhentarim military buildup in progress

## Overview
A hilly region along the Trade Way between Baldur's Gate and Daggerford. [[Harpers]] intel (Session 6) confirms the [[Zhentarim]] are building a **new headquarters** here and **gathering an army** — an escalation beyond the Daggerford occupation into open military preparation.

## Campaign Significance
- Strategically positioned between two major cities on the Trade Way
- A Zhent army here could threaten both [[Daggerford]] and [[Baldur's Gate]]
- [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] was briefed; told the party to "stay on target" — she's handling this
- The party considers this "above our pay grade"

# Troll Claw Hills

**Type:** Hill region
**Location:** Between [[Baldur's Gate]] and [[Daggerford]], along the Trade Way
**Status:** Zhentarim military buildup in progress

## Overview
A hilly region along the Trade Way between Baldur's Gate and Daggerford. [[Harpers]] intel (Session 6) confirms the [[Zhentarim]] are building a **new headquarters** here and **gathering an army** — an escalation beyond the Daggerford occupation into open military preparation.

## Campaign Significance
- Strategically positioned between two major cities on the Trade Way
- A Zhent army here could threaten both [[Daggerford]] and [[Baldur's Gate]]
- [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] was briefed; told the party to "stay on target" — she's handling this
- The party considers this "above our pay grade"

## Connected To
- [[Zhentarim]] — Building their new HQ here
- [[The Destabilization Strategy]] — Military buildup phase
- [[Harpers]] — Source of the intel (delivered to [[Viseli Bar]])

## Session Appearances
- [[Session 06 - Visitors Visitors]] — Identified via Harper intel as site of Zhent army buildup

### [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars]]
**[[General Martavis]] is en route to the Troll Claw Hills** -- going to confront [[Samus]] at the new [[Zhentarim]] HQ. **Asked the party for help** with Samus once the Duchess is safely in [[Waterdeep]]. Martavis commands the Phoenix cavalry but is going up against the Wolf faction's leadership at their fortified site.

### [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms]]
**Assault begins.** [[General Martavis|Martavis]] attacked the Zhentarim HQ overnight -- did NOT wait for the party. [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] teleported the party to a field 2 miles from the castle. Party rode hard through a makeshift fence gate strewn with bodies from both sides. As they approached the main building they saw **open double doors, combat inside, and [[General Martavis|Martavis]] kneeling**. Cliffhanger.

### [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here]]
**The camp, mapped.** The party rode in and observed the layout:

- **Large pavilion** on the left. A **woman peeked out** of it during the parley. [[Samus]] dismissed it hard: *"She doesn't know shit. It's a brothel."* — a deflection worth revisiting
- Past the pavilion, a **functional-looking building** — probably **barracks**
- Ahead, a **larger rectangular keep or hall** — the main building, **open double doors**, fighting inside
- **Courtyard on the right**, toward the barracks — where **[[General Martavis|Martavis]]'s Phoenix troops were fighting Zhents and winning**

**Inside the keep:** [[Vaelin]] read **many casters**. [[Kane]] recognized **Martavis's owl-mage** among them. Over the fight the keep produced a **Fireball**, an **airborne warlock** with three-beam Eldritch Blast + Repelling Blast, and an unidentified **mind control** that turned [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] against the party.

**What happened here:** [[Samus]] beat Martavis in single combat, then offered the party [[Samus's Bargain|a bargain]]. [[Kane]] shook on it; [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] attacked at the handshake. Samus filled the area with **darkness from his mouth**. The party fought blind until [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] dispelled it. **Combat still in progress at session end.**

### [[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar]]
**THE SITE IS GONE. THERE IS A HOLE TO HELL WHERE IT STOOD.**

[[Samus]] died in the keep -- decapitated by [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]]'s Lightning Bolt through the closed door. Minutes later:

1. **The ground shook.** Five minute warning
2. The party evacuated. **The keep collapsed as they ran**
3. **The ground itself began to collapse.** They kept running
4. **An orange glow** where the building had been
5. **A swarm of small winged creatures** flew up out of it; some flew at the party
6. **[[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] detected a Pit Fiend rising. With friends**

**Why:** [[Samus]] had contracted with a devil to secure the **brothel** -- the pavilion on the left, the one he deflected so hard on in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] -- **as a soul-recruitment operation staffed by five succubi.** Killing Samus called the note.

See **[[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]]**. It is **open and unwatched**, on the Trade Way between [[Baldur's Gate]] and [[Daggerford]], **one day's ride from [[Dragonspear]] and its own sealed [[The Avernus Portal|Avernus portal]] that the party is responsible for.**

**Also resolved here:** the surrendered Zhent spellcasters (including [[Eldrick]], Martavis's captive owl-mage), the first confirmed **succubus** kill of the campaign, and the loot -- [[Frost Brand Maul]], [[Samus's Helm]], +2 plate, a pact blade, 40 sets of full plate, and the troop payroll chest.

**The party is not coming back here.** They rode for [[Daggerford]] and [[Dragonspear]].

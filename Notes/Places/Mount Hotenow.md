# Mount Hotenow

**Type:** Volcanic Mountain
**Location:** East of [[Neverwinter]], Sword Coast (far north of [[Daggerford]])
**Status:** Significant — destination for the ritual to raise "the ancient ones"

## Campaign Significance
The ritual scroll recovered from the [[The Dead Three Temple|Dead Three temple]] beneath [[Viggio Martel]]'s manor names Mount Hotenow as the location where [[The Disk]] must be taken to complete the ritual and "raise the ancient ones." [[The Death Tyrant]] currently possesses the disk.

This is a long way from [[Daggerford]] — the party would need to travel the length of the Sword Coast to reach it.

## Connected To
- **Cult of the Dragon** — the ritual scroll connects the Dead Three operation to the Cult
- **Rofidian & Ravica** — names mentioned in the ritual scroll
- [[The Death Tyrant]] — carrying the disk, presumably heading here (via [[Dragonspear]]?)
- [[The Disk]] — must be brought here to complete the ritual

## Open Questions
- Is [[The Death Tyrant]] heading here directly, or via [[Dragonspear]]?
- What is at Mount Hotenow that enables the ritual?
- What are "the ancient ones"?
- How much time does the party have before the ritual is completed?

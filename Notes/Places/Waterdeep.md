# Waterdeep (City of Splendors)

**Type:** Major City
**Region:** Sword Coast
**Also Known As:** City of Splendors, Crown of the North

## Overview
The most important and influential city in the North, possibly in all Faerûn. A cosmopolitan metropolis of great culture, commerce, and political power. Potential refuge for [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] if Daggerford falls.

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Waterdeep).*

### Importance
The most important city in the North and perhaps all Faerûn. A truly marvelous cosmopolitan city attracting the most talented artisans, artists, and scholars from across the Realms.

### Trade
Hub for trading from mineral-rich northern lands, merchant kingdoms of Amn and Calimshan to the south, Inner Sea kingdoms to the east, and sea traders to the west. Named for its outstanding natural deepwater harbor.

### Government
Ruled by a cryptocracy of anonymous individuals known as the Masked Lords of Waterdeep. The Open Lord (currently Laeral Silverhand, one of the Seven Sisters and a Chosen of Mystra) rules openly and leads the [[Lords' Alliance]].

### History
Established by 1032 DR when Ahghairon became the first Lord. The Guild Wars led to chaos until the present system was instituted in 1273 DR with the Magisters and secret Lords firmly reestablished.

### Undermountain
Beneath the city lies an extensive dungeon complex. On its third level is Skullport, a secret city filled with the worst elements of humanity, stalked by drow and illithids.

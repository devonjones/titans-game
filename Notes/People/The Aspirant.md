# The Aspirant

**Role:** Death Knight of [[Dragonspear]] — [[Bathere]]'s lieutenant
**Status:** **DEAD** — killed in [[Session 07 - The Door of the Dead|Session 7]]
**Type:** Death Knight (reanimated paladin corpse)

## Overview
A death knight serving [[Bathere]] at [[Dragonspear]] Castle. Identified in [[Session 06 - Visitors Visitors|Session 6]] as the armored, horned figure standing beside Bathere. We called him "the Aspirant" from Bathere's terminology for his forces. After the fight, we confirmed that **his body is that of a paladin** — the reanimated corpse of someone who had taken (and been broken from) paladin oaths.

## Combat Contribution
- **Tried (and failed) to attack through [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s Wall of Force** — multiple rounds wasted on the barrier
- Eventually **[[Bathere]] dim-doored him through the wall** to the party's side
- **Held his action** while Bathere teleported — coordinated tactics
- **Killed by [[Kane]]:** Booming Blade + Hex (19) + Smite (7 radiant). Fell to a Spirit Guardian on the way down

## What We Know
- **The body was a paladin.** The oaths were presumably broken or twisted — death knights are typically fallen paladins
- Served [[Bathere]] as his close protection and melee muscle
- Possibly part of [[Bathere]]'s [[Dracolich Research|transformation research]] — or simply a weapon he commanded

## Open Questions
- **Whose body was it?** A local paladin who disappeared? A stranger? Would anyone recognize the face?
- **Did [[Bathere]] create him, or did [[The Death Tyrant|Ahmendril]] supply him?**
- **Is this tied to the Dead Three?** Death knights are a classic Bane/Myrkul signature
- **Should the body be given proper rites and burial** to lay the paladin's spirit to rest?

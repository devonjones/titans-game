# Laeral Silverhand

**Role:** Open Lord of [[Waterdeep]] -- head of the city's ruling council
**Status:** Ally
**Faction:** [[Lords' Alliance]] (leader), [[Harpers]] (confirmed Session 10)
**Power Level:** Famous wizard, **[[Chosen of Mystra]]**, one of the Seven Sisters

## Overview

[[Laeral Silverhand]] is the **Open Lord of Waterdeep** and the head of the city's ruling council. She is a famous wizard, one of the **Seven Sisters**, and a **Chosen of Mystra** (canon Forgotten Realms lore). Personally received [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|the Duchess]] when the party delivered her to Waterdeep -- they know each other and embraced when alone.

She is also a [[Harpers|Harper]], confirmed in [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]] when she revealed she already knew [[General Martavis]] was a Harper. The Harpers have penetrated to the very top of the [[Lords' Alliance]].

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars]]

**Council chamber.** The party arrives at the royal castle with [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|the Duchess]] to find the council in chaos:
- [[Dagult Neverember|Neverember]]: *"The city's impregnable."*
- [[Thrun]]: *"We need to destroy them before reinforcement."*

The Duchess requests a private conversation. Room empties except the Duchess, Silverhand, and the party. **Silverhand and the Duchess embrace** -- they know each other personally.

**[[Vaelin]]'s briefing:**
- Reports everything from [[Dragonspear]]: [[Bathere]], the dracolich research, [[The Goat Head Mask|the cursed helmet]]

**Lunch invitation:** party invited to meet her again the next day around lunchtime.

### [[Session 11 - When the World Burns]]

**The Library Briefing (morning).** Found buried in books. Looked at a clock, asked the party how they were. Then: ***"Get me a report on the army."***

**The Dead Three discussion:**
- Asked what the party knew about the Dead Three; held forth on history including the **Time of Troubles**
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] **already knew everything she covered** -- he wrote his doctoral thesis on the Dead Three
- BUT Silverhand had the classified appendix:
- **The Dead Three built a magical gateway to the Far Realm** (place of old gods, raw chaos and power)
- **They got in over their heads, closed the door, and spread the pieces around**
- **[[The Door of the Dead]] is one of those pieces.** [[Ahmendril]] holding it requires further investigation
- More concerned about **the Door** than [[The Goat Head Mask|the Mask]]
- The Mask **might be a phylactery**; **she tried to destroy it and could not**

**Troll Claw Hills:** wanted to discuss the [[General Martavis|Martavis]] backup mission **in committee** before approving. Party cooled heels.

**Public posture during the day:** played things close to the chest with the army outside ramping up for an attack.

**The Dragon Attack (overnight).** During the two-dragon assault on [[Waterdeep]], **Silverhand was on the castle roof with the party**. Shouted ***"To arms!"*** -- then **flew away toward the castle wall** to engage. (Wizard / Chosen flight, presumably.) Her actions during the rest of the battle unobserved by the party; she was working her own front.

**Status end of session:** alive, last seen flying toward the wall during the attack. The party has not yet checked in with her about the aftermath.

### [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms]]

**Rescue work all day.** Encountered by the party while lifting a building off victims. Briefly briefed on [[The Stolen Keystone|the keystone theft]]; helped continue rescue and rebuilding.

**The Library Briefing (second part).** After the party found her at the council, gave the **biggest single info dump of the campaign**:
- **Asked the party to find the other pieces of the door** -- formally tasked them
- Confirmed: **[[Ahmendril]] has 2 of 3 pieces of [[The Gate of the Far Realms]]**. Only [[Notes/Items/The Key|the Key]] is uncollected
- Each piece is connected to one of the [[The Dead Three|Dead Three]]
- **The Door is NOT Bane** -- it's Bhaal or Myrkul
- Believed the composite is **The Gate of the Far Realms** (working name)
- Cannot trace the teleportation runes [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] asked about

**Required an oath** for [[Lords' Alliance]] teleport-circle access. Summoned an [[Order of the Fist]] paladin (full plate, blue fist on breastplate) to administer the **Zone of Truth** ritual; then **Silverhand took drops of blood** and cast **Geas** to bind the oath.

**Morning of Session 12 end:** **anxiously awaiting the party** -- [[General Martavis|Martavis]] had **already attacked the Zhent castle** while the party was sleeping. Teleported the party to a field 2 miles out so they could intervene.

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Laeral_Silverhand).*

### Identity
Laeral Silverhand is one of the **Seven Sisters** -- powerful sorceress-Chosen of Mystra, daughters of Mystra and Dornal Silverhand. The sisters are among the most powerful mortal spellcasters in Faerûn.

### As Open Lord
Open Lord of Waterdeep -- the public face of the Masked Lords cryptocracy, leading the [[Lords' Alliance]]. Her predecessor was Dagult Neverember (so Neverember is a former Open Lord, possibly displaced or stepped aside).

### As Chosen of Mystra
A Chosen of Mystra retains divine power even in worlds where Mystra is weakened or dead. In this campaign's timeline, **Mystra is dead** (per Session 5 GM note) -- which makes Silverhand's status as Chosen unusual and her power especially significant.

### Family
Sister to Storm Silverhand (Harper bard), Alustriel (former High Lady of Silverymoon), and the other Sisters. Long history of wizardly accomplishment and political leadership.

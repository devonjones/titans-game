Identified in [[Session 05 - Signed S]] as the head of the Zhentarim in Daggerford. The "S" who signed notes ordering [[Councilman Moore]]'s murder and authorizing the Zhent security contract. **Reports to [[Ahmendril]]** (the current [[Zanithar]]; revealed Session 9). [[General Martavis]] is a [[Harpers|Harper]] deep-cover agent, not Samus's subordinate (revealed Session 9). Currently believed to be at the new [[Zhentarim]] HQ in the [[Troll Claw Hills]] (Session 10) -- [[General Martavis|Martavis]] is en route to confront him there.

## Combat Profile (Session 13)

What the party learned by fighting him:

- **Darkness from his mouth.** He *"opens his mouth and a cloud of black ink covers the area."* This is a **breath/aura effect, not a cast spell** -- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] noted he was dispelling *on the effect, not on the spell*, and multiple attempts failed (including one backed by a Fae point and one by Action Surge) before he finally broke it
- **Possible Legendary Resistance.** He rolled a natural 20 on [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]]'s [[Synaptic Static]] INT save and then *"chose to succeed"* -- that phrasing is verbatim Legendary Resistance. **Confirm with the GM.** If true, he has a limited pool of auto-successes that should be burned deliberately before committing a real save-or-die
- **Melee attacker.** Stepped into his own darkness and hit [[Kane]] twice
- **Grappleable.** Kane grappled him successfully; the grapple broke only because forced movement (an eldritch bolt) knocked Kane loose
- **Supported.** He is not alone -- see Camp Assets below

> **The [[The Assassin]] parallel.** [[The Assassin]] in [[Session 04 - Good Timing|Session 4]] used an **Aura of Darkness** -- explicitly a specific ability, not a spell -- and fought invisibly. Samus produces darkness the same way. Two enemies, same signature. Combined with the garbled speech behind "this thing on your face," there is a live question about **what Samus actually is.**

## ⚠️ THE DEVIL DEAL WAS PAID IN MASKS (Session 16)

In [[Session 16 - The Masks I Am Owed|Session 16]] the pit fiend at [[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]] opened on the party with:

> ***"Have you brought the masks? The masks I am owed?"***

**Samus owed a devil the masks.**

The brothel and its five succubi were the **consideration** -- what he received. **The masks were the price.**

Which finally explains [[Samus's Bargain|his bargain]]: ***"Bring me their helms."*** **Not heads. Helms.** He wasn't collecting trophies or simply killing rivals -- **he was sourcing masks to settle an infernal debt, using the party as his collection agent.** Three lieutenants, three masks, one payment.

And it explains the deflection. [[Kane]]: *"Why does [[Ahmendril]] have your chain?"* Samus: *"We have a mutually beneficial arrangement."*

> **He had two creditors.** [[Ahmendril]] held his **resurrection** ([[The Masks of Ahmendril]]); a devil held a **claim on the object**. The word *"arrangement"* covered both, and he disclosed neither.

**He was leveraged from both ends by the same piece of equipment.**

See [[The Devil's Claim]].

## The Devil Deal (Session 14)

After Samus died, the keep collapsed, **the ground opened, and an infernal gate appeared where it had stood.** [[General Martavis|Martavis]] explained why:

> ***"[[Samus]] had made a deal with a devil to secure a brothel to recruit souls."*** -- [[General Martavis|Martavis]]

**The pavilion was the operation.** The one he deflected on so hard in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]]: *"She doesn't know shit. It's a brothel."* It was staffed by **five succubi** ([[The Succubi Alliance]]) harvesting souls under an infernal contract.

**Samus was the collateral.** He died; the note was called; a **Pit Fiend** started climbing out. See [[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]].

This recontextualizes the whole man. He was not simply [[Ahmendril]]'s chained subordinate -- **he had a second creditor**, and the "mutually beneficial arrangement" line covered more than one arrangement.

> He built his whole game around not being seen -- darkness from his mouth, a face that garbled his own voice, a door closed twice. **A storm does not need to see you and it does not need the door open.**


## ⚠️ WHO HE ACTUALLY WAS (Legend Lore, Session 15)

[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] cast **Legend Lore** on [[Samus's Helm|the Ram Helm]] in [[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]]. The vision gave the party his origin.

### He is over a century old and he was never originally a Zhent

A battlefield, long ago. A **viking-esque figure marauding through combat** — Samus. **He was not wearing Zhent colors. He appeared to be the commander.**

**[[Ahmendril]] appears** — humanoid, elemental aura, drawn sword, a thrown Otiluke's Freezing Sphere that annihilates a crowd — **defeats him, and accepts his surrender.** They negotiate. Samus nods.

**They teleport to a wizard's library full of masks**, where [[Ahmendril]] — wearing an **owl mask**, his **pendant** at his neck — **crafts the ram's head mask** for him.

> [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] said in [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]] that Samus *"rose out of nothingness to lead the [[Zhentarim]]."*
>
> **He rose out of another century.**

### He has died before

**Post-credits scene:** [[Samus]] dies in a later combat — **an angel in the distance**, a **paladin stabbing him in the chest.** Fade to black.

**He comes back, kneeling before [[Ahmendril]], wearing the mask.**

[[Farraday]]'s parallel vision identifies that paladin: **a dark elf, female, white hair, blue fist on her shield** — the **[[Order of the Fist]]** — in a battle roughly **100 years ago** where a masked cabal confronted a god (possibly Helm) and lost to a band of heroes.

**Samus and [[Farraday]] were in the same masked group at the same battle, and both died there.**

### THE CHAIN WAS THE MASK

[[Kane]] asked him directly in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]]:

> **Kane:** *"Why does [[Ahmendril]] have your chain?"*
> **Samus:** *"We have a mutually beneficial arrangement. It's not your concern."*

**It was on his face.** [[Ahmendril]] resurrects his lieutenants through the masks he makes them — see [[The Masks of Ahmendril]]. That is the arrangement, that is the leverage, and it is why **he priced [[Samus's Bargain|his bargain]] in *helms*, not heads.** Taking a lieutenant's mask may be the only way to kill one permanently.

**And it explains his silence** when Kane asked whether the helmet carried a curse. He wasn't hiding a curse. He was watching the party pick up the thing that brings him back.

### Is he coming back?

[[Farraday]] died ~100 years ago and returned after **an ~80-year gap.** On that timeline, **no — not this campaign.**

**And the party has his mask.** [[Kane]] is carrying it. **The body in the hell-pit was never the anchor.**

# Samus

**Role:** Head of the Zhentarim in Daggerford
**Status:** Enemy
**Identity:** Likely the "S" who signed incriminating notes

## Affiliations
- **[[Zhentarim]]:** Leader of Daggerford operations

## Overview
Identified in [[Session 05 - Signed S]] as the head of the Zhentarim in Daggerford. The "S" who signed notes ordering [[Councilman Moore]]'s murder and authorizing the Zhent security contract. **Reports to [[Ahmendril]]** (the current [[Zanithar]]; revealed Session 9). [[General Martavis]] is a [[Harpers|Harper]] deep-cover agent, not Samus's subordinate (revealed Session 9). Currently believed to be at the new [[Zhentarim]] HQ in the [[Troll Claw Hills]] (Session 10) -- [[General Martavis|Martavis]] is en route to confront him there.

## What We Know
- Head of Zhentarim operations in Daggerford
- Notes signed "S" include:
  - [[General Martavis]]'s guest list
  - Contract for Zhentarim guards
  - *"If the smith is a problem, then deal with it"* — ordering Moore's murder
- Moore was staged **before** the Zhent security contract was signed — premeditated
- [[General Martavis]] may not know the party has these letters

## Overview
Identified in [[Session 05 - Signed S]] as the head of the Zhentarim in Daggerford. Likely the "S" who signed notes ordering [[Councilman Moore]]'s murder and authorizing the Zhent security contract. His relationship to [[General Martavis]] is unclear — Samus may be Martavis's superior, or they may be parallel authorities (political vs. military).

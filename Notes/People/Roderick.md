# Roderick
**Role:** Bandit (captured)
**Status:** In custody
**Real Name:** Unknown

## Overview
A bandit captured at the [[Riverdale]] tavern who cooperated with the party during interrogation. A war refugee who turned to banditry out of desperation. Recognized [[Cael Stormcrow]] by reputation. The only survivor of the three bandits captured at the tavern - the other two were killed by the smith's widow while the party was away.

## What He Told Us
- Confirmed the stolen goods were [[Daggerford]]'s taxes
- [[Merron]]'s camp was about a mile north of [[Riverdale]]
- Claims Merron was working for himself (no outside employer)
- War and poverty drove him to banditry: *"Being poor brought me here. War put us out of a job."*
- Recognized Stormcrow: *"Oh my god, you're Stormcrow."*

## What We Did
- [[Vaelin]] threatened him creatively (*"Do you know what you had for your last meal, or would you like it to be a mystery?"*)
- He cooperated and provided intel
- [[Vaelin]] healed his injured hand as a show of good faith
- Cael reinforced the message: *"Rosey palm and her five sisters are a sailor's best friend. You almost lost that friend today."*
- Told him he was at a crossroads and could choose his path

## Connections
- **[[Merron]]:** His leader

# Viggio Martel

**Role:** Council Member, Head of Trade
**Status:** Enemy

## Affiliations
- **[[Daggerford Council]]:** Member, Head of Trade - dominant force
- **[[Zhentarim]]:** Likely allied; benefits from and enables their presence

## Overview
Dominant force on the Daggerford council. Manipulative. Kane says he's part of the corruption in town.

## Relationships
- **[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]:** Enemy. Has usurped much of her power through the council.
- **[[Councilman Moore]]:** Enemy. Moore believes Viggio killed him.
- **[[General Martavis]]:** Likely allied; both benefit from the current corruption.
- **[[Elaine Drago]]:** Fellow council member. Likely adversarial given political alignments.
- **[[Margeet Patel]]:** Fellow council member.
- **[[Throm Ugala]]:** Fellow council member.
- **[[Maren Thane]]:** Fellow council member; Maren's financial troubles may make him a target for manipulation.

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]
Approached the party at the tavern, tried to chat them up and gather information. Evaded the party's questions. Invited the party to his party the following night. [[Councilman Moore]], via Speak with Dead, named Viggio as his killer (though he didn't witness it directly). [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] recommends attending his party.

### [[Session 02 - Riverdale]]
Not directly encountered, but the party reported to Lady Morwen that Moore believed Viggio killed him. We believe Viggio (or the corrupt council members) are the ones directing [[General Martavis]]'s operations via the security contract. His party is tomorrow.

### [[Session 03 - Through the Wall]]
Hosted the party at his manor (#23 on the map), guarded by [[Zhentarim]] Grey Wolves under Commander [[Bennet]]. Approached Stormcrow directly at the start of the party — sized us up, then introduced us to [[Padon]] (cobbletoner, Builder's Guild). Mid-party, slipped out of the ballroom while deliberately trying not to be seen. **Locate Object confirmed the missing fingers are on the ground floor of his manor during the party itself.**

### [[Session 04 - Good Timing]]
Walked back into the building after [[Margeet Patel|Margeet]]'s murder — suspicious timing. Was present in the ballroom during the chaos and the subsequent investigation.

[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] instructed him to unlock the hidden door — **he complied**. Storm and [[Vaelin]] deceived him about what they were locating; he appeared to believe them. While [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] was "guarding" him, **he vanished again.** Viseli covered with *"Viggio is in danger!"*

[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] formally and publicly **declared Viggio wanted for the murder of [[Councilman Moore]]**. [[Captain Hidar]] notified by Vaelin telepathically. A party authority figure agreed to arrest him if he reappears — lukewarm response.

**Viggio's current whereabouts: unknown.** Escaped via unknown means. A second secret door opened to stairs leading to the basement — possibly Viggio triggered it while fleeing, or knows the mechanism.

# Viggio Martel

**Role:** Council Member, Head of Trade
**Status:** Enemy

## Overview
Dominant force on the Daggerford council. Manipulative. Kane says he's part of the corruption in town.

- **[[Margeet Patel]]:** Fellow council member.
- **[[Throm Ugala]]:** Fellow council member.

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]
Approached the party at the tavern, tried to chat them up and gather information. Evaded the party's questions. Invited the party to his party the following night. [[Councilman Moore]], via Speak with Dead, named Viggio as his killer (though he didn't witness it directly). [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] recommends attending his party.

### [[Session 02 - Riverdale]]
Not directly encountered, but the party reported to Lady Morwen that Moore believed Viggio killed him. We believe Viggio (or the corrupt council members) are the ones directing [[General Martavis]]'s operations via the security contract. His party is tomorrow.

### [[Session 03 - Through the Wall]]
Hosted the party at his manor (#23 on the map), guarded by [[Zhentarim]] Grey Wolves under Commander [[Bennet]]. Approached Stormcrow directly at the start of the party — sized us up, then introduced us to [[Padon]] (cobbletoner, Builder's Guild). Mid-party, slipped out of the ballroom while deliberately trying not to be seen. **Locate Object confirmed the missing fingers are on the ground floor of his manor during the party itself.**

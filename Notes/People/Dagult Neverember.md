# Dagult Neverember

**Role:** Waterdeep council noble; former Open Lord (canon FR)
**Status:** Neutral / unverified

## Overview

A member of the [[Waterdeep]] ruling council. Present in the council chamber when the party arrived with [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|the Duchess]] in [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]]. Argued the **defensive** position in the council debate:

> *"The city's impregnable."*

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars]]
- Present in the council chamber during the chaotic debate over how to handle the [[Waterdeep|siege]]
- Position: hold and defend -- the city's walls will hold
- Pushed back against [[Thrun]]'s call for a preemptive strike

## Trustworthiness

**Unknown.** The party hasn't interacted with him directly. The council is in chaos and active [[The Pencheska Conspiracy|Pencheska]] operatives may be embedded in [[Waterdeep]] leadership. The [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|night attack on the Ellenfear estate]] proves the conspiracy is active in the city -- some of the nobles around Silverhand are plausible plants.

His "impregnable" stance might be:
- Genuine confidence in Waterdeep's defenses (the FR canon Open Lord position would support this)
- Stalling tactics from a compromised noble
- Pencheska false-flag passivity to keep the city from acting

## Open Questions
- Is he a [[The Pencheska Conspiracy|Pencheska]] plant?
- Why was he displaced as Open Lord? (Canon: replaced by [[Laeral Silverhand]])
- What's his actual relationship with Silverhand now?

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dagult_Neverember).*

Dagult Neverember is a former Open Lord of Waterdeep and former Lord Protector of Neverwinter. In canon, ambitious, politically savvy, and unpopular with many Waterdhavians by the end of his tenure. Known for prioritizing Neverwinter's rebuilding over Waterdeep's needs while Open Lord.

If he is the same Neverember in this campaign, he's a competent political operator with multiple loyalty axes and the connections to be either a hidden hand for good or a Pencheska asset.

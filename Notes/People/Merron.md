# Merron

**Role:** Bandit Leader
**Status:** Unknown (camp overrun by [[Zhentarim]]; no prisoners taken)

## Affiliations
- **Merron's Bandits:** Leader of a few dozen bandits occupying [[Riverdale]]

## Overview
Leader of the bandit group that occupied [[Riverdale]] and stole [[Daggerford]]'s tax revenue. His camp was located about a mile north of Riverdale. Claims to be working for himself.

## What We Know
- Led a few dozen bandits
- Occupied [[Riverdale]], terrorized the town
- Stole [[Daggerford]]'s tax shipment
- Camp was a mile north of the town
- His men claim he was working for himself
- The [[Zhentarim]] destroyed his camp with 100 cavalry and took no prisoners

## Open Questions
- Is he dead? The Zhents took no prisoners, but was he at the camp?
- Was he really independent, or working for someone?
- How did the [[Zhentarim]] know his camp's location?
- Was he set up to steal the taxes so the Zhents could "recover" them?

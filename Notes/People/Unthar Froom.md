# Unthar Froom

**Role:** Head of the [[Order of the Fist]]
**Race:** Dwarf
**Status:** Allied -- but [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] excludes him from full briefings; limited trust
**First Encountered (in scene):** [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms]]

## Overview

A **heavily armored dwarf** leading paladins in the aftermath of the [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] dragon attack on [[Waterdeep]]. **Symbol of the Fist** on his shield (blue fist iconography -- the [[Order of the Fist]]'s mark).

Encountered in [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]] giving orders to his paladins:

> *"Organize the paladins. Start hunting the Dead Three now."*

He recognized the party (or at least recognized [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]); the party recognized him by name.

## Why the Party Backs Off

[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] **remembered** that Unthar was **one of the people asked to leave the room** when the party first met with [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] in [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]].

That means: Silverhand specifically excluded him from the [[The Door of the Dead|Far Realm gateway]] / [[The Dead Three|Dead Three]] briefing. Whatever the reason -- compromised, low-priority, jurisdictional politics, prior bad blood -- **she didn't trust him with the full picture**.

So the party, following Celleborn's lead, **politely declines** to share full intel with him. They confirm only:
- The keystone was stolen by teleporting mobs
- The Dead Three are behind it
- There's a Cult of the Dragon angle

## Stormcrow's Floated Idea

[[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] later mentioned to [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|Lady Morwen]] the idea of **using Unthar / the Order as a message-routing channel**. Specifics unclear -- could be Order-of-the-Fist paladins as carriers, or use of their network for communications. Untested.

## Known Activities (Session 12)
- Organizing paladins for a **Dead Three hunt** in [[Waterdeep]]
- Took the party to a more private location to talk -- party declined to share details

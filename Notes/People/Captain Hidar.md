# Captain Hidar

**Role:** Guard Captain at Lady Morwen's manor
**Status:** Trusted by Lady Morwen

## Affiliations
- **Daggerford:** Guard Captain, serves [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]

## Overview
Commander of the manor guards. Knows some of the party members. Lady Morwen trusts him completely.

## Relationships
- **[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]:** Serves her loyally. She trusts him implicitly.
- **[[Kane]]:** Knows him; Kane asked him for intel on arrival.
- **[[General Martavis]]:** Aware of Zhentarim presence but plays his cards close.

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]
Greeted the party at the manor gates with a pair of guards. [[Kane]] asked him to fill them in on the situation. Acknowledged the heavy Zhentarim presence ("lots of black and gold") but didn't reveal if he has an operation running against them. The party now officially reports to him as Ducal Guard.

### [[Session 02 - Riverdale]]
Provided intel on the bandit occupation of [[Riverdale]] - a villager escaped and reported a few dozen bandits. The party used this information to plan the mission.

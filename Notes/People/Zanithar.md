# Zanithar

**Type:** Recurring entity / role (not a single person)
**Status:** Active -- current bearer is [[Ahmendril]]
**Identified by:** [[Laeral Silverhand]], [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]]

## What Silverhand Said

> ***"There have been many Zanithars. He is periodically killed and replaced."***

This was [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s response when [[Vaelin]] described [[Ahmendril]] -- the figure with the death-tyrant head, single red eye, eyestalk dreadlocks. She used the name **Zanithar** and immediately framed it as a recurring identity.

## What We Know
- **Zanithar is a role, not a person.** Killing one Zanithar produces another.
- The **death tyrant head** appears to be the signature presentation -- a uniform, not an injury.
- The cycle has happened **many times** over history.
- The current Zanithar is **[[Ahmendril]]**, encountered by the party in [[Session 04 - Good Timing]] and identified by [[Bathere]] in [[Session 06 - Visitors Visitors]].
- Silverhand evidently has prior knowledge of this entity and the cycle.

## ⚠️ Session 15: A Zanithar Died In The Waterdeep Sewers

[[Farraday]]'s Legend Lore vision shows her, roughly a century ago, fighting her way through **[[The Waterdeep Sewers|the Waterdeep sewers]]** with a shadow crew and **killing a Beholder Zanithar.**

- **Elementals rose in the room from her hand**
- **They came at him from all angles so he didn't know where to attack** -- a deliberate counter to the antimagic cone
- They ganked him

**She killed one of [[Ahmendril]]'s predecessors.**

**And then [[Ahmendril]] recruited her for it.** He came to her home, they had tea, they came to an agreement, and he made her a raven mask. See [[The Masks of Ahmendril]].

### The mask hypothesis

[[Ahmendril]] crafts masks that **bring his lieutenants back from death** after roughly eighty years. He **wears an owl mask himself**, and **there is a second owl mask on the wall** of his workshop.

> **Is the Zanithar mantle just the biggest mask?**
>
> *"There have been many Zanithars. He is periodically killed and replaced."* If the masks are return anchors, **Ahmendril's own owl mask may be how the Zanithar keeps coming back** -- and the second owl on the wall is either a spare or the next bearer's.
>
> If so, **the mask is the phylactery-equivalent the party has been looking for, and destroying it is how the cycle ends.**

## What We Don't Know
- **What IS a Zanithar?** Possible frames:
  - **Phylactery cycle** -- the Zanithar is a lich whose phylactery jumps to a new body when killed
  - **Possession** -- a demon/spirit/ancient consciousness that inhabits a host, with the host's body being reshaped (the death-tyrant head)
  - **Oath / mantle** -- a position in a cult or order; new bearers volunteer or are appointed
  - **Cursed bloodline** -- inherited identity, passed at the death of the previous
  - **A mask** -- **the leading candidate as of Session 15.** See above
- **How long has this been happening?** Centuries? Millennia?
- **Who knows about it?** Silverhand obviously does. [[Harpers]] presumably. The [[Lords' Alliance]] leadership likely
- **Can it be ended?** Or only delayed?
- **Is there a specific phylactery / artifact / oath / ritual that defines the role?**
- **Is the Zanithar always pursuing the same goal,** or does each bearer have their own agenda? (Current Zanithar is reviving two ancient dragons -- is that THE Zanithar mission, or just Ahmendril's twist?)

## Tactical Implication

**Killing Ahmendril does not end the threat.** This is the most important takeaway. The party must either:
1. **Understand the mechanism** of replacement and break it (find the phylactery, the bloodline, the artifact)
2. **Interrupt the cycle** at the right moment (catch the transition? destroy the next vessel?)
3. **Prevent the current Zanithar from completing his work** before he dies, so the next one doesn't inherit a finished ritual
4. Or all three

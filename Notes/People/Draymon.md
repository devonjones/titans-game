# Draymon

**Role:** Lieutenant of [[Ahmendril]]
**Status:** Enemy -- **in [[Chult]]**
**Named:** [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] (by [[Samus]])

## Overview

One of **three named lieutenants of [[Ahmendril]]**, disclosed by [[Samus]] during the [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] parley. [[Samus]] offered to release [[General Martavis|Martavis]] in exchange for the three lieutenants' helms.

> ***"And a third one, Draymon, in Chult."*** -- [[Samus]]

Nothing else is known. No description, no capabilities, no signature given.

## Why Chult Matters

[[Chult]] is a jungle peninsula far to the south -- **the most physically distant of the three lieutenants** on the Material Plane, and a serious expedition in its own right.

More to the point, Chult is **death-magic country**. In Forgotten Realms canon it is the home of Acererak's Tomb of Annihilation and the Soulmonger, and the seat of Chultan death-god worship. For a lieutenant of a **[[Zanithar]]** -- a recurring death-tyrant figure assembling a [[The Gate of the Far Realms|Far Realm gateway]] built by the [[The Dead Three|Dead Three]] -- Chult is not a random posting.

**Working theory:** Draymon is there for something specific, not just hiding. Candidates:
- A piece of the Gate, or intelligence about one
- Death-magic resources for the [[Zanithar]] cycle
- A ritual site comparable to [[Mount Hotenow]]

## The Travel Problem

Chult is far. The party's realistic options:
- **[[Lords' Alliance]] teleport circles** -- the party is now oath-bound and has network access. **Is there a circle in Chult?** Port Nyanzaru is a major trade port, so plausibly yes. This is the single most useful question to ask [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]
- **Sea voyage** -- weeks. [[Cael Stormcrow|Cael]] is a sailor and the party has maritime experience, but the time cost is enormous with [[Ahmendril]] two-thirds assembled
- **Teleport spell** -- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] or [[Vaelin]] would need the spell and a known destination

## Open Questions

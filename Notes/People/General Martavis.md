# General Martavis

**Role:** Zhentarim General
**Status:** Enemy (presumed)

## Affiliations
- **[[Zhentarim]]:** General, commands the forces occupying Daggerford

## Overview
Zhentarim military leader currently operating in Daggerford. Commands hundreds of well-equipped soldiers in half-plate.

## Relationships
- **[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]:** Adversarial. Was arguing with her when party arrived.
- **[[Viggio Martel]]:** Likely allied; the Zhentarim presence benefits from council corruption.
- **[[Captain Hidar]]:** Hidar is aware of the Zhent presence but relationship unclear.

## Connected Plots
- [[The Zhentarim Occupation]] - Commands the occupying force
- [[The Destabilization Strategy]] - His rapid deployment to [[Riverdale]] is suspicious
- [[Martavis's Emblem]] - His jacket bears a distinctive emblem that [[Kane]] recognizes from the Thayan civil war

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]
Was in [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]'s study arguing with her when the party arrived. Left the study and passed the party on the stairs; glanced at them but didn't engage. His horse was present at the manor.

### [[Session 02 - Riverdale]]
Arrived at [[Merron]]'s bandit camp north of [[Riverdale]] with 100 [[Zhentarim]] cavalry *before* the party got there. Took no prisoners. Recovered a chest believed to contain the stolen tax revenue. Disputed ownership of the taxes with [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]. Agreed to escort the gold back to [[Daggerford]]. Deflected [[Kane]]'s question about who gave him the order: *"Let's not waste time."* Delivered the chest to the Ducal Palace.

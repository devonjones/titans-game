# General Martavis

**Role:** Zhentarim General (Phoenix faction) / **Harper Agent**
**Status:** Ally (revealed Session 9)

## Affiliations
- **[[Harpers]]:** Agent -- deep cover within the [[Zhentarim]]
- **[[Zhentarim]] (Phoenix faction):** Commands the Phoenix troops, loyal to him personally
- **[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]:** Allied -- was protecting her interests from inside the Zhent structure

## Overview
Harper agent embedded deep within the Zhentarim, commanding the Phoenix faction. Operated under cover throughout Sessions 1-8, appearing as an enemy general while actually working to protect [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] and undermine the Wolf faction (the real enemy Zhents under [[Samus]]). Killed [[Viggio Martel]] and left the body pinned to a tree as a message. Revealed his Harper pin to the party in Session 9.

## Relationships
- **[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]:** Allied. The argument in Session 1 recontextualizes -- likely warning her, not threatening. His "lockdown" may have been aimed at the Wolf Zhents, not the Duchess
- **[[Kane]]:** Fellow Harper (though Kane didn't know). Kane recognized his Thayan war emblem but misread it -- the unit wasn't necromancer command staff; it was a Harper infiltration team
- **[[Viggio Martel]]:** Enemy. Martavis killed him. Viggio's mission was to replace the Duchess
- **Samus:** Samus believed Martavis reported to him. Martavis was actually reporting to the Harpers
- **[[Captain Hidar]]:** Relationship unclear -- Hidar may have known or suspected

## Connected Plots
- [[The Zhentarim Occupation]] - Infiltrated the occupying force from within
- [[Martavis's Emblem]] - **Resolved.** The emblem marks a Harper infiltration team, not a Zhent command unit

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]
Was in [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]'s study arguing with her when the party arrived. Left the study and passed the party on the stairs; glanced at them but didn't engage. His horse was present at the manor. *In retrospect: likely briefing or warning the Duchess.*

### [[Session 02 - Riverdale]]
Arrived at [[Merron]]'s bandit camp north of [[Riverdale]] with 100 [[Zhentarim]] cavalry before the party. Took no prisoners. Recovered the tax chest. Disputed ownership with [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]. Agreed to escort the gold back. Deflected [[Kane]]'s questions: *"Let's not waste time."* Delivered the chest to the Ducal Palace. Running soup kitchens and maintaining order. *All of this was genuine good governance under cover.*

### [[Session 03 - Through the Wall]]
Not directly encountered. Discussed in morning briefing -- did he deliver the taxes because we were watching? Storm's theory at the time was wrong -- Martavis delivered the taxes because that was the right thing to do.

### [[Session 08 - Running the Gauntlet]]
Declared the agreement with the Duchess "no longer in effect" -- full lockdown of Daggerford. His Phoenix forces sealed the town, then left overnight heading North. *He was positioning his forces against the Wolf army.* The party fought through 50 Wolf cavalry (not Phoenix). Kane spotted Martavis on a hilltop watching. Kane recognized him from the Thayan war -- but misidentified the emblem's meaning.

### [[Session 09 - The Wolf and the Phoenix]]
Sent an owl with a note to the Duchess: *"I heard you got my gift. If I was hostile, you wouldn't think it, you would know it. I need you to send your men to hit the flank of that army before the next river. Trust."* The party attacked the Wolf army's flank while Martavis's Phoenix cavalry hit from behind. His swordsman killed the dire troll in 6 swings. His men killed all survivors including one who surrendered -- no witnesses.

After the battle: asked [[Ono]] if any escaped. Requested a private word with the Duchess. When Kane asked about Thorn's Gap, said he was "right where he wanted to be." Told the party Waterdeep is accessible but will be under siege. **Showed his Harper pin.**

**Key revelations:**
- He **killed [[Viggio Martel]]** -- Viggio's mission was to replace the Duchess
- **Ahmendril is Samus's superior** -- the Death Tyrant runs the whole operation
- **Heading to the Troll Claw Hills** to hit the Zhent headquarters

### [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars]]
**Split off from the party at the start of the session to ride to the [[Troll Claw Hills]]** -- heading to the new [[Zhentarim]] HQ to confront [[Samus]] directly. **Asked the party for backup** with Samus once they had delivered the Duchess to [[Waterdeep]]. Per [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] later that day: she **already knew Martavis was a Harper** before the party arrived -- the Harper leadership has been tracking him all along.

# General Martavis

**Role:** Zhentarim General (Phoenix faction) / **Harper Agent**
**Status:** Ally (revealed Session 9) -- **VERIFY HIM. See [[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar|Session 14]] below**

## Affiliations
- **[[Harpers]]:** Agent -- deep cover within the [[Zhentarim]]
- **[[Zhentarim]] (Phoenix faction):** Commands the Phoenix troops, loyal to him personally
- **[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]:** Allied -- was protecting her interests from inside the Zhent structure

## Overview
Harper agent embedded deep within the Zhentarim, commanding the Phoenix faction. Operated under cover throughout Sessions 1-8, appearing as an enemy general while actually working to protect [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] and undermine the Wolf faction (the real enemy Zhents under [[Samus]]). Killed [[Viggio Martel]] and left the body pinned to a tree as a message. Revealed his Harper pin to the party in Session 9.

## Relationships
- **[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]:** Allied. The argument in Session 1 recontextualizes -- likely warning her, not threatening. His "lockdown" may have been aimed at the Wolf Zhents, not the Duchess
- **[[Kane]]:** Fellow Harper (though Kane didn't know). Kane recognized his Thayan war emblem but misread it -- the unit wasn't necromancer command staff; it was a Harper infiltration team
- **[[Viggio Martel]]:** Enemy. Martavis killed him. Viggio's mission was to replace the Duchess
- **Samus:** Samus believed Martavis reported to him. Martavis was actually reporting to the Harpers
- **[[Captain Hidar]]:** Relationship unclear -- Hidar may have known or suspected

## Connected Plots
- [[The Zhentarim Occupation]] - Infiltrated the occupying force from within
- [[Martavis's Emblem]] - **Resolved.** The emblem marks a Harper infiltration team, not a Zhent command unit
- [[Lady Morwen's Escape]] - Facilitated the escape by positioning Phoenix forces

### [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]
Was in [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]'s study arguing with her when the party arrived. Left the study and passed the party on the stairs; glanced at them but didn't engage. His horse was present at the manor. *In retrospect: likely briefing or warning the Duchess.*

Arrived at [[Merron]]'s bandit camp north of [[Riverdale]] with 100 [[Zhentarim]] cavalry before the party. Took no prisoners. Recovered the tax chest. Disputed ownership with [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]. Agreed to escort the gold back. Deflected [[Kane]]'s questions: *"Let's not waste time."* Delivered the chest to the Ducal Palace. Running soup kitchens and maintaining order. *All of this was genuine good governance under cover.*

### [[Session 03 - Through the Wall]]
Not directly encountered. Discussed in morning briefing -- did he deliver the taxes because we were watching? Storm's theory at the time was wrong -- Martavis delivered the taxes because that was the right thing to do.

### [[Session 08 - Running the Gauntlet]]
Declared the agreement with the Duchess "no longer in effect" -- full lockdown of Daggerford. His Phoenix forces sealed the town, then left overnight heading North. *He was positioning his forces against the Wolf army.* The party fought through 50 Wolf cavalry (not Phoenix). Kane spotted Martavis on a hilltop watching. Kane recognized him from the Thayan war -- but misidentified the emblem's meaning.

### [[Session 09 - The Wolf and the Phoenix]]
Sent an owl with a note to the Duchess: *"I heard you got my gift. If I was hostile, you wouldn't think it, you would know it. I need you to send your men to hit the flank of that army before the next river. Trust."* The party attacked the Wolf army's flank while Martavis's Phoenix cavalry hit from behind. His swordsman killed the dire troll in 6 swings. His men killed all survivors including one who surrendered -- no witnesses.

After the battle: asked [[Ono]] if any escaped. Requested a private word with the Duchess. When Kane asked about Thorn's Gap, said he was "right where he wanted to be." Told the party Waterdeep is accessible but will be under siege. **Showed his Harper pin.**

**Split off from the party at the start of the session to ride to the [[Troll Claw Hills]]** -- heading to the new [[Zhentarim]] HQ to confront [[Samus]] directly. **Asked the party for backup** with Samus once they had delivered the Duchess to [[Waterdeep]]. Per [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] later that day: she **already knew Martavis was a Harper** before the party arrived -- the Harper leadership has been tracking him all along.

### [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms]]

By the time [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] teleported the party in 2 miles out, **combat was already underway inside the main building**.

### [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here]]
**CLIFFHANGER RESOLVED: he was beaten, not killed.**

The party rode in to find him **kneeling mid-swing** with [[Samus]] standing over him. Samus turned, saw the party, and spoke to someone at his side. **Martavis stood and stepped back** as they approached -- then shouted ***"Stand down."***

His men *"looked perplexed"* at the stand-down order -- one of them snuck over to eavesdrop on the party's huddle and said *"What's going on, we were winning."* ([[Kane]] told him: *"Be ready."*)

**He was the hostage.** [[Samus]] used him as the entire basis of [[Samus's Bargain|the bargain]] -- kill [[Ahmendril]]'s lieutenants, get Martavis back. Samus eventually conceded him for nothing but the party riding out of the camp.

When [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] tried to talk to him privately, Samus cut it off: *"You're not in charge."* Martavis moved back and let it happen. Samus to him: *"Mark, if you want this to descend into combat now, OK. If you don't, you'd better speak."*

**Status at session end:** alive, on his feet, present at the camp — but the deal that would have freed him was broken when [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] attacked Samus at the handshake. His position in the ensuing fight is unknown.

## ⚠️ THE SESSION 14 FLAG -- IS THIS STILL MARTAVIS?

Four separate observations, in one session, that do not fit the man from Sessions 1-12.

### 1. He regenerates

**[[Vaelin]] rolled Perception and watched his wounds visibly close.** His read: **Martavis has regeneration of some kind.** Nothing in twelve sessions has suggested this.

### 2. He never needs healing

Later, while [[Vaelin]] worked down the line healing the entire camp at 2d6 a round, the party **noticed Martavis doesn't need any healing.** This is a man who was beaten in single combat by [[Samus]] the day before.

### 3. He was demonstrably magically compromised

He spent part of the fight **charmed and attacking [[Kane]]**. Whatever charmed him was still in that camp.

### 4. The behavior turned

| Before | Session 14 |
|---|---|
| Ran soup kitchens under deep cover | **Murdered a warlock** he had visibly considered taking prisoner |
| Recovered the taxes because it was right | **Spat on the floor** where the succubus had been |
| Twelve sessions maneuvering to inherit the Zhentarim | **Renounced the inheritance** -- they're all *"devil worshippers"* |
| -- | **Assassinated every one of [[Samus]]'s personal troops**, casters first, an hour outside [[Dragonspear]] -- **after [[Vaelin]] explicitly advised him to hold back on the reaping** |
| -- | Expressed regret that **the other four succubi got away** |

### And four shapechangers escaped this camp

[[The Succubi Alliance]] -- **five succubi**, one killed, **four at large.** Succubi are shapechangers with telepathy and charm. They were embedded in this exact camp, with these exact people.

### The counter-argument, stated fairly

- **He has always killed surrendering enemies.** [[Session 09 - The Wolf and the Phoenix|Session 9]]: his men killed all survivors including one who surrendered. *"No witnesses."* This is established, not new
- **He was just beaten, held hostage, and magically violated.** That changes people
- **Regeneration could be an item, a boon, or Harper kit** the party has never been read in on
- **He distinguished his own people from Samus's** -- [[Eldrick]], a surrendered caster, was *not* killed in the reaping and is riding point
- **"Devil worshippers" is now literally true.** [[Samus]] had an active devil contract. Refusing to command those troops is arguably correct

> **The cheapest test is [[Kane]]'s Divine Sense, and it is free.**

**Was he charmed in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] too?**

His ***"Stand down"*** -- shouted at the party while his own Phoenix troops were **winning** the courtyard fight, to his men's visible confusion (*"What's going on? We were winning"*) -- has never made sense as a tactical call. If he was already charmed at the parley, **the entire Session 13 negotiation was staged around a compromised hostage.**

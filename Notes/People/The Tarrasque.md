# The Tarrasque

**Type:** Titan / Legendary Monster
**Status:** At large -- pursuing the surviving dragon outside [[Waterdeep]]
**First Encountered:** [[Session 11 - When the World Burns]]

## What It Is

A **Tarrasque** -- the iconic legendary monster of Faerûn. Believed by sages to appear when **world-threatening events occur** -- a cosmic immune response to existential threats.

The party saw it for the first time in [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] when, at dawn, **a great wave of seawater rolled into [[Waterdeep]]'s docks** and the Tarrasque rose out of it.

## Observed Behavior (Session 11)

- Emerged from the sea at the docks at dawn during the two-dragon attack on [[Waterdeep]]
- **Killed Ravica** (the black dragon) at the docks -- chased her down using a breath weapon
- Then **stomped through Waterdeep in pursuit of Rofidian** (the red dragon -- the giant fire-breather Kane saw on the roof)
- **Wizard spells just bounce off** -- Magic Resistance is total; spells cast at it had no effect
- **Crashed through the city wall** breaking the besieging army's lines on the way out
- Now somewhere outside the city, hunting the surviving dragon
- **Piles of dead in its wake** -- burned or trampled

## Capabilities (Observed)
- Breath weapon (something that "burns or stabs" -- the piles of dead were both burned and stabbed; need to determine if that was the Tarrasque or the dragons)
- **Effectively immune to ranged spell attacks** -- magic resistance is what was observed; in 5e RAW the Tarrasque has Magic Resistance + Reflective Carapace (reflects spell attacks)
- Capable of breaking down a city wall by running through it
- Tracking / pursuit behavior toward specific targets (the dragons)

## Alignment / Motivation
**Unknown.** The lore says it appears in response to world-threatening events. It killed one dragon and pursued another. So far its actions have **incidentally helped the city** -- the besieging army was scattered, the dragons were driven off or killed. But:
- It killed a lot of citizens in transit
- It is not a tame ally
- It will not respond to orders, requests, or diplomacy

**Best read:** the Tarrasque hunts whatever it perceives as the world-threatening element. The dragons (Rofidian and Ravica?) qualified. The city did not. **Once the dragons are dealt with, the Tarrasque's interest in the city should end.** Or it may return to the sea. Or it may decide [[Ahmendril]] himself, or the [[The Door of the Dead|Door]], is the actual threat -- and turn its attention to those.

## What Triggered It

Likely the convergence of:
- Two **ancient dragons** rising and attacking a major city
- [[Ahmendril]] holding a **Far Realm gateway piece** (see [[The Door of the Dead]])
- The active operation of the **[[Zanithar]]** mantle
- The two-disc ritual completing

Any one of these may have been sufficient. The combination definitely was.

## Tactical Implications for the Party

- **Do not fight it.** It is rated for parties multiple tiers above current level, and spells bounce off
- **Do not try to herd it, recruit it, or scry on it.** It is a force of nature
- **Use it as a clock.** While it's chasing the surviving dragon, that dragon isn't doing other damage. Window of opportunity for other priorities
- **Watch where it goes.** If it heads for [[Ahmendril]], that's intelligence. If it heads back to sea, the immediate threat phase is over

## Open Questions

## Forgotten Realms / D&D Lore

The Tarrasque is the iconic kaiju-tier legendary monster of D&D. In 5e RAW (Monster Manual):
- CR 30, Legendary
- **Magic Resistance** (advantage on saves against spells/magical effects)
- **Reflective Carapace** (chance to reflect spell attacks back at the caster)
- **Siege Monster** (extra damage to objects/structures)
- **Breath Weapon** (fire), **Bite**, **Claws**, **Horns**, **Tail** -- all hideously damaging
- Generally one of a kind; sometimes lore implies others exist underground or in distant seas
- Sleeps for centuries; awakens during cataclysms

In Forgotten Realms canon, it has historically been associated with cataclysmic events on the Sword Coast and the South. **Its appearance is a sign that something far worse is in motion** -- it doesn't cause cataclysms, it responds to them.

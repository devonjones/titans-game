# Allaina

**Race:** Avariel (Winged Elf)
**Role:** Priestess
**Status:** Unknown - likely alive in a mountain sanctuary

## Overview
A young avariel priestess who was a passenger on [[The Moonrunner]] during its final voyage. Cael saved her life during a Kuo-toa attack at a port town, nearly dying in the process. She left for the mountains with her people three days before the unnatural storm destroyed the ship.

## Relationships
- **Cael Stormcrow:** Friend. He saved her life during the Kuo-toa attack, taking a spear through the side. They spent time together in the crow's nest during the crossing, talking about navigation, weather-reading from altitude, and her people's mountain sanctuaries. He views her as a friend - one shore leave, one fight, but when someone faces death beside you, it counts.
- **[[The Moonrunner]]:** Passenger on the final voyage.

## History

### The Crossing
Allaina was among a group of aarakocra and avariel passengers on [[The Moonrunner]]'s final voyage. Rare enough that even in the Moonshaes, winged elves were more legend than reality. She was curious about navigation, about reading weather from altitude, about the birds Cael kept in the crow's nest. They talked during the crossing - he shared the practical knowledge of his trade, she told stories of mountain sanctuaries and ancient aeries.

### The Kuo-toa Attack
At a small island port during shore leave, Kuo-toa attacked the town. Allaina got cornered by two of them. Cael saw it happening, read the angles, knew the outcome - and went in anyway.

He nearly died for it. A Kuo-toa spear punched through his side. But others arrived, the tide turned, and the creatures were overcome.

Allaina's gratitude was genuine and direct. Cael appreciated that.

### Departure
She and her people left for the mountains where they intended to settle. Cael went back to the *Moonrunner* with fresh stitches and a new scar.

Three days later, the unnatural storm found the ship.

## The Open Question
Cael has wondered - was [[The Moonrunner]] targeted because of who they'd carried? Did someone want those passengers dead and settled for destroying the ship after they'd already left? The timing makes him think.

Eleven crew died. The passengers had already disembarked, safe in their mountains.

If they ever cross paths again, Cael would want to know she made it - that at least someone from that voyage survived to see what they were aiming for.

# Bathere

**Role:** Lord of [[Dragonspear]] Castle
**Status:** Enemy — currently in combat with the party
**Race/Type:** Unknown — goat-headed, wings grew from his back; likely a fiend or devil

## Overview
A goat-headed figure who claims [[Dragonspear]] Castle as his own. Commands revenants, undead mobs, shadows, and wraiths. Identified [[The Death Tyrant]] by name as **Ahmendril** — *"the one that you met."* Claims to have no use for [[Viggio Martel]] or the [[Zhentarim]], but initiated combat when questioned.

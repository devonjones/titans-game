# Bathere

**Role:** Former Lord of [[Dragonspear]] Castle
**Status:** **DEAD** — killed by the party in [[Session 07 - The Door of the Dead|Session 7]]
**Race/Type:** Unknown — goat-headed, wings grew from his back; likely a fiend or devil. Was the mage of the Dragonspear operation.
**Caution:** The **[[The Goat Head Mask]]** (his helm) is **still attuned to him** — a potential resurrection vector. The body and the mask should be treated as still dangerous.

## Overview
A goat-headed figure who claims [[Dragonspear]] Castle as his own. Commands revenants, undead mobs, shadows, and wraiths. Identified [[The Death Tyrant]] by name as **Ahmendril** — *"the one that you met."* Claims to have no use for [[Viggio Martel]] or the [[Zhentarim]], but initiated combat when questioned.

## What We Know
- Lords over Dragonspear Castle — claims ownership
- Commands powerful undead: revenants (at least two as sentinels), shadows, wraiths, undead mobs, skeletons, undead horses
- Knows [[The Death Tyrant|Ahmendril]] and can name him
- Dismissed [[Viggio Martel]] and the [[Zhentarim]] — laughed at the suggestion of working with Viggio
- The room he occupied was full of enemies — prepared for a fight
- Goat head + wings + fiendish appearance + Dragonspear's portal to Avernus = likely a **devil or fiend**

## Cause of Death
- **Killed by [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]]'s Lightning Ball** — the killing blow after [[Kane]] transferred his Hexblade Curse and [[Vaelin]]'s Dispel Magic stripped his regeneration
- Contributing damage: Celleborn's **Sickening Radiance**, Stormcrow's **Lightning Bolt** (27), Storm Sphere tick damage, Spirit Guardians

## What Bathere Was Actually Doing
Recovered from his study in [[Session 07 - The Door of the Dead|Session 7]]:
- **Researching how to create a [[Dracolich Research|dracolich]]** — either for himself or in service to [[The Death Tyrant|Ahmendril]]
- Kept a **large dead dragon** in the cellars — raw material for the ritual
- Was trying (and failing) to Legend Lore **[[The Door of the Dead]]**. Believed it was created by the **Dead Three**. Ahmendril took the Door before we arrived
- Bathere was **guarding the [[The Avernus Portal|sealed Avernus portal]]** — possibly as a contingency for the ritual, or as part of his role at Dragonspear
- **Had no use for [[Viggio Martel]] or the [[Zhentarim]]** — his operation was parallel to theirs, not part of it

### [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars]] -- Recontextualization
**[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] revealed: Bathere did NOT know [[Ahmendril]].** Their operations were parallel, not coordinated. Bathere's dracolich research arc was his own pursuit -- the dead dragon, the lich notes, [[The Door of the Dead]] he was studying. Ahmendril showed up at [[Dragonspear]] and **took the Door** for his own ritual (which is about reviving two ancient dragons, NOT his own transformation). Earlier framing of Bathere as Ahmendril's lieutenant or research partner is wrong; he was using Bathere's site, not directing him.

[[The Goat Head Mask]] is still attuned to Bathere's corpse -- shown to Silverhand who confirmed the attunement.

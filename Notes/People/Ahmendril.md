## TL;DR
- Current bearer of the **[[Zanithar]]** mantle (recurring role, periodically killed and replaced)
- **Holds 2 of 3 pieces** of [[The Gate of the Far Realms]]: [[Notes/Items/The Door of the Dead|the Door]] (taken Dragonspear) and [[Notes/Items/The Stolen Keystone|the Keystone]] (taken Waterdeep, Session 11)
- **Only the [[Notes/Items/The Key|Key]] remains uncollected** -- and it's **divinely concealed** ([[The Planetar's Warning]])
- Used the two discs to revive **[[Rofidian]]** (red, fled) and **[[Ravica]]** (black, killed by [[The Tarrasque|Tarrasque]])
- ~~Commands [[Samus]] who runs Zhent operations~~ **[[Samus]] IS DEAD** ([[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar|Session 14]]), killed by [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]]. Ahmendril has **lost his mortal operations chief**, and the only defector in his coalition died with him
- **Three named lieutenants** (Session 13): **[[Farraday]]** (raven mask, [[Waterdeep]]), **[[Celine]]** (Astral Plane), **[[Draymon]]** ([[Chult]])
- **AN EXPERT MASK MAKER** (Session 15). Keeps a **workshop / wizard's library with a wall of masks**; wears an **owl mask**; **a second owl mask sits on the wall**
- **The masks resurrect his lieutenants** -- see [[The Masks of Ahmendril]]. **THE CHAIN IS THE MASK**
- **Was a humanoid gish** in a vision from 100+ years ago -- elemental aura, drawn sword, a thrown 6th-level evocation. The beholder form is later
- **He recruits the people who beat him** -- [[Samus]] after defeating him, [[Farraday]] after she killed a [[Zanithar]]
- **He has magically edited [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s memory** -- she doodles his owl mask. See [[Silverhand's Missing Memory]]
- **His pendant has been at his neck for over a century** -- primary candidate for [[Notes/Items/The Key|the Key]]
- **Beaten once before**, ~100 years ago, by heroes including the [[Order of the Fist]]
- **Findable through the craft:** [[Neverwinter]], [[Baldur's Gate]], [[Waterdeep]]; masks sell at **Midsummer**
- **Magic suppression** via his death-tyrant eye (Session 12)
- **"Moves around a lot"** per the planetar -- no fixed location
- **The three lieutenants are now his entire visible structure.** [[Farraday]] is in [[Waterdeep]], and the party is headed there

# Celine

**Role:** Lieutenant of [[Ahmendril]]
**Status:** Enemy -- **usually in the Astral Plane**
**Named:** [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] (by [[Samus]])

## Overview

One of **three named lieutenants of [[Ahmendril]]**, disclosed by [[Samus]] during the [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] parley. [[Samus]] offered to release [[General Martavis|Martavis]] in exchange for the three lieutenants' helms.

> ***"Celine. Usually in the astral plane."*** -- [[Samus]]

Nothing else is known. No description, no capabilities, no mask or signature given.

## The Access Problem

**The party has no obvious way to reach the Astral Plane.**

Known options, none currently in hand:
- **Astral Projection** (9th-level cleric/wizard/warlock) -- well above the party's current tier
- **Plane Shift** (7th-level) -- [[Vaelin]] is the closest candidate but is not there yet
- A **portal, gate, or color pool** -- would need to be found
- **[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]** -- a Chosen of Mystra could plausibly send the party, and has already proven willing to teleport them into an assault

Practically: **Celine is the hardest of the three to reach**, and probably the last one the party gets to. [[Samus]] recommending [[Farraday]] first is consistent with him knowing this.

## Why the Astral Plane?

Speculative, but worth flagging:
- The Astral Plane is where **dead gods drift** -- and the campaign's antagonist arc runs through the [[The Dead Three|Dead Three]] and the [[The Far Realm|Far Realm]]
- It is also a **transit medium** -- anything staged there can reach anywhere
- If [[Ahmendril]] "moves around a lot" (per [[The Planetar's Warning|the planetar]]), an astral-based lieutenant is an obvious logistics hub
- **[[Notes/Items/The Key|The Key]] is divinely hidden.** A place outside the Material Plane, adjacent to dead divinity, is exactly the sort of place a divinely-concealed object might sit

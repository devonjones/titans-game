# Councilman Moore

**Role:** Council Member, Vestial Armor & Smiths
**Status:** Dead (killed the day before Session 1)

## Affiliations
- **[[Daggerford Council]]:** Member (deceased), Vestial Armor & Smiths

## Overview
Was Lady Morwen's ally on the council. Murdered the day before the party arrived.

## Relationships
- **[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]:** Was her ally on the council. His death is a major blow to her position.
- **[[Viggio Martel]]:** Enemy. Moore believes Viggio killed him.
- **[[Elaine Drago]]:** Fellow council member; likely allied given shared support of Lady Morwen.

## Death Details
- Killed by a single stab wound in the back (the killing blow)
- Multiple additional stab wounds in the chest (overkill/passion)
- Right hand index finger cut off post-mortem
- Other recent bodies also missing index fingers (pattern)

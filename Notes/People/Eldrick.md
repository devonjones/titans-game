# Eldrick

**Role:** Spellcaster serving [[General Martavis]]; **the owl-mage**
**Status:** Ally -- recovered from [[Samus]]'s keep, riding scout
**Familiar:** An **owl**
**Raw-notes spellings:** "Eldred," "Eldrick"

## Overview

The caster whose **owl carried [[General Martavis|Martavis]]'s note to the party in [[Session 09 - The Wolf and the Phoenix|Session 9]]** -- the *"Trust"* note that set up the flank attack on the Wolf army at the ford.

He was captured at some point before or during [[General Martavis|Martavis]]'s assault on the [[Troll Claw Hills]] HQ, and was **inside [[Samus]]'s keep** when the party arrived.

## The Session 13 Question, Answered

In [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]], [[Kane]] recognized **Martavis's owl-mage among the men inside the keep** during the parley huddle. The vault has carried the open question ever since:

> ***Captive, or turned?***

**Answer: captive.**

In [[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar|Session 14]], after [[Samus]]'s death and the general surrender:

- He was among the **surrendered spellcasters** -- the ones who *"look a little more disgruntled than the rest"*
- **He was badly hurt.** *"Eldred is particularly fucked up"*
- **[[Kane]] stopped his bleeding** personally, as part of turning on the diplomacy with that group
- By the road march to [[Daggerford]], **he and a familiar scout had ridden ahead**, with **his owl circling behind the column**

He was working for Martavis the whole time, held by Samus, and went straight back to work.

> **He survived [[General Martavis|Martavis]]'s reaping.** An hour outside [[Dragonspear]], Martavis assassinated all of [[Samus]]'s personal troops, **beginning with the casters.** Eldrick was a surrendered caster in that camp and he is riding point instead. That is a meaningful line: Martavis distinguished his own people from Samus's.

## Why He Matters

**He is the party's best inside witness to the keep.**

He was held in the building that produced, across two sessions:
- **Many casters** ([[Vaelin]]'s read)
- An **airborne warlock** -- Eldritch Blast + Repelling Blast, three beams, level 11+
- A **Fireball** for 36
- **An unidentified mind-control effect** that seized [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] and turned [[Lightning Ball]] on the party
- **Five succubi**, one killed and four escaped

**Nobody has interviewed him.** He can plausibly answer:
- **Who controlled [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]]** -- the single biggest unresolved question from [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]]
- Who the airborne warlock was, and whether he died
- How many succubi there were and **what the four who escaped look like**
- What [[Samus]]'s devil contract was, and **the devil's name** ([[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]])
- **Whether [[General Martavis|Martavis]] was charmed in Session 13**, and for how long
- What [[Samus's Helm|Samus's helm]] is

## Open Questions

1. **Eldred and Eldrick -- one man or two?** The raw notes use both within the same session. **Almost certainly one man**; both appear in the context of Martavis's casters, and only one owl-mage is on record. Worth a table confirmation
2. **Is he a [[Harpers|Harper]] too**, or just a Zhent caster loyal to Martavis personally?
3. **How long was he held, and what did he see?**
4. **Was he charmed?** Everyone in that camp is now suspect. [[Vaelin]]'s aura would have stripped it
5. **What can his owl do for the party?** Familiar scouting is exactly what the party lacks on the road

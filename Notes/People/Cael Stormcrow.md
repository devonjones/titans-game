# Cael Stormcrow

![[cael2.png|200]]

**Player:** Devon
**Race:** Variant Human (Moonshae Ffolk)
**Class:** Storm Sorcerer 8 / Tempest Cleric 2
**Level:** 10
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral

## Affiliations
- **[[Emerald Enclave]]:** Member - "Convergence" or "The Walking Storm"
- **[[The Moonrunner]]:** Former crew (Navigation Hand) - sole survivor
- **[[The Party]]:** Member
- **Daggerford Ducal Guard:** Appointed by [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]], reporting to [[Captain Hidar]]

## Overview
Former crow's nest navigator on the *Moonrunner* out of Moonshae. Sole survivor when an unnatural storm destroyed the ship five years ago. Lightning struck him and he became a convergence of sky and sea - touched by both Akadi (Lady of Air) and Istishia (Lord of Water). The Emerald Enclave gave him purpose when he needed it most.

## Appearance
- White hair (bleached from the roots by Akadi's touch)
- Storm-gray eyes that shift to blue-green when emotional
- Lightning scars (Lichtenberg patterns) across back, shoulders, and right arm
- Wears the Storm Cage (copper Faraday cage armor)
- Carries The Fulcrum (copper belaying pin focus)

## Personality
- Direct and plainspoken - calls things what they are
- Dry, dark humor ("Could be worse. Could be on fire AND sinking.")
- Tactically engaged - thinks in positioning and backup plans
- The weather responds to extreme emotions

## Relationships
- **[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]:** Allied. She watched him stand in a pillar of lightning for six hours during the Daggerford Cleansing. Her file on him: "Useful. Reliable. Requires purpose or becomes dangerous."
- **[[Kane]]:** Met at the Hilltop Defense eight months ago. "Storm and shadow" - the Ashen Spears' name for them fighting together.
- **[[Allaina]]:** Avariel priestess he saved during a Kuo-toa attack, days before the *Moonrunner* went down. Views her as a friend.
- **Dara Thornmantle:** Enclave ranger who found him and brought him in.
- **Therin:** Enclave druid who trained him to channel rather than suppress.

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]
Arrived in Daggerford with the party. Mentioned [[Viggio Martel]]'s party invitation to [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]. Present at the morgue for Speak with Dead on [[Councilman Moore]].

### [[Session 02 - Riverdale]]
Lightning bolted the bridge approach at [[Riverdale]]. Entered the tavern from the front. Hit a bandit with chromatic orb, demanded surrender from another. Interrogated the [[Roderick]]: *"Rosey palm and her five sisters are a sailor's best friend. You almost lost that friend today."* The bandit recognized him: *"Oh my god, you're Stormcrow."* Went with [[Vaelin]] to recover prisoners - found two dead, took the survivor.

## The Haunting
Eleven people died when he lived. He doesn't know if he earned that or just got lucky. Their names: Captain Brennan, First Mate Kyla, and nine others whose faces he remembers in dreams.

## Overview
A cleric who keeps his faith close to his chest. Wears a cloak over armor and carries a plain, totally non-reflective shield. The lack of ornamentation and reflection suggests either humility or a desire to remain unnoticed.

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]
Arrived in Daggerford with the party. Cast Speak with Dead on [[Councilman Moore]]'s corpse at the morgue, learning that Moore believes [[Viggio Martel]] killed him. The morgue dwarf asked if Vaelin can locate objects - a potential lead.

### [[Session 02 - Riverdale]]
Led the interrogation of the [[Roderick]] at [[Riverdale]] with creative menace: *"Do you know what you had for your last meal, or would you like it to be a mystery?"* Extracted intel about [[Merron]]'s camp and the stolen taxes. Showed mercy by healing the bandit's injured hand and telling him he was at a crossroads. Went with Stormcrow to recover prisoners from the tavern afterward - found two dead, one alive.

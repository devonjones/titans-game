## Overview
A cleric who keeps his faith close to his chest. Wears a cloak over armor and carries a plain, totally non-reflective shield. The lack of ornamentation and reflection suggests either humility or a desire to remain unnoticed.

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]
Arrived in Daggerford with the party. Cast Speak with Dead on [[Councilman Moore]]'s corpse at the morgue, learning that Moore believes [[Viggio Martel]] killed him. The morgue dwarf asked if Vaelin can locate objects - a potential lead.

### [[Session 02 - Riverdale]]
Led the interrogation of [[Roderick]] at [[Riverdale]] with creative menace: *"Do you know what you had for your last meal, or would you like it to be a mystery?"* Extracted intel about [[Merron]]'s camp and the stolen taxes. Showed mercy by healing the bandit's injured hand and telling him he was at a crossroads. Went with Stormcrow to recover prisoners from the tavern afterward - found two dead, one alive.

### [[Session 03 - Through the Wall]]
The session's MVP. Proposed using *Locate Object* to track the missing fingers; requested horses from [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] for the trip into town. Followed the ping to [[Viggio Martel]]'s manor (#23) — the fingers are there. Narrowed the cast to specifically the dwarf victim's finger; still confirmed. At [[Viggio's Party|the party]], cast again — **ground floor ping**. The fingers are present in the building.

Made the key contact of the evening: chatted with [[Elaine Drago]], who greeted him as *"one of Morwen's friends from out of town."* She asked — carefully, positioning so Viggio couldn't see — whether the party was there to investigate the deaths. Confirmed she opposes Viggio and is aware of the disappearances.

### [[Session 04 - Good Timing]]
**Resurrected [[Margeet Patel]]** using a ring Storm pulled from her dead finger (a diamond of sufficient value). Stayed with [[Elaine Drago]] to keep her safe while others investigated.

In the secret room: cast *Locate Object* again — the ping passed through [[Viggio Martel|Viggio]] (standing in the doorway) and continued beyond, leading to the chest. Co-deceived Viggio with Storm, implying they were looking for the assassin's weapon rather than the fingers.

At the bottom of the stairs: cast *Darkvision* (300-foot range) on the entire party. Healed [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] (13 hp) before the confrontation. **Notified [[Captain Hidar]] telepathically**: *"Viggio guilty, escaped. Send troops to Viggio's manor. We have Elaine and Margeet here, safe."*

Witnessed [[The Death Tyrant]] and the [[The Dead Three Temple|Dead Three temple]]. Stuck with [[Elaine Drago]] through the chaos.

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]
Mentioned in the Seneschal's dossier as neutral but "might be good, strangely." Not directly encountered.

### [[Session 03 - Through the Wall]]
First direct encounter at [[Viggio's Party|Viggio's party]]. Confirmed as an opinion-maker in the room — the kind of person others look to. [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] wingmanned [[Kane]] to her. She and Kane exchanged flirtatious pleasantries, then slipped off together to the sitting room — without a chaperone. While there, [[Kane]] discovered dampness on the floor and identified wet footprints that appear to walk through a wall. Possible secret passage.

### [[Session 04 - Good Timing]]
**Killed** by [[The Assassin]] in the hall outside the sitting room — fled when [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] propelled her out, but didn't make it. Storm found her body.

**Resurrected** by [[Vaelin]], using a ring Storm removed from her dead finger — a diamond ring with sufficient value for the spell. Received multiple heals. First words upon returning: *"What happened?"* Kane: *"Assassin. They suck."*

Kept safe by [[Kane]] through the remainder of the session.

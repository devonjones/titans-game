# Rofidian

**Type:** Ancient Red Dragon (revived)
**Status:** **FACE TO FACE WITH THE PARTY** as of [[Session 17 - Something That Belongs to Me|Session 17]]
**First Named:** [[Session 05 - Signed S]] (in the Abyssal ritual scroll)
**Resurrected:** Off-screen prior to [[Session 11 - When the World Burns]]

## Summary

One of two **ancient dragons** that [[Ahmendril]] revived using the two-disc ritual (see [[The Disk]]). Named in the Abyssal vellum scroll alongside **[[Ravica]]**. Attacked [[Waterdeep]] in [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] -- Kane saw him on the roof, **a giant dragon breathing fire**.

When the [[The Tarrasque|Tarrasque]] rose from the sea and killed [[Ravica]] at the docks, Rofidian was the second dragon the Tarrasque pursued. **Crashed through the city wall and fled into the wider world, Tarrasque on his tail.**

## ⚠️ HE LANDED IN FRONT OF US (Session 17)

At the end of [[Session 17 - Something That Belongs to Me|Session 17]], with the devils routed:

- **The dragon flew over the battlefield and the magic went away**
- **It is a red dragon.** It landed
- **He does not seem to like the paladins**
- > ### ***"I believe you have something that belongs to me."***

**He has evidently lost the [[The Tarrasque|Tarrasque]].**

*(Identification is by elimination -- the campaign has exactly one ancient red dragon. Not stated by name at the table.)*

### What does he want?

> **⚠️ CORRECTED.** The object the vault called a *"dragon-control scroll"* is **[[The Ritual Tablet]]** — a **resurrection ritual that only *potentially* confers control**, and the GM is explicit that *"potentially"* is doing the heavy lifting. **The party has no working leash on this dragon and never did.** See [[The Disk]].

**Leading candidate: [[The Ritual Tablet]] — which the party does hold.**

It is the ritual that **raised him.** It does not command him, but it is *his*, and a dragon will not tolerate it in someone else's hands. **He does not need it to work; he needs it gone.** And if the party's seven-session belief that it held a leash ever leaked in-fiction — a briefing, the council, the note left with [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] in [[Session 16 - The Masks I Am Owed|Session 16]] — **the party advertised exactly the rumour that brings a dragon to the door.**

**Second candidate: whatever [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s father took from a dragon hoard.**

In [[Session 16 - The Masks I Am Owed|Session 16]], **Celleborn was called away by his father to research *"something they got from a dragon hoard."*** One session later an **ancient red dragon** lands in front of the party and says *"I believe you have something that belongs to me."*

**Dragon-hoard material, in the hands of the party's own family, immediately before a dragon turns up claiming property.** That is the tightest fit on the board, and it means the thing he wants may not even be on this battlefield.

**Other candidates:**
- **[[Ravica]]'s corpse** — his paired dragon, killed by the [[The Tarrasque|Tarrasque]], and **being harvested for armor and weapons** by Celleborn's father's team under Gentle Repose in [[Waterdeep]]. A red dragon objecting to his counterpart being butchered for parts is not a stretch
- **A mask.** [[Farraday]] stole [[The Goat Head Mask|the goat mask]] **out of a dragon's lair** ([[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]]) — though the **pool of acid** there points black rather than red. **[[Kane]] is carrying [[Samus's Helm|another mask]] right now**, and a devil on this same field spent the whole session demanding masks it claimed it was owed
- Hoard taken from anywhere else the party has been

### The antimagic

**"We feel the magic go away"** as he flew over. **This is the second time** -- the first was in [[Session 16 - The Masks I Am Owed|Session 16]], immediately before **[[Ahmendril]] appeared**, and was attributed to Ahmendril's death-tyrant suppression.

**So either Rofidian carries his own, or [[Ahmendril]] arrived with him.** Nobody saw Ahmendril in Session 17.

### The decision in front of the party

**The party holds [[The Ritual Tablet]] and has no control over him at all.** Those are both true at once, and that is the whole shape of this negotiation.

**But he talked first** — so did the pit fiend. **The first job is finding out what he actually means**, because the party may be holding it without knowing, or it may be in [[Waterdeep]] in Celleborn's father's workshop.

Refusing starts a CR 24 fight at the end of a session that put [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] on the floor and took [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] from 208 to 60 hit points. **Ask before offering.**

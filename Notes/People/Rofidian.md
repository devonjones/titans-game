# Rofidian

**Type:** Ancient Red Dragon (revived)
**Status:** **FACE TO FACE WITH THE PARTY** as of [[Session 17 - Something That Belongs to Me|Session 17]]
**First Named:** [[Session 05 - Signed S]] (in the Abyssal ritual scroll)
**Resurrected:** Off-screen prior to [[Session 11 - When the World Burns]]

## Summary

One of two **ancient dragons** that [[Ahmendril]] revived using the two-disc ritual (see [[The Disk]]). Named in the Abyssal vellum scroll alongside **[[Ravica]]**. Attacked [[Waterdeep]] in [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] -- Kane saw him on the roof, **a giant dragon breathing fire**.

When the [[The Tarrasque|Tarrasque]] rose from the sea and killed [[Ravica]] at the docks, Rofidian was the second dragon the Tarrasque pursued. **Crashed through the city wall and fled into the wider world, Tarrasque on his tail.**

## ⚠️ HE LANDED IN FRONT OF US (Session 17)

At the end of [[Session 17 - Something That Belongs to Me|Session 17]], with the devils routed:

- **The dragon flew over the battlefield and the magic went away**
- **It is a red dragon.** It landed
- **He does not seem to like the paladins**
- > ### ***"I believe you have something that belongs to me."***

**He has evidently lost the [[The Tarrasque|Tarrasque]].**

*(Identification is by elimination -- the campaign has exactly one ancient red dragon. Not stated by name at the table.)*

### What does he want?

**Leading candidate: the Abyssal control scroll, in [[Vaelin]]'s possession.**

Per [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] in [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]], the scroll is **dragon-control instructions** -- *whoever holds it commands the revived dragons.* This vault has called it **"the only known leash"** on Rofidian for seven sessions.

**A dragon would come for the object that commands him.** And it is standing in front of him, in a cleric's pack.

**Other candidates:**
- **[[Ravica]]'s corpse** -- his paired dragon, killed by the [[The Tarrasque|Tarrasque]], **harvested for armor and weapons** by [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s father's team and held under Gentle Repose in [[Waterdeep]]
- **A mask.** [[Farraday]] stole [[The Goat Head Mask|the goat mask]] **out of a dragon's lair** ([[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]]). That lair had a **pool of acid** -- which points black, not red -- **but [[Kane]] is carrying [[Samus's Helm|a mask]] right now**, and a devil on this same field spent the session demanding masks
- Hoard taken from anywhere the party has been

### The antimagic

**"We feel the magic go away"** as he flew over. **This is the second time** -- the first was in [[Session 16 - The Masks I Am Owed|Session 16]], immediately before **[[Ahmendril]] appeared**, and was attributed to Ahmendril's death-tyrant suppression.

**So either Rofidian carries his own, or [[Ahmendril]] arrived with him.** Nobody saw Ahmendril in Session 17.

### The decision in front of the party

**Hand over the scroll and the party disarms itself of the only leash on an ancient red dragon. Refuse, and they start a CR 24 fight at the end of a session that nearly killed [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] and took [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] from 208 to 60.**

**He talked first.** So did the pit fiend. **There is a negotiation here.**

## Tactical / Strategic

- **The control scroll** (Abyssal vellum, in [[Vaelin]]'s possession) supposedly commands the revived dragons. Whether it works on Rofidian, requires the original ritualist to invoke, or is inert without further action is unknown. **Have not tested.**
- If the Tarrasque kills him, the threat ends (for one dragon)
- If Rofidian survives the Tarrasque, he becomes a free-roaming ancient red dragon in the world -- a campaign-shaping threat
- [[Ahmendril]] may still believe he controls Rofidian; the dragons' Waterdeep attack may have been Ahmendril's order, or the dragons acting on their own revival imperatives

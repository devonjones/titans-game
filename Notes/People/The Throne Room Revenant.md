# The Throne Room Revenant

![[revenant.webp]]

**Role:** Sentinel at [[Bathere]]'s throne room
**Status:** Unknown — left behind when the party went upstairs
**Type:** Revenant (probable)

## Overview
A girlish undead figure standing beside [[Bathere]]'s throne in the reception room of [[Dragonspear]] Castle. Spoke with the party and directed them upstairs. Has a distinct personality — answered Kane's question with attitude rather than hostility.

## Appearance
- Young woman, undead — pale skin, white hair, armored
- Distinctive and individual, not a mindless undead
- Stands as a sentinel beside the empty throne

## What She Said
- *"Who wants to know?"* — when Kane asked where her master was
- *"I am your doom. The one you seek is up through those doors and up those stairs."*

## Notes
- Revenants are powerful undead driven by a specific purpose or vengeance — she may have her own story
- Directed the party to Bathere without resistance — following orders, or indifferent?
- A second revenant was encountered on the grand staircase above
- The GM's comment to study Viseli's Cloak came during this sequence — may be relevant to future encounters with her

# Farraday

**Role:** Lieutenant of [[Ahmendril]]
**Status:** Enemy -- **confirmed in [[Waterdeep]], living in [[The Waterdeep Sewers|the sewers]].** [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] has given the party a location
**Class:** **Warlock** (shadow/fiend patron -- purple energy, elemental summoning)
**Age:** **130+.** Orphaned ~100 years ago as a pre-teen; **died and returned after an ~80-year gap**
**Mark:** Wears a **raven's head mask**
**Named:** [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] (by [[Samus]])

## Overview

One of **three named lieutenants of [[Ahmendril]]**, disclosed by [[Samus]] during the [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] parley in the [[Troll Claw Hills]]. Samus offered to release [[General Martavis|Martavis]] in exchange for the lieutenants' helms, and **specifically recommended killing Farraday first**.

> ***"Farraday. Wears a raven's head mask. In Waterdeep."*** -- [[Samus]]

## ✅ THE SESSION 10 IDENTIFICATION IS CONFIRMED

**[[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]] settled it.** [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s Legend Lore vision ended with **Farraday descending on [[Ellenfear Manor|Celleborn's parents' house]] -- and the party watching themselves being attacked.** She flees; the vision ends.

**She is the raven-masked attacker who nearly killed [[Cael Stormcrow|Cael]]** (two failed death saves) and went out the window.

**And [[Ahmendril]] briefed her personally immediately before the hit.**

## Her Life (Legend Lore, Session 15)

Two castings. [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s covered the middle and late years; **[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s -- made after [[Kane]] showed her Farraday's face with Minor Illusion -- reached back to childhood.**

### Origin

- A **raven-haired girl, pre-teen**, at home with her parents. She goes off to school
- **Chaos in the city. A battle.** She makes her way home. **Her parents do not**
- **Street urchin. A life of crime**
- **Cornered, she calls out for power**

**Power answers.**

- **Shadows form**
- **Purple streaks of energy shoot from her hands**

She grows, becomes powerful, **joins a group of adventurers**, comes home wealthier and stronger. **The sense is that she wanted to clean up the criminal element in her town.**

> **She is a warlock**, and she was **a hero** -- an orphan who clawed up out of the gutter and went home to fix it.
>
> [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] was touched. *"Poor thing."*

### The Sewer Campaign

She slips down an alley into **[[The Waterdeep Sewers|the sewers]]**, joined by **friends who dwell in the shadows**. Through complex tunnels. They surround and wipe out **a meeting of underground criminals**, then keep killing their way deeper --

**until they reach a Beholder. A [[Zanithar]]. And they kill him.**

- **Elementals rise in the room from her hand**
- **They come at him from all angles so he doesn't know where to attack** -- a textbook answer to a beholder's antimagic cone
- They gank him

**She killed one of [[Ahmendril]]'s predecessors.**

### Recruitment

She comes out of the sewers and goes into a home. **An owl-faced figure is standing there -- [[Ahmendril]].**

**They have tea.** They talk. **They come to an agreement.**

He takes her to the **workshop**, where the mask collection has grown since the [[Samus]] vision. **He crafts a raven mask for her.** She nods and disappears into the city.

> **This is his method.** He recruits the people who beat him. See [[The Masks of Ahmendril]].

### Death

Later he appears again. **They go up an invisible flight of stairs.** A group is there, **all wearing masks.**

**They all see a god. It seems like it might be Helm.**

**They fight.** A band of heroes joins, and **[[Ahmendril]]'s side loses.**

- **A ram's-head figure** -- [[Samus]] -- **is stabbed in the chest by a dark elf paladin: female, white hair, BLUE FIST ON HER SHIELD** ([[Order of the Fist]])
- **Farraday dies**

**And then she is kneeling before [[Ahmendril]]. The era has changed.**

### The Dragon's Lair

They talk again. She nods. She goes outside the city.

**She and her crew scale a mountain.** They wait. **A dragon leaves.** They enter the lair.

**She finds another mask settled in a pool of acid. Well-hidden.**

**They bring it back to [[Ahmendril]]. IT WAS [[The Goat Head Mask|THE GOAT MASK]].**

> **She is the reason [[Bathere]] had that mask.** And the party has been carrying it since [[Session 07 - The Door of the Dead|Session 7]].
>
> **Acid pool = black dragon.** The campaign's is **[[Ravica]]**.

### Present Day

**She returns to [[The Waterdeep Sewers|the sewers]] and sets up a home. Keeps a low profile.**

Talks to [[Ahmendril]]. **Then hits [[Ellenfear Manor]].**

## The Session 10 Fight (now attributed)

**The party has already fought her.**

In [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]], the [[The Pencheska Conspiracy|Pencheska]] night attack on [[Ellenfear Manor]] included a figure that [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] spotted with **Detect Invisibility**: *"raven mask, leather armor, light clothing."* That attacker fought invisibly, nearly killed [[Cael Stormcrow|Cael]], and **escaped by jumping out a window**. Per [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] follow-up, the figure who went over the bannister **flew away**.

Devon's note at the table when Samus named her: *"She had tried to kill us and jumped out a window, invisible."*

**Confirmed in [[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]].**

## Why This Matters

The [[The Pencheska Conspiracy|Pencheska Conspiracy]] is not a parallel organization riding the same wave -- **it is [[Ahmendril]]'s operation, run by his named lieutenant.** The raven-mask iconography is **Farraday's personal signature**.

That collapses several open questions at once:
- The Session 10 attack was an Ahmendril operation
- The [[The Stolen Keystone|keystone theft]] in [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] plausibly falls under the same lieutenant
- The "arch-wizard" whose teleport runes [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] couldn't read may be Farraday's asset -- or Farraday herself

## Known Capabilities (from Session 10, if the ID holds)

- **Invisibility** -- operated invisibly through the fight
- **Magical flight** -- flew away after going over the bannister
- **Poison** -- Cael failed a poison save and gained the Poisoned condition
- **Heavy melee damage** -- 28-damage stabs, repeatedly
- **Force damage** attack -- Celleborn had to Counterspell one
- **Disciplined withdrawal** -- extracted rather than dying in place
- **Pre-positioned intelligence** -- knew where the party would be sleeping despite a last-minute change of plan

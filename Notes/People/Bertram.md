# Bertram

**Role:** Dwarf Sergeant, Daggerford City Guard
**Status:** Ally

## Overview
Sergeant commanding the Duchess's troops that arrived to secure [[Viggio Martel]]'s manor after the party cleared the Dead Three temple.

## Session Appearances

Arrived with City Guard troops to secure Viggio's manor. Reported that [[Captain Hidar]] went back to guard [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] personally when he learned Viggio had escaped. Accompanied the party to notify the Zhentarim captain that his duties at the manor were done.

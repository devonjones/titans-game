# The Assassin

**Role:** Unknown — hired killer or operative
**Status:** Enemy
**Identity:** Unknown

## Overview
An invisible operative who attacked [[Kane]] in the sitting room during [[Viggio's Party|Viggio's party]], nearly killing him in a single strike. Used an **[[Aura of Darkness]]** as cover — a specific ability, not a spell. Escaped through an exterior door before being identified.

## Capabilities
- **Invisibility** — operated invisibly throughout
- **Aura of Darkness** — filled the sitting room with magical darkness; the aura moved with them and exited through a door
- **Blade attack** — 73 hp damage to [[Kane]] in one hit; nearly lethal
- **Necrotic damage** — attacks dealt necrotic damage to [[Viseli Bar]]
- High AC or evasion — Viseli's hits connected but the creature escaped

## What They Said
*"You are not supposed to be here."*
— Whispered in Kane's ear before the attack. Implies prior intelligence about the party's presence and orders about who should or shouldn't be in that room.

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 04 - Good Timing]]
Attacked [[Kane]] in the sitting room (73 hp, one hit), then fought [[Viseli Bar]] in the dark. Rolled two natural 1s at advantage against Viseli — still dealt 33 total necrotic damage. Escaped via exterior door when Viseli got her size increase and began dealing serious damage.

## The Samus Parallel (Session 13)

**[[Samus]] produces darkness the same way this creature did.**

In [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]], Samus *"opens his mouth and a cloud of black ink covers the area."* [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] then failed repeated dispel attempts, explicitly noting he was casting **on the effect, not on the spell** — the behavior of a non-spell darkness ability, exactly like this creature's **[[Aura of Darkness]]**.

Shared profile:
| Trait | [[The Assassin]] (S4) | [[Samus]] (S13) |
|---|---|---|
| Darkness | Aura of Darkness — an ability, not a spell | Black ink from the mouth — resists dispel-as-spell |
| Melee lethality | 73 hp to [[Kane]] in one hit | Beat [[General Martavis|Martavis]] in single combat |
| Hard to affect | Survived Viseli's hits, escaped clean | Nat 20 + *"chose to succeed"* on a DC 19 save |
| Fate | **Escaped. Still out there** | **Dead (Session 14)** -- 82 damage from [[Kane]] in two rounds, then decapitated by [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] through a closed door |
| Infernal ties | Unknown | **Confirmed** -- devil contract, five succubi on staff |

Add that **something is on Samus's face** garbling his speech even after he removed his hat, and there is a live question about whether these two are **the same kind of creature** — or the same organization's signature equipment.

**Counter-consideration:** [[Farraday]] is now the leading candidate for the [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]] raven-masked attacker, and she also fought invisibly. Three separate enemies with invisibility/darkness kits is a pattern worth naming even if they're distinct individuals.

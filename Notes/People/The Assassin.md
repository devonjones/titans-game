# The Assassin

**Role:** Unknown — hired killer or operative
**Status:** Enemy
**Identity:** Unknown

## Overview
An invisible operative who attacked [[Kane]] in the sitting room during [[Viggio's Party|Viggio's party]], nearly killing him in a single strike. Used an **[[Aura of Darkness]]** as cover — a specific ability, not a spell. Escaped through an exterior door before being identified.

## Capabilities
- **Invisibility** — operated invisibly throughout
- **Aura of Darkness** — filled the sitting room with magical darkness; the aura moved with them and exited through a door
- **Blade attack** — 73 hp damage to [[Kane]] in one hit; nearly lethal
- **Necrotic damage** — attacks dealt necrotic damage to [[Viseli Bar]]
- High AC or evasion — Viseli's hits connected but the creature escaped

## What They Said
*"You are not supposed to be here."*
— Whispered in Kane's ear before the attack. Implies prior intelligence about the party's presence and orders about who should or shouldn't be in that room.

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 04 - Good Timing]]
Attacked [[Kane]] in the sitting room (73 hp, one hit), then fought [[Viseli Bar]] in the dark. Rolled two natural 1s at advantage against Viseli — still dealt 33 total necrotic damage. Escaped via exterior door when Viseli got her size increase and began dealing serious damage.

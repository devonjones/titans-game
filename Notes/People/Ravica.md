# Ravica

**Type:** Ancient Black Dragon (revived)
**Status:** **Dead.** Killed by the [[The Tarrasque|Tarrasque]] at the [[Waterdeep]] docks
**First Named:** [[Session 05 - Signed S]] (in the Abyssal ritual scroll)
**Resurrected:** Off-screen prior to [[Session 11 - When the World Burns]]
**Killed:** [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]]

## Summary

One of two **ancient dragons** that [[Ahmendril]] revived using the two-disc ritual (see [[The Disk]]). Named in the Abyssal vellum scroll alongside **[[Rofidian]]**. Attacked [[Waterdeep]] in [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] -- [[Vaelin]] saw her on the roof, **a second dragon toward the docks**.

When the [[The Tarrasque|Tarrasque]] rose from the sea at dawn, **it killed Ravica first**, using a breath weapon to chase her down at the docks, before turning to pursue [[Rofidian]].

## What We Know

- **Black** dragon (matches the black half of the two-disc yin-yang ritual)
- **Ancient** size category
- Operated near the docks during the attack (consistent with black dragons' affinity for water)
- Killed before she could do significant damage beyond the docks district
- The two-disc ritual that revived her is from the [[The Dragoncult Wars|Dragoncult Wars]] era

## Tactical / Strategic Notes

- **Her death does NOT end the threat.** The dragons may be a means, not the end -- [[Ahmendril]] still holds [[The Door of the Dead]] and the [[The Far Realm|Far Realm gateway]] plan continues
- The body / corpse of an ancient dragon could be reanimated (see [[Bathere]]'s [[Dracolich Research|dracolich research]]) -- **the corpse is a strategic concern** if it's still at the [[Waterdeep]] docks
- [[The Disk]] that was drained for her revival is presumably now expended

# The Chultan Visitors

**Type:** Unknown — scaled humanoids under magical disguise
**Status:** **At large in [[Waterdeep]]. Unexamined**
**Encountered:** [[Session 16 - The Masks I Am Owed|Session 16]]

## What Happened

Walking the streets of [[Waterdeep]], **[[Vaelin]] noticed that some people were disguised — or possibly illusory.** They appeared to be **tourists**.

The party offered to help them find their way in the city.

**[[Vaelin]], casually touching one of them, noticed SCALES.**

**They said they were just visitors from [[Chult]], trying not to cause offense with their appearance.**

A conversation followed.

## Why This Matters

**[[Chult]] is where [[Draymon]] is** — one of [[Ahmendril]]'s three named lieutenants, disclosed by [[Samus]] in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]].

The vault's standing read on [[Chult]] has been that it is **the most distant and least reachable** of the three lieutenant postings.

**And now Chultans are standing in [[Waterdeep]] under magical disguise**, in the same city where [[Farraday]] runs [[Ahmendril]]'s operations out of [[The Waterdeep Sewers|the sewers]].

## Three Readings

**1. Innocent.** Yuan-ti, lizardfolk, tabaxi, or another scaled Chultan people, disguised to avoid alarming a xenophobic city that has just been attacked by two dragons and a tarrasque. **Their stated reason is entirely reasonable**, and the party was offering directions, not interrogating them.

**2. [[Draymon]]'s people.** The Chult posting arriving in [[Waterdeep]] — possibly on the same business [[Farraday]] conducts here, possibly to collect something.

**3. Yuan-ti specifically.** In [[Chult]], yuan-ti means Dendar, the Soulmonger, and death-cult infrastructure — **precisely the profile of a [[Zanithar]] operation.** Yuan-ti malison and abomination both have innate spellcasting including **Disguise Self at will**, which matches exactly what [[Vaelin]] saw.

The party offered to help them find their way and moved on.

> **This is the cheapest open thread in the campaign to close and the easiest to lose.** They are mobile, disguised, and unidentified, in a city of a hundred thousand people.

## Open Questions

1. **Who are they?** Species, names, number
2. **Are they connected to [[Draymon]]?**
3. **Why [[Waterdeep]], and why now?** The city is mid-reconstruction with its wall breached
4. **Were they disguised, or illusory?** [[Vaelin]] flagged both possibilities — and he felt **scales**, which means something physical was there
5. **Did they seek the party out,** or was the encounter chance? **The party approached them**
6. **Can they be found again?**

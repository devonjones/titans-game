# Lady Estrell

**Role:** Wizard
**Status:** Ally — **presented to the party by [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]** in [[Session 16 - The Masks I Am Owed|Session 16]]
**Introduced:** [[Session 16 - The Masks I Am Owed|Session 16]]

## Overview

A wizard **[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] presented to the party** at the [[Lords' Alliance]] council, as they prepared to assault [[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]].

She is **the arcane replacement for [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]**, who was called away by his father that session.

## Observed Capability

In the opening rounds against the pit fiend:

- **Tendrils of earth broke from the ground and wrapped around the pit fiend.** He broke out of them

*(Spell unidentified — a grapple/restrain effect. **Maelstrom**, **Transmute Rock**, or similar. It cost the pit fiend a turn even on a successful escape.)*

**[[Vaelin]] gave her 39 temporary HP** before the fight — the largest single allocation, suggesting she is squishy and the party values her.

## ⚠️ Vetting

**[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] assigned her — and Silverhand is magically compromised.**

See [[Silverhand's Missing Memory]]: she was charmed into forgetting something important, and **doodles [[Ahmendril]]'s owl mask** without knowing why. Her judgment about [[Unthar Froom]] already looks like it may have been tampered with.

**This is not an accusation.** Estrell fought the pit fiend on the party's side in round one, which is more than most plants would do. But:

- **Nobody has cast anything on her**
- **Nobody knows who she is** beyond "a wizard"
- **Four succubi from [[Samus]]'s camp are still unaccounted for** ([[The Succubi Alliance]]) — shapechangers, at large
- The party was, that same session, **touching disguised strangers on the street and finding scales** ([[The Chultan Visitors]])

**[[Kane]]'s Divine Sense is free and detects fiends and shapechangers within 60 feet.** Worth using once, on everyone new.

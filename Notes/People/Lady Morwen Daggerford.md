**Role:** Duchess of Daggerford
**Status:** Ally

## Overview
The ruler of Daggerford, currently in a weakened position with her power usurped by the council and Zhentarim forces.

## History with the Party
### Cael - The Daggerford Cleansing (2 years ago)
Aberrant corruption emerged from the Delimbiyr River - twisted things trying to use water and weather for unnatural purposes. Cael walked into the river and called the storm down on himself for six hours straight while Emerald Enclave druids and Lady Morwen's soldiers dealt with the corruption. Locals say he stood in a pillar of lightning and never moved. Lady Morwen watched from the walls.

Afterward, she approached him directly: *"Are you in control of that, or is it in control of you?"* His answer: *"Yes."* She's trusted him since - not because he's safe, but because he's useful and honest about what he is.

### Kane - Preventing the Zhentarim Takeover (years ago)
Kane, working with the Harpers, provided intelligence that prevented an earlier Zhentarim takeover of Daggerford. The nature of their relationship suggests this wasn't a single incident but ongoing cooperation. When the party arrived at the manor, she walked directly to Kane and hugged him - a level of familiarity that goes beyond professional respect.

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Morwen_Daggerford). Campaign may diverge.*

### Background
Lady Morwen is the elder sister of Duke Maldwyn Daggerford. Despite the tradition of primogeniture passing the title to the eldest male, many in town believed Morwen was better suited to rule. In 1486 DR, when primogeniture was suspended, she officially became Duchess of Daggerford.

### Character
As a young adult, Morwen joined the Daggerford militia at the lowest rank and worked her way up to commanding general over a decade. She regularly trains with the militia and pays visits to the shrine of Tempus (god of war). She is described as a capable and charismatic leader.

### The Pencheska Conspiracy (Canon Warning)
In canonical FR lore, a succubus named **Pencheska** (originally summoned by the Red Wizard lich Tarul Var) conspired with Baroness Wynne Cromm to capture Morwen. While the real duchess was imprisoned in Cromm's Hold, Pencheska posed as Lady Morwen in Daggerford. The false "Lady Morwen" brokered an agreement with the Zhentarim for the town's "safety."

As of 1489 DR in canon, it was unknown whether the individual ruling Daggerford was the real Morwen or still the succubus in disguise.

**Campaign Relevance:** The Zhentarim presence in Daggerford and Kane's intel about "succubi allies" may connect to this storyline. [[Margeet Patel]]'s ties to the Red Wizards of Thay are also notable given Pencheska's master was a Thayan lich.

### Daggerford
The town is named for Tyndal, a boy who killed six lizardfolk with only a dagger while finding a ford across the Delimbiyr (Shining River) some 400 years ago. The dukes of Daggerford claim descent from Tyndal; their arms display a bloody silver dagger on a deep blue field.

Daggerford sits on the Trade Way between Waterdeep and Baldur's Gate, serving as a vital mercantile hub where river goods transfer to caravans.

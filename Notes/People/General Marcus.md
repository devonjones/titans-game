# General Marcus

**Role:** Commander of the Wolf [[Zhentarim]] cavalry
**Status:** **Dead** -- killed in [[Session 08 - Running the Gauntlet]]
**Faction:** Wolf [[Zhentarim]] (under [[Samus]] / ultimately [[Ahmendril]])

## Overview

A Zhent military officer commanding the **Wolf** cavalry that intercepted the party on the road to [[Waterdeep]] while they escorted [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|the Duchess]]. **Killed in the cavalry engagement in [[Session 08 - Running the Gauntlet|Session 8]]** -- he was the "captain" the party looted for orders at the end of the fight.

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 08 - Running the Gauntlet]]
Commanded the **50 cavalry** that tried to flank the party during the escape. The party formed a wedge and broke through; **[[Ono]] soloed the high-level paladin**; the rest of the line broke when the lieutenant called "fall back" and the party killed roughly 10 more during pursuit. **Marcus's body was looted for orders** at the end of the fight (alongside the lieutenant's).

### [[Session 09 - The Wolf and the Phoenix]]
**Identified by name** in [[General Martavis|Martavis]]'s debrief. Marcus was the Wolf army commander -- the captain the party had killed and looted last session. Part of the corrected hierarchy: **[[Ahmendril]] → [[Samus]] → [[Viggio Martel|Viggio]] / General Marcus.** Marcus was a peer of Viggio in the operational chain (Viggio = council manipulation, Marcus = military command).

## Connections
- [[Samus]] -- Direct superior; Wolf faction
- [[Ahmendril]] / [[Zanithar]] -- Ultimate command authority
- [[Viggio Martel]] -- Peer in the enemy hierarchy
- [[General Martavis]] -- Phoenix faction commander (Harper); Marcus was his opposite number in the Wolf faction
- [[The Zhentarim Occupation]] -- Operational plot

## Notes
- The party never met him in life -- only killed him in combat as the "captain" they were fighting
- Loot from his body included orders that the party still has (revisit if relevant)
- His death broke the Wolf cavalry; combined with Martavis's reveal, the Wolf army was decisively scattered at the ford in [[Session 09 - The Wolf and the Phoenix|Session 9]]

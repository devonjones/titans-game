# The Death Tyrant (Ahmendril)

**Role:** Unknown — apparent architect of the Dead Three ritual
**Status:** Enemy
**True Name:** Ahmendril (identified by [[Bathere]] in Session 6)

![[beholder_face.webp]]

## Overview
A figure of tremendous power encountered in the [[The Dead Three Temple|Dead Three temple]] beneath [[Viggio Martel]]'s manor. Humanoid body with the head of a death tyrant (undead beholder) — a single massive red eye, dreadlock-like eyestalks. His presence caused all magic to vanish from the area; when he left, magic returned.

Calm, deliberate, and entirely unbothered by the party's arrival. Completed his ritual before they reached him, took what he needed, and left — pausing only long enough to give orders.

## Appearance
- Humanoid body, **death tyrant head** — single glowing red eye, eyestalk dreadlocks
- Dark scale armor, blue cloak
- **Two gemstone pendants**: one blue, one red
- Katana-style blade
- Surrounded by undead figures

## Abilities
- **Antimagic field** — all magic in the area ceased while he was present; restored upon his departure (whether innate or from a carried item is unknown)
- Commands undead (multiple skeletal figures present in the temple)
- Apparent mastery of ritual magic — was completing a ritual on the altar

## What He Said
*"Ah, good timing."* — upon seeing the party arrive
*"Bring the bodies to Dragonspear."* — over his shoulder as he departed

The phrase "good timing" is ambiguous: does he mean the party's arrival was convenient for him (they witnessed something he wanted witnessed)? Or was it simply a dismissive remark?

## Actions Observed

- Acknowledged the party, gave orders to followers to bring the bodies to [[Dragonspear]], and departed without engaging
- Antimagic effect was present throughout his stay; ended when he left

# Ahmendril, A Zanithar

**Role:** Architect of the dragon-revival ritual; current bearer of the "Zanithar" mantle
**Status:** Enemy (recurring -- killing him does not end the threat)
**Known Aliases:** Ahmendril, "the Death Tyrant," [[Zanithar]]

![[beholder_face.webp]]

> ***"There have been many Zanithars. He is periodically killed and replaced."*** -- [[Laeral Silverhand]], Session 10

## What He Is

[[Ahmendril]] is the current bearer of the **[[Zanithar]]** mantle -- a recurring figure that gets killed and replaced. The death-tyrant head appears to be the signature presentation of a Zanithar, not a personal injury or transformation. Whatever the role is -- title, possession, lich-cycle, oath -- killing Ahmendril simply hands the mantle to the next one.

This dramatically reframes the threat. Defeating "Ahmendril" is necessary but not sufficient. The role itself must be understood and broken.

## Appearance
- Humanoid body, **death tyrant head** -- single glowing red eye, eyestalk dreadlocks (Zanithar's signature)
- Dark scale armor, blue cloak
- **Two gemstone pendants**: one blue, one red
- Katana-style blade
- Surrounded by undead figures

## Abilities
- **Antimagic field** -- all magic ceased while present; restored on departure
- Commands undead
- Apparent mastery of ritual magic

## The Real Plan (Session 10 Recontextualization)

Per [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s briefing in [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]]:

- Ahmendril is **bringing back two ancient dragons** -- this is what "raise the ancient ones" actually meant
- **Two discs** (black + red, "mixing into each other"), not one. We've seen the one from the [[The Dead Three Temple|Daggerford temple]]; the second is somewhere else
- The Abyssal vellum scroll from [[Session 05 - Signed S|Session 5]] is **dragon-control instructions** -- whoever holds it commands the revived dragons
- Connection to the **[[The Dragoncult Wars|Dragoncult Wars]]** -- historical precedent
- **[[Bathere]] did NOT know Ahmendril.** Their projects were parallel, not coordinated. Bathere's dracolich research may have been *used* by Ahmendril, but Bathere wasn't taking orders from him

### What This Means
- Ahmendril is NOT necessarily pursuing personal lichdom (that read came from Bathere's notes, which were Bathere's own arc)
- "The ancient ones" = two ancient dragons being resurrected, not Ahmendril's transformation
- "Rofidian" and "Ravica" from the scroll are likely the **names of the two dragons**, not cultists

## What He Said (Session 4)
*"Ah, good timing."* -- upon seeing the party arrive
*"Bring the bodies to Dragonspear."* -- over his shoulder as he departed

In hindsight: the bodies he ordered to Dragonspear were probably **disc fodder** -- more essence to drain. The Daggerford ritual was preparing one disc; bodies were being shipped on to feed another (or the same) drain.

## Confirmed Possessions

- **[[The Door of the Dead]]** -- Dead Three artifact, instant death on touch. Taken from Dragonspear
- **[[The Disk|One of the two discs]]** -- the one from the Daggerford temple
- Presumed access to the **second disc** (wherever it is)

The Abyssal **control scroll** is currently in [[Vaelin]]'s possession -- a high-priority artifact that takes away Ahmendril's leash on the dragons if the ritual completes.

[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] reframed the stakes again: **[[The Door of the Dead]] is a piece of a sealed Far Realm gateway built by the Dead Three.** Ahmendril holding it should be "further investigated" -- he's not just reviving dragons, he may be assembling pieces of a gateway to a place of old gods and raw chaos.

**Two dragons attacked [[Waterdeep]] the same night** -- possibly Rofidian and Ravica revived. The Tarrasque rose from the sea and killed one. If those were Ahmendril's dragons, his ritual succeeded faster than expected and he no longer has control of at least one of them (it's dead).

Ahmendril's known portfolio now includes:
1. **The Door of the Dead** (Far Realm gateway piece)
2. **The Disk** (at least one; both probably drained at this point)
3. **The Zanithar mantle**
4. **Possibly Rofidian and Ravica** (one dead via Tarrasque; one fled)

Status: still at large; presumed at or near [[Mount Hotenow]]. [[General Martavis|Martavis]]'s [[Troll Claw Hills]] mission targets [[Samus]] who reports to Ahmendril.

# Aura of Darkness

**Type:** Magical ability (not a spell)
**First Observed:** [[Session 04 - Good Timing]]

## What It Is

A **persistent zone of magical darkness** that surrounds the bearer and moves with them. Distinguished from the *Darkness* spell by:
- **Travels with the user** rather than being centered on a point or object
- Persists without concentration (or with concentration the party could not break by killing the caster)
- Affected an entire room and exited through a door alongside its bearer

Described in [[Session 04 - Good Timing|Session 4]] as **"a specific ability, not a spell."**

## Observed Use

### [[Session 04 - Good Timing]]
[[The Assassin]] used Aura of Darkness to attack [[Kane]] in [[Viggio Martel|Viggio]]'s sitting room. The darkness:
- Fell on the room instantly
- Concealed the Assassin (invisible already, but the darkness denied vision-based detection from other angles too)
- **Moved with the Assassin** -- "the darkness moved toward an exterior door and left"
- Did not dissipate when [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] hit the Assassin

## Mechanical Read

This is consistent with abilities like:
- **Devil's Sight + Darkness** combo (warlock pact)
- A homebrewed personal-aura ability
- An item / artifact effect
- A class feature from a non-standard build

It is **not** the *Darkness* spell (centered on a point, dispelled by concentration loss).

## Open Questions

1. Is this an innate ability of the user, an item, or a spell-like effect?
2. Who or what creature has this kind of Aura natively?
3. Can it be dispelled? Counterspelled?
4. Did the same operator return in the [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]] [[The Pencheska Conspiracy|Pencheska night attack]]? That attack used different signature ([[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] cast Detect Invisibility and saw raven masks instead) -- probably a different operator

## The Infernal Hypothesis (Session 14)

[[Samus]] -- the other confirmed user of a non-spell darkness effect -- turned out to have an **active devil contract** and **five succubi** working for him ([[The Succubi Alliance]], [[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]]).

**Devils and their servants come with darkness as standard equipment.** That is now the most economical explanation for the same signature turning up on two unrelated enemies in the same campaign: **not the same creature, but the same supplier.**

If that holds, it retroactively implicates [[Viggio Martel|Viggio]]'s manor -- where [[The Assassin]] deployed this in [[Session 04 - Good Timing|Session 4]], directly above a [[The Dead Three Temple|Dead Three temple]] -- as having had an infernal connection the party never identified.

**Still unresolved:** Samus is dead and his body is in a hell-pit, so the effect can't be examined. **[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] did eventually dispel Samus's darkness** in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] after multiple failures -- so whatever it is, it is not undispellable, just resistant.

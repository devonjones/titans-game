# The Disk

![[disk.png]]

**Type:** Unknown artifact
**Current Holder:** [[The Death Tyrant]]

## Description
A circular disk with a gold rim. The face is divided in a yin-yang pattern — one half black, one half red. Rested on stone in the [[The Dead Three Temple|Dead Three temple]] beneath [[Viggio Martel]]'s manor before [[The Death Tyrant]] picked it up and departed.

## What We Know
- Was present on the altar (or nearby) during the ritual in the basement temple
- [[The Death Tyrant]] retrieved it before leaving — it was clearly important enough to take
- He departed with it, ordering the bodies brought to [[Dragonspear]]

## Theories
- **Ritual product:** The disk was created or charged by the ritual performed on the altar
- **Ritual key:** The disk enabled or powered the ritual
- **Symbol:** Red and black yin-yang may represent a Dead Three pairing — Bhaal (blood/murder) and Myrkul (death/undeath), or Bane (tyranny) and Myrkul

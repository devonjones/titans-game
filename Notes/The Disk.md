# The Disk(s)

![[disk.png]]

**Type:** Ritual artifact -- vessel for sacrificial essence used to revive dragons
**Count:** **TWO** (revealed [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]])
**Current Status:** **DRAINED AND USED.** Both discs were completed; the two ancient dragons (Rofidian and Ravica) were resurrected and attacked [[Waterdeep]] in [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]]. The discs themselves are presumably with [[Ahmendril]] or expended

## Description (Disk 1)
A circular disk with a gold rim. The face is divided in a yin-yang pattern -- one half black, one half red.

## What We Know (Session 10 update from [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]])

- **There are TWO discs**, not one
- **Two colors**: black and red, **"mixing into each other"**
- Each disc is being **drained** (filled with essence) at a separate ritual site
- The two discs together are the **vessel system for reviving two ancient dragons**
- Connected to the **[[The Dragoncult Wars|Dragoncult Wars]]** -- this is a known ritual pattern, not a novel scheme
- The accompanying **Abyssal vellum scroll** is **dragon-control instructions** -- whoever holds it commands the revived dragons

## The Two Sites

### Disk 1 -- Daggerford / [[The Dead Three Temple]]
- Drained via the sacrifice of two refugees on the altar
- Recovered by [[Ahmendril]] in [[Session 04 - Good Timing|Session 4]]
- Color: presumably one of black/red (the disk's face mixes both -- the one we saw was visibly yin-yang)

### Disk 2 -- Location Unknown
- Existence confirmed by [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]
- Same ritual pattern; presumably also draining innocents
- The bodies [[Ahmendril]] ordered shipped to [[Dragonspear]] in [[Session 04 - Good Timing|Session 4]] may have been intended for this second drain (Dragonspear was a waypoint or transfer site)
- **Finding this disc and the second site is now a major plot priority**

## The Targets

Two **ancient dragons** are the targets of revival. Names in the scroll:
- **Rofidian**
- **Ravica**

Almost certainly the dragons' names, not separate cultists or operators (per Session 10 reread).

## The Scroll

The **Abyssal vellum scroll** ([[Session 05 - Signed S|Session 5]]) was originally read as ritual instructions. [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] confirmed: it actually contains **directions to CONTROL the dragons after they're revived.**

**Currently in [[Vaelin]]'s possession.** Top-priority artifact -- without the scroll, [[Ahmendril]] has no leash on what he resurrects. With the scroll in our hands, we hold the only known control mechanism.

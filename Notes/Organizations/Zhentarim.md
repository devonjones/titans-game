# Zhentarim (The Black Network)

**Type:** Mercenary/Criminal Organization
**Status:** Enemy

## Overview
An unscrupulous mercenary organization that cultivates power through smuggling, backroom deals, and mercenary work. Known for providing the "best guards money can buy" - if you don't ask too many questions about the cargo or cause.

## Relationships
- **[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]:** Adversarial. The Zhentarim have effectively occupied Daggerford under the guise of "security."
- **[[General Martavis]]:** Zhentarim general commanding forces in Daggerford.
- **[[Viggio Martel]]:** Likely allied; council corruption benefits the Zhentarim presence.
- **[[Kane]]:** Enemy. Kane provided Harper intelligence that prevented an earlier Zhentarim takeover of Daggerford.
- **[[Harpers]]:** Longstanding enemies. The Harpers actively oppose Zhentarim operations.
- **[[Lords' Alliance]]:** Enemies. The Lords' Alliance was founded specifically to oppose the Zhentarim.
- **[[Red Wizards of Thay]]:** Have worked together; both are pragmatic about alliances that serve their interests.

## Theory: The Destabilization Strategy

The Zhentarim may be deliberately fomenting chaos across the region to take control of Daggerford:

### Evidence
- **Organized monster tribes** invading the Sword Coast (monsters don't organize themselves)
- **Civil war in the Dalelands** (Zhentarim involvement confirmed)
- **Succubi being used** (demonic allies suggest coordination)
- **Banditry up, patrols absent** (creates insecurity)
- **Refugees flooding Daggerford** ([[Kane]] and [[Viseli Bar]] witnessed caravans of displaced people on the road)
- **Locals angry about migrants** (social division)
- **Zhentarim conveniently ready** with hundreds of well-equipped soldiers

### The Play
1. Create crises across the region (war, monsters, bandits)
2. Drive refugees toward Daggerford
3. Overwhelm local resources and create social tension
4. Offer "security" services when the town can't cope
5. Use corrupt council members ([[Viggio Martel]]) to legitimize presence
6. Undermine [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]'s authority
7. Eventually control the town through "protection"

The timing and coordination suggest this is deliberate strategy, not coincidence.

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Zhentarim).*

### Origins
The Black Network was founded almost a century ago by Manshoon, a dark wizard of Zhentil Keep. Through guile, murder, and intrigue, he created a cabal of wizards (the Black Cloaks), beholders, wealthy Moonsea merchants, and servants of evil temples - most notably the temple of Bane.

### Goals
The Zhentarim follow a simple creed: amass wealth, power, and influence to dominate Faerûn. They smuggle slaves, poisons, and contraband. They have spies everywhere. Their ambition is to control a trade route linking the Moonsea with the Sword Coast via Darkhold.

### Organization
- **Fang:** Novice
- **Wolf:** Experienced
- **Viper:** Veteran
- **Ardragon:** Leadership

*Note: The "Grey Wolf" unit under Commander [[Bennet]] may designate a specific operational division within the Wolf tier, or a sub-faction reporting through a different chain of command than [[General Martavis]].*

Members think of themselves as family and rely on the Network for resources and security, while maintaining enough autonomy to pursue personal interests.

### Public Face
Common folk know the Zhentarim as the people to hire when you need the best guards or mercenaries money can buy. Most don't know the full extent of their operations - which is exactly how the Zhentarim prefer it.

### Daggerford Connection
In canonical lore, the succubus Pencheska (posing as [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]) brokered an agreement with the Zhentarim for the town's "safety" - matching exactly what's happening in this campaign.

**Sources:** [Zhentarim](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Zhentarim), [Pencheska](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Pencheska)

# Lords' Alliance (Council of Lords)

**Type:** Political/Military Alliance
**Status:** Ally (Kane's secondary faction)

## Overview
A partnership of merchant cities from the Sword Coast, the North, and Western Heartlands. Members include Waterdeep, Silverymoon, Baldur's Gate, and Neverwinter. Founded to oppose the Zhentarim and other sinister factions through unified defense and economic cooperation.

## Relationships
- **[[Kane]]:** Affiliated through [[The Ashen Spears]], who serve the Lords' Alliance as an elite reconnaissance unit.
- **[[The Ashen Spears]]:** Elite unit serving the Alliance, based in Waterdeep.
- **[[Zhentarim]]:** Primary enemy. The Alliance was founded specifically to oppose them.
- **[[Harpers]]:** Allied. Kane serves as a "shared resource" between the two organizations.

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lords'_Alliance).*

### Origins
Founded in 1325 DR as a partnership among cities along the Sword Coast and in the North. The first leader was Lord Piergeiron of Waterdeep. Their goal was unified defense of northern cities and promotion of economic interests.

### Purpose
Formed specifically to oppose the growing influence of the Black Network (Zhentarim) and the Shadow Thieves of Amn.

### Beliefs
- If civilization is to survive, all must unite against dark forces
- Glory comes from protecting one's home and honoring its leaders
- The best defense is a strong offense

### Agents
Alliance agents include sophisticated bards, zealous paladins, talented mages, and grizzled warriors. They are chosen for loyalty and trained in observation, stealth, innuendo, and combat. They carry quality equipment (often disguised) and spellcasters tend to have communication scrolls.

### Hiring Adventurers
The Alliance has a history of hiring adventurers to raid Zhentarim strongholds and scout their activity. Local groups with an interest in combating evil quickly gain status and contacts through Alliance association - and make enemies of the Black Network.

### Notable Members
Connerad Brawnanvil, Dagult Neverember, Laeral Silverhand (Open Lord of Waterdeep), Ulder Ravengard.

# Order of the Fist

**Type:** Paladin order
**Iconography:** Blue fist emblem (on shields, breastplates)
**Head:** [[Unthar Froom]] (dwarf)
**Status:** Allied -- but [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] limits party-Order information flow
**First Referenced:** [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms]]

## What We Know

- A paladin order based in or operating out of [[Waterdeep]]
- **Hunts the [[The Dead Three|Dead Three]]** as a stated mission
- **Allied with the [[Lords' Alliance]]** -- an Order-of-the-Fist paladin administers the LA teleport-circle oath ceremony
- **Has access to or knowledge of the [[Lords' Alliance]] teleport-circle network** -- at least at the gatekeeper level
- **[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] knows them but limits the head's access** to top-secret Dead Three / Far Realm intelligence

## Members Encountered

### [[Unthar Froom]] (Head)
Heavily armored dwarf; encountered in [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]] organizing a Dead Three hunt in post-attack Waterdeep. **[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] previously excluded him** from the Far Realm briefing.

### The Oath Paladin (Unnamed)
A **human/humanoid in full plate, blue fist on breastplate**, summoned by Silverhand to administer the teleport-circle oath ceremony in [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]]. Cast **Zone of Truth** on the room. Witnessed the party take the oath. Worked alongside Silverhand's **Geas** binding.

## What the Order Does (Observed)

- **Anti-Dead Three operations** -- organizing in Waterdeep right after the dragon attack
- **Gatekeeping the Lords' Alliance teleport network** -- administers the oath required before anyone gains access
- **Zone of Truth** ritual capability -- standard paladin Order toolkit
- Presumably standard paladin order activities: protection, healing, militant religious work

## ⚠️ THEY BEAT AHMENDRIL A CENTURY AGO (Session 15)

[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s Legend Lore vision on [[Farraday]] in [[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]] showed a battle roughly **100 years ago**:

- [[Ahmendril]] leads **a group all wearing masks** up an invisible flight of stairs
- **They all see a god — possibly Helm**
- They fight. **A band of heroes joins the fray, and [[Ahmendril]]'s side loses**
- **A dark elf paladin — female, white hair, BLUE FIST ON HER SHIELD — stabs the ram's-head figure ([[Samus]]) in the chest**
- **[[Farraday]] dies in the same battle**

**The Order of the Fist has fought [[The Masks of Ahmendril|the masked cabal]] before, and won.**

Note the canon resonance: the Order of the Gauntlet is aligned with **Helm**, among others — and Helm may be the god the cabal confronted on those stairs.

## ⚠️ Re-evaluate The Exclusion

[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] **politely declined to share full intelligence with [[Unthar Froom]]** in [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]] **because [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] had excluded him** from the earlier briefing. She never explained why.

**[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] has since been found to be magically edited** — she doodles an owl mask and cannot recall what she lost. See [[Silverhand's Missing Memory]].

> **Isolating the party from the one faction with institutional memory of [[Ahmendril]] is exactly the shape of edit he would make.**
>
> **The Order may hold what the Open Lord no longer does.** This exclusion was made by a compromised mind and should be revisited.

## ✅ In The Field (Session 16)

**[[Unthar Froom]] was already mobilizing against [[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]]** when the party walked into the council -- and **brought five heavily armored veterans on half an hour's notice.**

**What the Order contributed:**
- **Six paladins** into a CR 20 devil fight
- **The tactical briefing** -- pit fiends are immune to fire and poison, resist spells
- **Stacked Aura of Protection: +10 to saves** for anyone within 10 feet of the paladins and [[Kane]]. **The single most important mechanical fact of the engagement**
- **Finished the Erinyes** [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] crit for 68

> **They did this for allies who had been withholding intelligence from them since [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]].** See [[Unthar Froom]]. **The exclusion should be considered closed.**

## Trust Posture

The Order is **on the right side**, but the party's posture toward it is:
- **Engage with the leadership formally** (e.g., the oath-administering paladin)
- **Don't volunteer top-tier intelligence to the head** ([[Unthar Froom]] specifically) until [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] clears it
- **Treat the Order as a network resource**, not a confidant -- they can help with operations, but the Dead Three / Gateway briefing is above their access level for now

# Emerald Enclave

**Type:** Druidic Organization
**Status:** Ally (Cael's faction)

## Overview
A widespread group of wilderness survivalists who preserve the natural order while rooting out unnatural threats. Decentralized, hardy, and reclusive. They work with civilized folk to promote the health of the natural world rather than opposing progress entirely.

## Relationships
- **Cael Stormcrow:** Member. The Enclave took him in after his transformation and gave him purpose. They use him as a living divination tool against elemental corruption.
- **[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]:** Allied. Enclave druids and Morwen's soldiers worked together during the Daggerford Cleansing.
- **Dara Thornmantle:** Enclave ranger who found Cael and brought him in.
- **Therin:** Enclave druid who trained Cael to channel rather than suppress his powers.

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Emerald_Enclave).*

### Origins
Founded in the Year of the Thoughtful Man (374 DR), originally based on the island of Ilighôn off the Vilhon Reach. They established themselves as a powerful force when they defeated Turmish forces sent against them by the wizard conclave known as the Windlass.

### Leadership
The Elder Circle (or Circle of Three) is the governing body - only three members at a time, consisting of the most powerful druids or religious leaders.

### Beliefs
- The natural order must be respected and preserved
- Forces that seek to upset the natural balance must be destroyed
- The wilderness can be harsh; not everyone can survive without assistance

### Religion
Most members venerate Eldath (Goddess of Singing Waters), Mielikki (Lady of the Forest), or Silvanus (the Treefather).

### Membership
Barbarians, druids, and rangers of good or neutral alignments are commonly drawn to the Enclave. Members learn "druidspeak," a unique language with similarities to thieves' cant, never taught to outsiders.

### Methods
The Enclave prefers to work with civilized folk rather than using violence. They've been known to allow woodcutters in forests that need culling - preservation through management, not isolation.

### Pop Culture
The Emerald Enclave appears in *Dungeons & Dragons: Honor Among Thieves* (2023), where Doric the tiefling druid is a member organizing resistance in Neverwinter.

# The *Moonrunner*

**Type:** Merchant Vessel
**Status:** Destroyed (5 years ago)
**Home Port:** Caer Callidyrr, Alaron, Moonshae Isles

## Overview
A solid merchant vessel that worked the trade run from Callidyrr to Baldur's Gate. Destroyed by an unnatural storm five years ago. Cael was the sole survivor. The eleven crew who died still haunt him.

## Relationships
- **[[Cael Stormcrow]]:** Navigation hand. Stationed in the crow's nest - watching for hazards, caring for navigation birds, reading weather patterns. Captain Brennan trusted his weather sense more than any instrument. The only survivor.

## The Final Voyage (5 years ago)

### The Passengers
The last run carried unusual passengers - a group of aarakocra and two winged elves (avariel). Among them was **[[Allaina]]**, a young priestess who spent time with Cael in the crow's nest during the crossing. They talked about navigation, weather-reading, and her people's mountain sanctuaries.

### Shore Leave
The *Moonrunner* dropped the passengers at a small island port for supplies. Kuo-toa attacked the port town. Cael saw Allaina cornered by two of them, read the angles, knew the outcome - and went in anyway. Nearly died for it (spear through the side), but others arrived and the Kuo-toa were overcome.

Allaina thanked him. She and her people left for the mountains. Cael went back to the ship with fresh stitches and thought that was the end of it.

### The Storm
Three days later, the unnatural storm found them.

It came from nowhere - wrong in every way. Wind spiraling in patterns, clouds roiling green-black, lightning striking in sequences. Captain Brennan shouted that someone had called this storm.

Cael was in the crow's nest reading weather when the world went white.

The bolt struck the mast. For an eternal moment, he felt everything - every drop of water in the ocean, every particle of air in the sky, the exact point where they met. **And that's where he was.**

He fell. Between the rigging and the waves, something opened inside him. He remembers drowning. He remembers lightning running through the water. He remembers that he couldn't die because he'd become part of something that doesn't die.

### After
Days later, Cael crawled onto a beach south of Daggerford. Wreckage of the *Moonrunner* scattered along the shore. Eleven dead. One survivor.

In the debris, he found a copper belaying pin - The Fulcrum. Over the following weeks, he found pieces of the mainmast (oak, struck by lightning) and fulgurite (glass formed where lightning hits sand) on the beach. He carved them, bound them with copper wire, integrated them into the pin. Everything about the transformation became one object.

## The Question
Cael has wondered since - was the *Moonrunner* targeted because of who they'd carried? Did someone want those passengers dead and settled for destroying the ship after they'd already left? He'll never know for certain. But the timing makes him think.

Eleven crew died. The passengers had already disembarked, safe in their mountains.

Sometimes he thinks about that.

## Legacy
- **The Fulcrum:** Cael's copper belaying pin focus, incorporating wood from the mainmast and fulgurite from the beach
- **The Name "Stormcrow":** Cael was in the crow's nest when the storm struck. Now he IS the storm.
- **The Haunting:** Eleven died when he lived. Sometimes he hears them in the thunder, sees them in the lightning's afterimage.

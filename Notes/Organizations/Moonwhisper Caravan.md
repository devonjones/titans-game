# Moonwhisper Caravan

**Type:** Traveling Community/Merchant Enterprise
**Status:** Ally (Kane's family)
**Current Location:** Near Daggerford, traveling the Trade Way

## Overview
A legendary traveling enterprise that's been rolling across Faerûn for over a century. More than merchants - it's a mobile community of herbalists, fortune tellers, performers, acrobats, tinkers, exotic animal handlers, and various "specialists." The wagons ARE home; the traveling folk ARE family.

## Key Members
- **Syndra Vyreth:** [[Kane]]'s mother. Master herbalist specializing in rare Underdark fungi. Pragmatic and warm.
- **Theren Vyreth:** [[Kane]]'s father. Alchemist and storyteller, brews medicinal tinctures and "recreational" elixirs. Jovial and optimistic.

The Vyreth family has been with the Moonwhisper for three generations, serving as the caravan's primary healers and alchemists.

## Relationships
- **[[Kane]]:** Born and raised in the caravan. The Moonwhisper is home. He sends his parents coin when he can but hasn't seen them in about three years. It hurts more than he admits.
- **[[Red Wizards of Thay]]:** 18 years ago, Red Wizards intercepted the caravan and conscripted 31 young men, including Kane. The caravan survived, minus its sons.

## Reputation
The Moonwhisper has a... reputation. Deserved or not, people whisper about "carnies," "gypsies," and "tinkers." Some claim they're all thieves and con artists. Others swear by their healing remedies and incredible shows.

The truth? Like most traveling folk, they know how to survive in a world that doesn't always welcome them. You make friends, you remember faces, you know which guard takes bribes and which merchant is fair. You know people in every town.

## The Network
Kane grew up knowing someone in every major city along the Sword Coast - not spies or informants, but fellow travelers, friendly barkeeps, street vendors who remembered the caravan, people who owed favors. It's not a network he built; it's just how caravan folk live.

This "I know a guy" culture proved invaluable when the [[Harpers]] recruited him. His caravan-folk contacts became crucial to Harper operations across the region.

# The Ashen Spears

**Type:** Elite Mercenary/Reconnaissance Unit
**Status:** Ally (Kane's brothers)
**Base:** Waterdeep

## Overview
Seven survivors who escaped Thayan slavery together with Kane. They rose from the ashes of their old lives, armed with the spears they carried when they escaped. Now serve the [[Lords' Alliance]] as an elite reconnaissance unit while keeping Kane's secrets.

## History

### Formation (18-12 years ago)
When Red Wizard necromancers conscripted 31 men from the [[Moonwhisper Caravan]], Kane and seven others survived the brutal years as war-slaves. They became known as "The Ashen Spears" among the conscripts - the unit that kept surviving when they shouldn't have.

### The Pact and Escape
After Kane made his pact with the Raven Queen and swore his oath of vengeance, he trained his brothers in secret. Their survival rate became "unusual." After a year of planning, they broke formation during a chaotic battle and fled across the border.

### Service to the Alliance
Once free, Kane contacted the [[Lords' Alliance]] with intelligence on Thayan operations. The Ashen Spears became an elite unit fighting for the revolution that overthrew the necromancer regime. After the war ended 15 years ago, they remained together as a Lords' Alliance reconnaissance unit.

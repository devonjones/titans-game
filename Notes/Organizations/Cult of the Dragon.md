# Cult of the Dragon

**Type:** Dragon-worshipping evil cult
**Status:** Active threat -- linked to the current ritual
**First Referenced:** [[Session 05 - Signed S]] (named in the Abyssal ritual scroll)

## Overview

A cult that venerates dragons and works to bring about a future where dragons rule the world. In canonical Forgotten Realms lore, the Cult historically worked to **transform chromatic dragons into dracoliches** -- believing prophesied dead dragons would dominate the world.

In this campaign:
- **Referenced in the Abyssal ritual scroll** from the [[The Dead Three Temple|Daggerford temple]] ([[Session 05 - Signed S|Session 5]])
- The two-disc ritual to revive **[[Rofidian]]** (red dragon) and **[[Ravica]]** (black dragon) is connected to the Cult of the Dragon
- Connected to the **[[The Dragoncult Wars|Dragoncult Wars]]** -- the historical conflict [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] referenced ([[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]])
- [[Ahmendril]] / [[Zanithar]] may be working on behalf of the Cult, OR using their methods, OR running parallel; the relationship is unclear

## Forgotten Realms Lore (Brief)

The **Cult of the Dragon** was founded by the lich Sammaster, who interpreted a prophecy of Maglubiyet to mean dead dragons would rule the world. The Cult's signature work is converting chromatic dragons into dracoliches via the **Ritual of Dragon Death**. In 5e canon, the Cult was largely defeated during the Tyranny of Dragons arc, but factions persist.

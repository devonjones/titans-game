# The Dead Three

**Type:** A trio of evil deities, jointly referenced
**Members:**
- **Bane** -- god of tyranny, fear, and ambition
- **Bhaal** -- god of murder and violent death
- **Myrkul** -- god of death and the undead

## Why They Matter

The Dead Three are not just a pantheon; they are a coordinated faction whose works keep recurring in the campaign:

- **[[The Dead Three Temple]]** beneath [[Viggio Martel]]'s manor in [[Daggerford]] -- desecrated former Lathander shrine, used for the disc-draining ritual
- **[[The Door of the Dead]]** -- revealed [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] as a fragment of a **sealed Dead Three Far Realm gateway**. The Dead Three built the gateway, got in over their heads, closed it, and scattered the pieces around the world
- **[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] wrote his doctoral thesis on the Dead Three** -- he is the party's subject-matter expert
- [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] briefed the party on Dead Three history in [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]], including the classified Far Realm angle

## The Far Realm Gateway (Session 11 Reveal)

The most important Dead Three story in the campaign:

> The Dead Three made a magical gateway to the **[[The Far Realm|Far Realm]]** -- a place of the old gods, raw chaos and power. They got in over their heads, **closed the door, and spread the pieces around** the world to keep it from being reassembled.

[[The Door of the Dead]] is one of those pieces. [[Ahmendril]] has it.

## Connections

- **[[The Dead Three Temple]]** -- Daggerford operation
- **[[The Door of the Dead]]** -- Far Realm gateway fragment
- **[[The Far Realm]]** -- What the gateway opens to
- **[[Ahmendril]]** / **[[Zanithar]]** -- Possibly the cult line that maintains the work
- **[[The Goat Head Mask]]** -- Possibly a Dead Three phylactery design
- **[[Bathere]]** -- Dead Three iconography in his temple but operating in parallel to Ahmendril
- **The Time of Troubles** -- Historical period when the gods walked the earth; relevant to Dead Three history (Bane and Myrkul died and were replaced during/after; Bhaal also died at Time of Troubles and resurrected later)

## Lord Celleborn's Knowledge

Celleborn knows the public history of the Dead Three exhaustively (doctoral thesis). The party should treat him as the in-character source on:
- Dead Three theology and symbology
- Dead Three artifacts in the field
- Time of Troubles aftermath
- Distinguishing Dead Three work from other dark powers

Silverhand's classified material covers what the public record doesn't: **the Far Realm gateway** and possibly more.

## Forgotten Realms Lore (Brief)

- **Bane** -- god of tyranny; died at Battle of the Gods, returned; Black Network ([[Zhentarim]]) is his
- **Bhaal** -- god of murder; died at Time of Troubles, returned; spawned the Bhaalspawn cycle
- **Myrkul** -- god of death; died at Time of Troubles, replaced by Cyric, eventually returned in some forms
- All three were originally **mortals** who ascended after the death of Jergal (the original death god)

# Chosen of Mystra

**Type:** A title / status; mortals granted divine power and tasks by Mystra (goddess of magic)
**Known Member in Campaign:** **[[Laeral Silverhand]]**

## What It Is

A **Chosen of Mystra** is a mortal selected by the goddess Mystra to be her direct agent in the world. Chosen are granted enhanced spellcasting ability, longevity, and specific divine tasks. The most famous Chosen include Elminster, the Seven Sisters (Storm, Alustriel, Laeral, etc.), and Khelben "Blackstaff" Arunsun.

In canon Forgotten Realms, **Mystra has died and returned** multiple times across editions. **In this campaign, Mystra is dead** (per the GM's Session 5 note: *"Mystra is dead in this campaign's timeline"*).

## The Mystra-Is-Dead Wrinkle

If Mystra is dead in the current timeline, what does it mean for [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] to still be "Chosen of Mystra"?

Possibilities:
1. **Her Chosen status preserves the original gifts** -- longevity, enhanced magic, divine sanction -- even after the goddess died
2. **Mystra's death was incomplete** -- she persists in a diminished form that still recognizes her Chosen
3. **The Weave responds to Chosen directly** without needing Mystra as an intermediary
4. **Silverhand operates on inertia** -- powerful enough to run on her own without divine refresh

Either way, Silverhand is **demonstrably still functioning** -- ruling [[Waterdeep]], doing wizard things at the highest tier, flying around during the [[Session 11 - When the World Burns|Session 11]] dragon attack, and known throughout the world as a Chosen.

## Why It Matters

- [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] is **at minimum** a top-tier wizard. Her Chosen status is layered on top
- The Chosen carry knowledge / mandates from Mystra that mortals don't have access to -- relevant to [[The Far Realm|Far Realm]] / [[The Dead Three|Dead Three]] cosmology
- The Time of Troubles (when the gods walked the earth) involved the original Chosen prominently -- shared institutional history

## Forgotten Realms Lore (Brief)

The **Seven Sisters** (Laeral, Storm, Alustriel, Dove, Qilué, Sylune, the Simbul) are all Chosen of Mystra and daughters of Mystra (in some accounts via her mortal incarnation). Elminster of Shadowdale and Khelben "Blackstaff" Arunsun are also famously Chosen. The Chosen are some of the most powerful spellcasters in Faerûn and act as Mystra's direct hands.

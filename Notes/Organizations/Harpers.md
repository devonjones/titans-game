# The Harpers (Those Who Harp)

**Type:** Secret Society
**Status:** Ally (Kane's primary faction)

## Overview
A semi-secret organization dedicated to preserving historical lore, maintaining the balance between nature and civilization, and defending the innocent from evil. They operate through deception, misdirection, and covert action rather than open conflict. Often called "the meddlers of Faerûn."

## Relationships
- **[[Kane]]:** Agent. The Harpers recruited him about 9 years ago to use his skills and caravan-folk connections for good. He runs information networks, smuggles refugees, and gathers intelligence on slaver operations.
- **[[Zhentarim]]:** Longstanding enemies. The Harpers actively oppose Black Network operations.
- **[[Red Wizards of Thay]]:** Enemies.
- **[[Lords' Alliance]]:** Allied. Kane serves as a "shared resource" between the two organizations.
- **[[Lady Morwen Daggerford]]:** Allied through Kane's work preventing a Zhentarim takeover.

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Harpers).*

### Origins
Created near the founding of Myth Drannor by elven elders, generals, and priests, led by an elven mage called "Lady Steel" and advised by a young Elminster. After Myth Drannor's fall, the organization was refounded by Elminster and other influential individuals.

### Beliefs
The Harpers believe in the power of the individual over sprawling kingdoms with oppressive laws. They seek balance between civilization and nature, societal order and personal liberty. They considered Myth Drannor the ideal expression of this balance.

### Organization
Deliberately informal to maintain secrecy. Some members take roles as spymasters, handlers, and agents, but there's no true hierarchy.

**Ranks:**
- **Watcher:** Starting rank
- **Harpshadow:** Experienced
- **Brightcandle:** Veteran
- **Wise Owl:** Senior
- **High Harper:** Leadership (elected by secret ballot)

### Methods
Harpers usually operate in secret, alone or in small groups. Many are elves, rangers, or bards. They work through deception and covert action, preferring to avoid open conflict.

### Enemies
Opposed by the Cult of the Dragon, Iron Throne, Red Wizards, Zhentarim, and the church of Bane.

### Sponsoring Deities
Deneir, Eldath, Lliira, Mielikki, Milil, Mystra, Oghma, Selûne, Silvanus, Tymora, and the entire Seldarine.

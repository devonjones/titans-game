# Red Wizards of Thay

**Type:** Magocracy/Evil Wizard Cabal
**Status:** Enemy

## Overview
The notorious spellcasters who rule the inhospitable country of Thay. Slavers, demonologists, and magical experimenters, the Red Wizards are feared throughout Faerûn. They constantly scheme for world domination through military conquest, political infiltration, and magical trade.

## Relationships
- **[[Kane]]:** Mortal enemies. Red Wizard necromancers conscripted Kane and 30 others from the Moonwhisper Caravan 18 years ago. He spent 6 years as a war-slave fighting for them before escaping with [[The Ashen Spears]].
- **[[Margeet Patel]]:** Has ties to the Red Wizards (nature unclear). Strangely, the Seneschal's dossier suggests she "might be good."
- **[[Harpers]]:** Enemies.
- **[[Zhentarim]]:** Pragmatic allies when interests align.
- **Pencheska:** The succubus who impersonated [[Lady Morwen Daggerford]] was originally summoned and enslaved by the Red Wizard lich Tarul Var.

## Session Appearances

### [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers]]
[[Margeet Patel]] is noted in the Seneschal's dossier as having Red Wizard ties. Given [[Kane]]'s history with Thayan necromancers and the Pencheska connection, this is significant.

## Forgotten Realms Lore

*Canon background from the [Forgotten Realms Wiki](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Red_Wizards_of_Thay).*

### Origins
In 922 DR, Thay asserted independence from Mulhorand through a rebellion led by Ythazz Buvaar, who became the first zulkir. The Thayans allied with the demon lord Eltab to overcome their enemies.

### Leadership
The eight **zulkirs** each specialize in one school of magic. Chief among them is **Szass Tam**, an undead archmage (lich). The zulkirs choose **tharchions** (regional governors) for day-to-day leadership while they scheme for world domination.

### Practices
- Slavery is fundamental to Thayan society
- Necromancy is practiced openly
- Demonic pacts are common
- Magical experimentation on living subjects

### Enemies
Constantly at war with:
- **Rashemen** - thwarted by the Witches of Rashemen
- **Aglarond** - turned back by the Simbul of the Seven Sisters
- **Mulhorand** - their country of origin

### Thayan Enclaves
After failed military conquests, the Red Wizards pivoted to economic infiltration. Thayan enclaves in foreign cities publicly sell magic items while quietly trafficking drugs, wicked spells, and slaves - building political influence for eventual domination.

### Szass Tam's Civil War
Szass Tam's rise as sole ruler sparked civil war with the other zulkirs. He used the Spellplague to devastate the land and end the conflict through catastrophe.

### Campaign Relevance
The Pencheska conspiracy involves a succubus originally enslaved by the Red Wizard lich **Tarul Var**. The connection between [[Margeet Patel]], the Red Wizards, and the current Daggerford crisis warrants investigation.

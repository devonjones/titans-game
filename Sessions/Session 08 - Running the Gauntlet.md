# Session 8: Running the Gauntlet
**Date:** 4/23/26

> *Guest star: Michael Haley playing [[Ono]], a friend of [[Kane]]'s from their military days. Also known as "Stormcrow" -- yes, there are two of them now.*

## Morning at Dragonspear

- [[Vaelin]] wakes. Has a message from [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|the Duchess]]: hurry up
- Tells the Duchess the party will arrive by end of day
- Wakes us. Our horses survived the night
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] **stays behind to check on the seal** (the [[The Avernus Portal|Avernus portal]] relic in the fountain)
- We head out North toward [[Daggerford]]

## The Road North

### Ono
- Horses get *Longstrider*. We ride hard
- Spot someone signaling us on the road. We approach
- It's **[[Ono]]** -- a friend of [[Kane]]'s from their military days together. Also goes by "Stormcrow"
- They served together (presumably in [[The Ashen Spears]] or the Thayan war)

### Blood on the Road
- About three-quarters of the way to [[Daggerford]], [[Kane]] spots recent blood on the road
- [[Ono]] reports: a body fell from a horse and was dragged away
- No time to investigate. We ride on

## Daggerford -- Lockdown

### The Gates
- [[Zhentarim]] of the **Phoenix** faction have locked the town down
- *"Halt. No entry into the city!"*
- The guard eyes us, chats with unknown parties behind him, then opens the gates
- We enter. The gates lock behind us

### The Castle
- Another contingent of Zhent Phoenix guards at the castle -- same experience
- We enter around 5 PM

### Briefing with the Duchess
- [[Captain Hidar]] greets us and leads us inside
- [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|The Duchess]] looks anxious but relieved
- *"I've lost control here."*
- **The council meeting went well, but when they spoke to [[General Martavis|Martavis]], he said the agreement is no longer in effect. Lockdown.**
- We raise the old plan: [[Lady Morwen's Escape|the escape to Waterdeep]]
- She agrees
- **The council is staying. Her seneschal is staying.** Only the Duchess leaves
- Reminder: [[Captain Hidar]] is the captain of her personal guard

## The Escape

### Through the Storm Drain
- We move to the NW corner of the castle yard
- The Duchess casts **Disguise Self** -- takes on [[Kane]]'s appearance
- She leads us to a culvert and opens it
- [[Ono]] covers our tracks
- We dismount and guide the horses through the storm drain
- We exit onto the bank of a river

### Crossing the River
- [[Vaelin]] asks the horses if they're willing to walk on water with us
- Casts **Mass Healing Word** on the horses (?)
- We get across the water and head East toward the road

### We're Being Followed
- Perception checks: horsemen are following us

## Viggio

### The Body on the Tree
- [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] sees someone leaning against a tree
- [[Vaelin]] looks closer -- the person is **pinned to the tree**, not leaning
- Discussion follows. We identify the body as **[[Viggio Martel|Viggio]]**
- **Viggio is dead.** Pinned to a tree. Someone got to him first

### The Plan
- Grab Viggio's corpse, stuff it in the **Bag of Holding**
- Run until we find a good ambush position for the mounted followers

### The Bird
- [[Ono]] spots a bird circling above -- probably a familiar
- More discussion. It's getting dark

## Camp

### The Tiny Hut
- [[Vaelin]] casts **Tiny Hut**
- [[Kane]] takes up overwatch position a short distance from camp

### Speak with Dead
- We try Speak with Dead on [[Viggio Martel|Viggio]]
- *"Why should I help you?"*
- Uncooperative even in death. Classic Viggio

### Night Watch
- Two outside the hut on watch
- Stormcrow casts **Fog Cloud** around the hut

## Dawn -- The Race to Waterdeep

### Breaking Camp
- Horses still here, unmolested
- Plan: if we see the bird, cast Fog Cloud
- Leave the Tiny Hut standing as misdirection
- We set out

### The Bird Returns
- We see the bird
- Stormcrow casts **Fog Cloud**. We enter it, then stop
- [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|The Duchess]] receives word: **the Zhentarim have left Daggerford. Overnight. Heading North.**
- Collective *"Oh shit."* They're ahead of us
- [[Vaelin]] casts some kind of flight spell, goes invisible, goes up, and **destroys the familiar** with a mace. The body disappears

### Riding Hard
- We ride on. No followers visible
- (Generally takes an hour to recast Find Familiar -- we have a window)
- *[Some trivia happened here. Devon got locked out of Google and missed it. Probably for the best.]*
- We're close to [[Waterdeep]]

## The Army

### The Blockade
- **An army of several hundred** on the road between us and [[Waterdeep]]
- Ships flying [[Zhentarim]] flags visible on the water
- [[Vaelin]] is up in the air scouting
- We kill another bird familiar
- Put up a Fog Cloud, then ride for the forest

### The Cavalry
- **50 cavalry** coming from the forest direction -- they're cutting us off
- Decision: we have to go through them
- **Kill them all. Form a wedge.**

## The Battle

### They Come to Us
- The cavalry double-moves to close distance

### The Fight
- **Stormcrow** opens with **Storm Sphere** ("a swirly damage thing")
- **[[Ono]]** engages first -- **takes out the high-level paladin**
- **[[Kane]]** smites a guy, kills him. Hits another
- **[[Vaelin]]** casts **Blade Barrier** -- a book appears (of course it does)
- **[[Viseli Bar|Viseli]]** rips a man's head off and throws the pieces at the next line

### The Rout
- [[Ono]] murks another, then relocates
- **Stormcrow casts Lightning Ball -- three dead**
- A lieutenant shouts *"Fall back!"*
- They flee. **Three more die to the Blade Barrier**
- We double-move to pursue. Kill the lieutenant. Kill another **10** before breaking off
- The survivors appear to be heading back to their army

### Loot
- We loot the **captain** and the **lieutenant** for orders

## Martavis

- [[Kane]] looks up -- **[[General Martavis]] is at the top of the hill behind us**
- They are stopped. **Aghast.**
- Kane recognizes him: **Martavis was a commander for the enemy side in [[Thay]]**
- This confirms the [[Martavis's Emblem|emblem connection]] from Session 2 -- Martavis's unit wasn't at the real fighting because they were command staff for the necromancers

## Key Developments

### Lady Morwen's Escape -- Executed
- The Duchess is out of [[Daggerford]]. The council and seneschal stayed behind
- She's traveling with the party toward [[Waterdeep]]
- The Zhent Phoenix faction locked down the town, then **left overnight heading North** -- presumably to set up the blockade we just fought through
- See: [[Lady Morwen's Escape]]

### Viggio is Dead
- Found pinned to a tree on the road outside [[Daggerford]]
- Someone killed him -- unclear who. The Zhents? An assassin? His own allies cleaning house?
- Uncooperative via Speak with Dead: *"Why should I help you?"*
- Body is in the Bag of Holding

### The Zhentarim Army
- Several hundred troops blocking the road to [[Waterdeep]]
- Ships with Zhent flags on the water -- naval blockade as well
- 50 cavalry sent to intercept us specifically
- The army we saw at the [[Troll Claw Hills]] (Session 6 intel) appears to have moved North
- **Martavis is present, commanding from the hilltop**

### Martavis Unmasked
- [[Kane]] now confirms: Martavis was **a commander on the enemy side** during the Thayan civil war
- His emblem (spotted in Session 2) belonged to a unit that was present in Thay but absent from the actual fighting
- They were likely **command and logistics for the necromancers** -- not frontline soldiers, but the people running the war
- He's aghast watching us carve through his cavalry -- he wasn't expecting real resistance

### Ono
- [[Ono]], played by guest star Michael Haley
- Friend of [[Kane]]'s from their military days
- Also goes by "Stormcrow" -- the party now has two
- Competent fighter: **soloed the high-level paladin** in the cavalry engagement

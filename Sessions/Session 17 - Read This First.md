# Read This First — Before Session 17

*Read aloud at the table. About a minute. Rename this file into the Session 17 write-up when the session is done.*

---

## We are standing in the middle of a fight. Here is how we got here.

We killed **Samus**. Stormcrow put a lightning bolt through a closed door and took his head off.

What we did not know is that Samus had **sold his soul-brothel to a devil** — and he was the collateral. The moment he died, the debt came due. His keep collapsed, the ground opened, and a **gate to Hell** tore open where it had stood. A **pit fiend** started climbing out of it.

Then we learned what this war is actually about. **Ahmendril is a mask maker.** He keeps a workshop with a wall of masks. He wears an owl mask himself. And the masks **bring his lieutenants back from the dead** — we watched Samus die on a battlefield a hundred years ago and then kneel before Ahmendril, masked and alive. The same thing happened to **Farraday**: an orphan, a street thief, a warlock who became a hero and killed a beholder Zanithar in the Waterdeep sewers. Ahmendril came to her house for tea, made her a raven mask, and she has been his ever since. She died once. It took eighty years, and she came back.

She is also the one who nearly killed Stormcrow in Celleborn's parents' house.

**And Silverhand has a hole in her memory.** Something charmed her into forgetting, and when Vaelin got her doodling, she drew an owl mask. She says it would take a *Wish* to fix.

---

## Last session we chose the gate over the sewers.

Vaelin worked out that **something physical is holding the gate open, and the pit fiend is carrying it.** Kill it, take the thing, close the gate.

So we did it properly. Kane checked Farraday's location against his own street sources — it holds up. He proposed bringing the paladins. **Unthar Froom was already mobilizing** and brought five armored veterans on half an hour's notice. Silverhand gave Vaelin a **scroll of Banishment** and loaned us a wizard, **Lady Estrell**. Vaelin cast **Hero's Feast** out of a magic bowl and handed out temporary hit points until Viseli was sitting at 208.

*(Celleborn was away — his father called him in to look at something out of a dragon hoard.)*

On the way we passed some tourists on the street who were magically disguised. Vaelin touched one and felt **scales**. They said they were visitors from Chult. We gave them directions and moved on. **Nobody checked who they were.** Chult is where Draymon is.

We left a note with Silverhand explaining everything we know, in case we don't come back.

Then she teleported us onto the field.

---

## Where we are right now

A pit fiend standing where the castle used to be. An Erinyes on each side of him. **So many devils.** And a **second pit fiend**, a few hundred yards back — exactly like the vision Kane had that morning.

The pit fiend's first words were not an attack. They were a question:

> ## *"Have you brought the masks? The masks I am owed?"*

We said: **what masks?**

He shouted: **"SEARCH THEM!"**

So the masks are what Samus owed a devil. *"Bring me their helms"* was never about trophies — he was trying to pay off a debt, and he wanted us to do the collecting. **Kane is carrying Samus's mask right now.**

Stormcrow opened with **Chain Lightning** — one group of devils down, another bloodied. **Viseli crit an Erinyes for 68** with the frost maul she took off Samus, and the paladins finished it. The pit fiend looked **surprised** and shouted **"fall back!"** Kane killed the other Erinyes for 57. Estrell wrapped the big one in tendrils of earth and he tore out of them. Stormcrow landed a **Static Cage** for 30.

Then everyone rolled a Dexterity save, **the magic went out of the air** — and **Ahmendril appeared.**

Kane shouted at the pit fiend: *why are you attacking us, when Ahmendril has all the masks?*

**Then the fireballs started falling.**

## Things to remember when we pick up

- **Stay within 10 feet of Kane and the paladins** — that is **+5 to every saving throw**
- **Stay within 30 feet of Vaelin**
- **Pit fiends are immune to fire and poison.** They are **not** immune to lightning
- They have **magic resistance** — advantage on saves against spells
- **Vaelin still has both Banishments.** His own, and the scroll
- **Hero's Feast** was giving us poison immunity, fear immunity, advantage on Wisdom saves, and +14 max HP. **Check whether it survived whatever Ahmendril did**
- **The thing holding the gate open is on the pit fiend.** That is the objective. Not killing everything
- Our own fallen devils **discorporate** rather than die — they re-form back home

## And the open question nobody has answered

**Why attack us at all, when Ahmendril is standing right there holding every mask the devil is owed?**

Kane has already asked it out loud. We find out tonight whether a pit fiend listens.

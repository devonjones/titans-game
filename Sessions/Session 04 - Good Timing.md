# Session 4: Good Timing
**Date:** 2/26/26

> *Note: The second half of this session was chaotic in play. Notes from the basement sequence onward should be treated as approximate.*

## The Sitting Room — The Attack

- [[Kane]] and [[Margeet Patel|Margeet]] continue in the sitting room; Kane investigates the wet footprints while keeping up appearances
- [[Viseli Bar]] stays nearby in the hall, close enough to catch Kane's high sign
- Kane confirms: **there's a secret door here**
- The room goes very dark
- Kane hears a whisper in his ear: *"You are not supposed to be here."*
- A blade goes into his back — **73 hp damage**. Kane goes down.

## The Fight in the Dark

- Viseli hears a scream. Enters the sitting room. Finds [[Margeet Patel|Margeet]], terrified. Propels her out of the room — Margeet runs. A thump from the hall.
- Viseli finds Kane's limp body. Medicine check: **stabilizes him.**
- **Viseli rages.**
- The invisible attacker tries to strike Viseli — rolls two 1s at advantage. Viseli's arm swings back and clips something. A voice: *"Fuck."*
- Darkness. Viseli swings — hits. Takes **8 necrotic damage.** Swings again — hits. Takes **7 necrotic damage.**
- Viseli activates her [[Cloak of Tranquility]] — **grows one size category**, dealing extra damage on all weapon attacks.
- Deals **18 damage** to the attacker.
- The darkness moves toward an exterior door — and leaves. It was an **[[Aura of Darkness]]**. The attacker escapes.
- **Total necrotic damage to Viseli: 33 (18+15).**

## "Murder!"

- Viseli picks up Kane and carries him to the ballroom. **Shouts "Murder!"**
- [[Maren Thane]] approaches: *"What happened?"* Viseli: *"An aura of darkness attacked my friend!"*
- Stormcrow casts *Cure Wounds* on Kane. **3 hp.** (Noted.)
- [[Viggio Martel]] walks back into the building.
- Storm goes to collect [[Margeet Patel|Margeet]]. **She's dead.** The thumping sound from the hall — Margeet didn't make it out.
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] goes to the murder room to investigate.

## Margeet's Resurrection

- [[Vaelin]] attempts to raise funds for a resurrection.
- Storm pulls a ring from Margeet's dead finger — hands it to [[Vaelin]].
- Vaelin casts *Resurrection* using the ring (presumably a diamond of sufficient value).
- Margeet lives. Multiple heals.
- Margeet: *"What happened?"* Kane: *"Assassin. They suck."*

## The Investigation — Party Authority

- Disagreement about who has jurisdiction to investigate.
- **The Ducal Guard wins** — we were the ones attacked.
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] instructs [[Viggio Martel|Viggio]] to unlock the hidden door with the wet footprints.
- [[Vaelin]] stays with [[Elaine Drago]]. [[Kane]] stays with [[Margeet Patel|Margeet]]. Both to keep them safe.

## The Secret Room

- Vaelin casts *Locate Object* — the ping passes through Viggio, standing in the doorway, and continues beyond.
- **Storm and Vaelin deceive Viggio**: they imply they're searching for the assassin's weapon, not the missing fingers. Viggio appears to believe them (or pretends to).
- [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] positions herself next to Viggio — ostensibly as a "guard."
- Storm, Celleborn, and Vaelin enter the secret room.
- Locate spell leads to **a small chest.**
- Kane casts *Heal* on himself. Thoroughly.
- Viggio disappears. **Viseli shouts: "Viggio is in danger! Somebody find him!"**

## The Chest

- Celleborn attempts to pick the chest's lock.
- Storm stops him. Casts *Shatter* — **area of effect, chest destroyed against the wall.**
- Inside the chest: **the missing fingers.** And other things (nature unclear).

## Viggio Formally Accused — Then Gone

- Storm confronts Viggio. **Viggio is gone.**
- Celleborn announces publicly: **Viggio Martel is wanted for the murder of Councilman Moore.**
- A party authority figure responds: *"Sure. If he reappears, we will arrest him."* (Lukewarm.)
- Someone rolls insight.
- **Kane demands Viggio show himself.**
- A secret door opens. Steps lead down.

## Notifying Hidar

- Vaelin contacts [[Captain Hidar]] **telepathically**: *"Viggio guilty, escaped. Send troops to Viggio's manor. We have Elaine and Margeet here, safe."*

## Descent

- Celleborn hears **chanting from the basement**. *"We have to get downstairs, now!"*
- Viseli roars. Heads for the stairs.
- The party descends. NPCs (Elaine, Margeet) at the rear.
- Kane casts *Light* on Viseli.
- At the bottom: Vaelin casts *Darkvision* (300-foot range) on the entire party.

## The Temple

- A **former temple of Lathander**, now desecrated.
- **Symbology of the Dead Three** covers the walls.
- Doors lead further in.
- Vaelin heals Viseli (13 hp).
- Bad guys appear.

## **The Antimagic — And the Death Tyrant**

- **All magic vanishes.** The entire party feels their spells and abilities go dark.
- A figure emerges: **humanoid body, death tyrant for a head.** Single massive red eye. Dreadlock-like eyestalks. Dark scale armor, blue cloak. Two gemstone pendants — blue and red. Katana-style blade.
- **Two slain bodies on the altar.**
- He picks up a **disk — red and black, like a yin-yang.**
- Sees the party arrive. Says: ***"Ah, good timing."***
- Turns to leave. Pauses. Looks at the party.
- Over his shoulder: ***"Bring the bodies to Dragonspear."***
- Exits. **Magic returns to the room.**

## Key Discoveries

### The Assassin
- Invisible; used **[[Aura of Darkness]]** (a specific ability, not a spell)
- Blade attack + necrotic damage
- *"You are not supposed to be here"* — implies prior knowledge of the party's presence
- Escaped through an exterior door; identity unknown
- Strong enough to drop Kane in one hit

### The Fingers
- **Recovered.** Confirmed physical possession — satisfies [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|Lady Morwen]]'s demand for proof.
- Were stored in a locked chest in the secret room behind the hidden door
- Chest also contained **other things** (unidentified)
- Their purpose remains unclear — connected to the Dead Three ritual below?

### The Temple
- Former shrine to Lathander (god of dawn) beneath Viggio's manor
- Now bears Dead Three symbology — a deliberate desecration
- Two bodies were sacrificed on the altar
- The death tyrant was performing or completing a ritual here

### The Death Tyrant
- Calm, unhurried, not threatened by the party
- Completed whatever he came to do before the party arrived — *"good timing"*
- Took the red and black disk
- Directed followers to bring the altar bodies to **[[Dragonspear]]**
- The antimagic field came and went with him

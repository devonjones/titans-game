# Session 5: Signed S
**Date:** 3/12/26

> *Note: Combat details are approximate — the session was fast and chaotic. Damage numbers recorded where caught.*

## The Temple Fight

### Opening
- Kane: *"It's time to kill."*
- Party gathered at one end of the room. Bad guys clustered around the altar at the other end.
- One of the cultists begins casting — **magical darkness** fills the center of the room.

### The Battle

**Vaelin** runs forward. Activates **Twilight Sanctuary** (all allies within range gain 10 temp HP per round). Casts **Daylight** — the darkness dispels, except around the altar itself, which remains shrouded.

**Viseli** charges in, raging. Gains 14 temp HP from rage, then another 14 temp HP from Twilight Sanctuary at end of turn. Hit twice — **15 force damage.** (Barbarian Rage does NOT reduce force damage.)

A figure moves briefly through the darkness — glimpsed, then gone.

**Viseli** stabbed — **20 damage, halved to 10 by Rage.** Hit again — **21 damage, halved to ~10 by Rage.**

**Kane** moves up next to Viseli. Another figure moves through the darkness.

**Storm** casts a **5th-level lightning ball** — 48 damage, max — against the altar group. Gets hit by a **critical strike** — 22 hp (partially absorbed by temp HP from Twilight Sanctuary).

### Shadow of Moil
One of the enemies is using **Shadow of Moil** — a small ball of darkness surrounding the caster. Heavily obscured, cannot be targeted directly.

**Celleborn** casts **Fireball** against the group near Viseli. Damage to enemies unclear (chaotic note-taking).

**Kane's** turn — deals damage. Sentinel feat triggers: *"Does Sentinel prevent him from moving?" Yes.*

**Vaelin** — **Spirit Guardians** up. Moves into the group. Enemy must make a Wisdom save — **10 damage.** Hit by Eldritch Blast — no damage (shielded or missed). Spiritual Weapon (flavored as a floating book) clubs a bad guy.

An enemy **whiffs against Viseli and drops his weapon.** Another **disengages and moves behind the party** — no attack of opportunity due to Disengage.

**Viseli** stabbed again — **22 damage (11 after Rage).**

**Storm** — **5th-level Lightning Bolt** through the Viseli group. **Two bad guys go down.**

**Celleborn** — gestures. Nothing happens. (Spell fizzled or was countered?)

**Vaelin** — dodges (bonus action), gaining disadvantage on incoming attacks. Clubs another bad guy with the spiritual weapon book.

**Kane** switches his **Hex** to another target. (Hex was active — not previously noted.)

### The Cleanup
A cultist makes a run for it. **Viseli** ignores him — jumps over Kane to attack a closer enemy. **12 damage.** Enemy whiffs back.

**Kane** chases the runner. Hits once. **SMITES.** The runner drops.

**Storm** — another **5th-level lightning ball** against an invisible enemy, gambling on location. One target ignores it entirely. **The other one dies** — his body appears as he hits the floor, invisibility broken by death.

**Celleborn** — Firebolt on the last standing enemy. **19 damage.**

**Vaelin** — finishes the last one. **Kill.**

**We won.**

## Investigation

### The Altar
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] heads toward the altar where two bodies lay
- Some sort of ritual magic was used — the bodies are **drained**
- Celleborn asks [[Vaelin]] to examine and explain

### The Scroll
- Vaelin goes to collect [[Elaine Drago]] and [[Margeet Patel]], who were waiting outside the door
- **Notices a scrap of parchment on the ground.** Points it out. Picks it up.
- It's **vellum**, not parchment — higher quality
- Vaelin tries reading it. **Written in Abyssal.** Celleborn translates:

> **The Ritual of the Ancient Ones:**
> - Ritual sacrifice of **two innocents**
> - Place their essence on a **disk**
> - Take the disk to **Mount Hotenow** (volcanic mountain east of [[Neverwinter]])
> - Finish the ritual there to **raise the ancient ones**
> - Connected to the **Cult of the Dragon**
> - Names mentioned: **Rofidian** and **Ravica**

### The Critical Question
**Why are cultists of the Dead Three performing a ritual to benefit the Cult of the Dragon?**

The disk [[The Death Tyrant]] took in [[Session 04 - Good Timing|Session 4]] — the red and black yin-yang disk — is now confirmed as a ritual artifact carrying the essence of two sacrificed innocents. Its destination is Mount Hotenow, not [[Dragonspear]] as we previously understood. *(Or is Dragonspear a waypoint?)*

## Key Discoveries

### The Ritual Scroll
- Written in **Abyssal** on vellum
- Describes a ritual to **raise the ancient ones**
- Requires: sacrifice of two innocents → essence placed on a disk → disk taken to **Mount Hotenow** → ritual completed there
- Connected to the **Cult of the Dragon**
- Names **Rofidian** and **Ravica** (identities unknown)
- This recontextualizes the [[The Death Tyrant]]'s operation — it's not just Dead Three worship; there's a Cult of the Dragon angle

### The Incriminating Notes
- Signed "**S**" — likely **Samus**, head of the Zhentarim in Daggerford
- Prove that [[Councilman Moore]]'s murder was **ordered** ("if the smith is a problem, deal with it")
- Moore was staged **before** the Zhent security contract was signed — premeditated
- Include the contract itself and Martavis's guest/operational documents
- **Physical proof** linking the Zhentarim leadership to the murders and the occupation

### The Victims
- The two sacrificed bodies on the altar **look like refugees**
- The council members don't recognize them
- Refugees being used as ritual sacrifices connects to [[The Refugee Crisis]] — are the displaced being harvested?

### Force Damage
- Multiple enemies dealt **force damage** — Rage does not reduce it
- This is a deliberate counter to Viseli's tankiness
- Suggests the cultists were prepared or equipped specifically

### Cult of the Dragon Connection
- Dead Three cultists performing a ritual for the Cult of the Dragon is unexpected
- Possible explanations: alliance, shared patron, one serving the other, or the "ancient ones" serve both factions' interests
- **Mount Hotenow** as destination — volcanic, east of Neverwinter, far north of Daggerford

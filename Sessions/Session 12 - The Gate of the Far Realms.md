## Unthar Froom and the Order of the Fist

- A heavily armored **dwarf and two others** come down the street
- **Symbol of the FIST on his shield**
- The party recognizes him: **[[Unthar Froom]]**
- He's giving orders: *"Organize the paladins. Start hunting the Dead Three now."*
- He asks the party what's going on
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] tells him **the keystone is missing**, stolen by mobs that teleported in

- Conversation continues; Unthar moves the party to **a more private location** (possibly a secondary crime scene) to talk

- BUT [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] **remembers**: *Unthar was one of the people asked to leave the room when we first met with [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]*
- That means Silverhand specifically excluded him from the briefing -- so Celleborn **politely declines to share more**
- The party DOES confirm: **the Dead Three are behind it**, and there's a **Cult of the Dragon** angle

(Per [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] later: *"We shouldn't be talking with random strangers about these things."*)

## The Black Dragon -- Ravica

- The dragon is **as big as a building**
- It has a **15-foot bite wound** in it (from the Tarrasque)
- The dragon's name is **[[Ravica]]**. Black.
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] sends a note to his father asking for help butchering and harvesting
- Celleborn evaluates which parts would be useful for armor and weapons
- **Celleborn's father arrives** with a **wizard, an alchemist/smith, and a merchant** to assist with the harvest

### Confirmed Cult of the Dragon
**These dragons were resurrected by the [[Cult of the Dragon]].**
- Two dragons. Black and red. **[[Rofidian]] is red, [[Ravica]] is black**
- This **matches the two-disc colors** (red + black) from [[The Disk|the disc set]]
- The connection between the discs, the Cult of the Dragon, the [[The Dragoncult Wars|Dragoncult Wars]], and Ahmendril's plan is now fully nailed down

### Gentle Repose
- **[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] casts Gentle Repose on the dragon corpse**
- Keeps it from rotting **and from being resurrected again** -- denies the Cult / Ahmendril a re-revival

### The Rest of the Day
- Party makes a day of it: **repairing things, helping people, harvesting Ravica**

## Silverhand's Briefing

### The State of Play

[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] joins. She asks the party to **find the other pieces of the door**.

**[[Viseli Bar|Viseli]]** asks if Silverhand can trace the teleportation source from the runes on the ground. **No.**

> ***"The bad guy already has two of the three pieces. The only part missing is the Key."*** -- [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]

**Ahmendril holds two pieces.** He has:
- **[[Notes/Items/The Door of the Dead|The Door of the Dead]]** (taken at Dragonspear)
- **[[Notes/Items/The Stolen Keystone|The Keystone]]** (just stolen from Waterdeep)

What's missing: **[[Notes/Items/The Key|The Key]]**.

### Is the Mask the Key?
- Discussion: could [[The Goat Head Mask|the Mask]] be the Key?
- Argument against: **Ahmendril failed to take the Mask** at Dragonspear -- if it were the Key, he would have. So probably not
- The Mask stays in the "Bathere's phylactery" category

### The Gods Connection
- Silverhand believes each of the three pieces is **connected to one of the Dead Three**
- **The Door is NOT Bane** (Tyranny). The Door is either **Bhaal** (Murder) or **Myrkul** (Death). (The party's earlier read: the Door's death-effect points to Myrkul)
- The combined artifact has a name -- believed to be **THE GATE OF THE FAR REALMS** (uncertain; working name)

## The Planetar

[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] casts **Divination** to locate the Key -- **4th-level**, **as a ritual**.

A **planetar appears**.

> ***"Such questions are forbidden."*** -- The Planetar

- Celleborn: *"Two of them have already been acquired by bad guys. They are trying to recreate the gate, and we need to stop them."*
- Planetar: *"It is important that such things should not be found. Go find the other pieces."*
- Celleborn: *"OK, where are they?"*
- Planetar: *"Seek the one who stole them."*
- Celleborn: *"Where?"*
- Planetar: ***"He moves around a lot."***

### The Implication
- **Find [[Ahmendril]]. He moves around a lot.**
- [[Vaelin]] casts a spell ([spiritual-insight type; specific spell uncertain -- Commune or similar])
- Conclusion: **the gods do NOT want the Key to be found.** A *planetar* appeared to a 4th-level Divination -- that's divine intervention, not normal channels
- The gods have a stake; they are actively opposing the Key being located

## The Oath -- Lords' Alliance Teleport Network

- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] asks [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] about **teleport circles**
- She **requires an oath** before allowing knowledge / use of them

### The Paladin and the Oath
A man in **full plate walks in. Blue fist emblazoned on his breast.** ("Order of the Fist" iconography -- consistent with [[Unthar Froom]]'s shield.)

> *"You called for me?"*
> *"Yes, I have some who need to take the oath."*

The oath:
1. **Not to use the portals except in emergencies**
2. **Not to traffic anyone with no business with the [[Lords' Alliance]]**
3. **Not to traffic enemies of the same**
4. **Not to reveal knowledge of the Lords' Alliance teleport circles to anyone who has not taken the oath**

The paladin casts **Zone of Truth**. The party takes the oath. **[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] takes drops of blood** from each. Then she casts **Geas** to bind the oath.

## Key Discoveries

### The Architecture: Door + Keystone + Key = THE GATE OF THE FAR REALMS
- The three-piece gateway model is **confirmed** by [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]
- The compound name (tentative): **The Gate of the Far Realms**
- Ahmendril has **two pieces** (Door, Keystone)
- **Only the Key remains uncollected**
- Each piece is connected to one of the Dead Three. **Door is Bhaal or Myrkul, NOT Bane.** Best guess: Door = Myrkul (death effect, "Door of the Dead" name)

### The Cult of the Dragon Connection Locked In
- **[[Rofidian]] (red) and [[Ravica]] (black) were resurrected by the Cult of the Dragon** -- not just a methodology reference; the Cult is an operating party
- The two-disc colors match the two-dragon colors
- The Cult is in the Ahmendril / Zanithar coalition. (Or vice versa.)

### The Planetar -- The Gods Are In Play
- A **divine being intervened** at a routine 4th-level ritual divination
- The gods do not want the Key found
- This is divine-tier opposition to the gateway being completed -- the Far Realm threat is significant enough to draw celestial attention
- It also means **the gods know who has the Key, and are actively withholding the location**

### Ahmendril's Eye Has Magic Suppression
- Discussion confirmed: **the death-tyrant eye carries a magic-suppression effect** consistent with what the party experienced in [[Session 04 - Good Timing|Session 4]] (all magic vanished while Ahmendril was present)
- This is now reframed as **a deliberate, controlled ability**, not an ambient artifact effect
- Tactical implication: **Ahmendril is in an antimagic zone of his own** when facing him

### Vaelin Holds the Control Scroll
- Confirmed in-fiction: [[Vaelin]] holds the Abyssal **dragon-control scroll**
- This is the **only known leash** on [[Rofidian]] (Ravica is dead)
- DON'T lose it. DON'T test it without intel

### Lords' Alliance Teleport Network -- Joined
- Party has **formally joined the LA teleport network under oath + Geas**
- The administrator was an **Order of the Fist paladin** (blue fist iconography)
- This is now a **strategic asset**: rapid travel anywhere there's a circle, contingent on emergency / non-enemy use
- The Geas means breaking the oath has real mechanical / divine consequences

### Order of the Fist -- Inside Information / Limited Trust
- [[Unthar Froom]] is its head, and **hunts the Dead Three** as his explicit mission
- But: **[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] excluded him** from the Session 10/11 briefings
- **The party should be cautious sharing with him until Silverhand says otherwise**
- The Order is allied (Lords' Alliance, oath-takers) but specific Order members may not be on Silverhand's full-trust list

### Ahmendril Moves Around a Lot
- The Planetar's only specific intelligence: **"He moves around a lot."**
- Don't expect to scry a fixed location
- Catching him requires either luck, intelligence from the Zhent network ([[Samus]] interrogation if alive), or going where he must be (the assembly site / [[Mount Hotenow]] / wherever the Gate needs to be activated)

## Fighting Blind

Nobody in the party has darkvision on the field of relevance. This becomes the entire fight.

### Vaelin
- Moves up, **feeling for where he thinks [[Kane]] was** before the darkness fell
- Hears the flapping of wings
- **Gets a hand on Kane's shoulder**

### Stormcrow
- Moves
- **Casts [[Lightning Ball]] above the doorframe** -- targeting a *point*, which works blind
- **It catches [[Kane]] too.** Some damage

> **This is exactly the right blind-fire call.** [[Lightning Ball]] targets "a point you choose within range" with no line-of-sight clause, and the lightning arcs around corners. Stormcrow's sight-dependent spells -- [[Static Cage]], [[Chromatic Orb]], [[Lightning Lure]], [[Counterspell]] -- were all dead the moment the ink hit.

### Celleborn -- The Dispel Attempts
**Celleborn is the party's only dispel option here** -- Stormcrow has no Dispel Magic in his kit.
- **Attempts to dispel the darkness -- casts on the effect, not on the spell**
- **Uses a Fae point -- no go**
- **Action Surge -- no go**

### The Enemy Fireball
- **[[Vaelin]] takes 10 damage, pushed back 10 feet** (more Repelling Blast)
- **Everyone rolls a DEX save. Fireball. 36 damage** -- except [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]], who takes half

### Viseli

- Then: someone **clumsily stumbles into her leg** and starts swinging

## Key Discoveries

### Ahmendril Has Three Named Lieutenants
For the first time the party has **names and locations** for [[Ahmendril]]'s command structure:
- **Farraday** -- raven's head mask, **[[Waterdeep]]**. **Already fought the party in [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]]** and escaped out a window. Samus recommends killing her first
- **Celine** -- usually in the **Astral Plane**
- **Draymon** -- **Chult**

This is the best actionable intel on the Ahmendril organization the party has ever had, and it came from Samus voluntarily.

### Samus Wants Ahmendril's Lieutenants Dead
The Zhentarim leader is **trying to hire the party to decapitate his own ally's command structure.** Samus and Ahmendril have a *"mutually beneficial arrangement"* -- and Samus is quietly moving against it. **[[Ahmendril]] holds Samus's chain** (per Kane's question, unrefuted). The Zhent-Death Tyrant alliance is not solid; it is a leash, and Samus resents it.

### Samus May Have Legendary Resistance
The *"chooses to succeed"* on [[Synaptic Static]] matches **Legendary Resistance** wording. If confirmed, he has a limited pool of auto-saves and the party should burn them deliberately before committing a real save-or-die.

### Samus's Darkness Is a Breath Weapon, Not a Spell
He **opened his mouth and produced a cloud of black ink.** [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s dispel attempts failed repeatedly -- he explicitly noted he was casting *on the effect, not on the spell*, which is the behavior of a **non-spell darkness effect**. This matches [[The Assassin]]'s **Aura of Darkness** from [[Session 04 - Good Timing|Session 4]].

Add the **garbled speech behind "this thing on your face"** and there's a real question: **what is Samus?**

### Fighting Blind Cost the Party the Engagement
The darkness neutralized: Counterspell, [[Static Cage]], [[Chromatic Orb]], [[Lightning Lure]], Heart of the Storm's rider, Wrath of the Storm, and every attack roll in the party. Meanwhile the enemy casters -- **who could evidently see or target fine** -- landed a Fireball, three Eldritch Blasts, and a mind control. **The enemy had a darkness answer and the party did not.**

### The Party Broke the Deal First
[[Kane]] shook on it. [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] opened fire on the handshake. **Samus's line -- *"Doesn't appear I can count on any honor in here"* -- is now on the record**, in front of Martavis, Martavis's men, and the whole Zhent camp. Whatever else happens, the party are now oathbreakers in this negotiation, and Kane's *"drop this darkness and we can have an honorable fight"* did not land well.

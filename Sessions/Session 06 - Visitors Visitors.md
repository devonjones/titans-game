# Session 6: Visitors Visitors
**Date:** 3/26/26

> *Note: Session ended mid-combat (Sierra left at 10 PM). Combat in Bathere's throne room is unresolved.*

## Morning at the Manor

- Party is fully healed after a long rest
- **The Duchess has left the building** — whereabouts not specified

### Harper Intel
- [[Viseli Bar]] is visited by a **wood elf** carrying a message from the [[Harpers]]
- Intel: The [[Zhentarim]] are **building a new headquarters in the Troll Claw Hills**, between [[Baldur's Gate]] and [[Daggerford]], and **gathering an army**
- Viseli reports this to the team over breakfast

### Planning
- Original plan: travel south to [[Dragonspear]] to interrupt the ritual (the disk, Mount Hotenow, "the ancient ones")
- The Zhent army HQ is above our pay grade — let the Duchess deal with it
- We discuss whether to brief the Duchess on the Harper intel before leaving
- We trade notes with her. Her response: **"Stay on target."**
- The butler has set us up with full saddlebags and provisions

## Dragonspear Castle

### Approach
- [[Vaelin]] gives everyone **Darkvision (300 feet)**
- Things in the mist, in the castle — movement? Figures behind arrow slits?
- Stormcrow **calms the wind**
- Buildings visible on the left
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] sends **Icarus** (owl familiar) to scout

### Icarus Reports
- Gatehouse, stables, carriage house
- **Creatures standing perfectly still** — undead, waiting

### Clearing the Approach
- Try to move stealthily, but we're on horseback
- Clear the stables first
- [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] looks into the stable: **an undead horse**, standing there, following its last orders
- A carriage visible nearby
- Open another building: **skeletons** present
- Tie horses at the rail around the fountain

### Inside the Gatehouse
- Up the stairs — **dried blood on the landing**
- **Magic Mouth** activates: *"Just leave it on the platform."*
- Celleborn checks for traps, examines the door — **magically locked**
- [[Vaelin]] finds a **pile of bodies** — mostly humans, dead a long time
- [[Kane]] creates an **illusion of a package** and leaves it on the platform where the Magic Mouth directed
- The pile of bodies is **undead** (reanimated or waiting to be)

### Upper Levels
- Barracks upstairs — **skeletons** present
- Another staircase up
- Kane **climbs the wall** of the gatehouse to the promenade
- Sees undead standing on the promenade
- Finds the **gate lever** inside the gatehouse — pulls it, gate opens
- Another **Magic Mouth** activates: *"Visitors, visitors."*
- Another pile of bodies in the corner

### The Throne Room
- Double doors — unlocked
- A **reception room with a throne** — no one sitting on it
- A figure stands next to it: **a girlish undead** — possibly a **revenant**

![[revenant.webp]]
- Kane: *"Where is your master?"*
- She replies: *"Who wants to know?"*
- Kane: *"I am Kane. We are your visitors."*
- Three exits: one left, one right, spiral stairs up at the back
- She says: *"I am your doom. The one you seek is up through those doors and up those stairs."*

### The Grand Staircase
- Kane and Viseli open the doors she indicated — **grand staircase leading up**
- A side door present. Celleborn rolls Arcana: 12 (insufficient)
- Another **revenant** on the stairs
- Kane asks if the master is above. It says yes.
- **Kane knocks.**
- The door opens.

## Bathere

![[bathere.webp]]

We are greeted by a **goat-headed figure**.

*"Who are you and what are you doing in my castle?"*

Kane: *"We were told to bring our bodies here."*

It smiles.

*"Why are you here?"*

We ask: *"Are you working with Viggio?"*

He laughs. *"No. Why would I work with Viggio?"*

*"I am **Bathere**. The one that you met is **Ahmendril**."*

*(Note: "The one you met" likely refers to [[The Death Tyrant]] from Session 4 — now identified as **Ahmendril**.)*

### The Fight Begins
- Bathere: *"Commence battle."*
- **Wings grow from his back**
- The room fills with **green acrid smoke**
- Initiatives rolled — the room is full of enemies

### Combat (In Progress — Session Ended Mid-Fight)

**Kane** holds the door open for ranged attacks. Stormcrow warns the party **not to enter the room**.

**Celleborn** casts **Sickening Radiance** in the center of the room — DC 20 Con save for everything inside. Glowing green fog fills the space. Most things in the room are likely dead or neutralized.

**Viseli** whiffs twice.

**Stormcrow** casts a **lightning ball** into the room. Reinforces the kill zone.

**Shadows** come through the walls and attack the party, then fade back into the walls.

**Footsteps from below** — reinforcements.

**A wraith** attacks [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]: **26 necrotic damage, permanent HP reduction.**

**Vaelin** — Spirit Guardians and Spiritual Weapon. 6 damage.

**Kane** attacks.

**Undead mob** reaches [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]]. Celleborn's spell does 26 damage to it. The mob hits Viseli three times: **27 slashing + 30 necrotic.** The revenant beside Viseli also attacks — misses initially, then hits for **16 necrotic.**

> *Session ended mid-combat. Sierra left at 10 PM. Celleborn was doing something when we stopped.*

## Key Discoveries

### Bathere
- **Goat-headed figure** — lord of [[Dragonspear]] Castle
- Wings grow from his back — fiendish or demonic?
- Has no use for [[Viggio Martel]] or the [[Zhentarim]]
- Commands revenants, undead, wraiths, shadows, and undead mobs
- Given the portal to Avernus beneath Dragonspear, his goat-headed appearance suggests a **fiend** or **devil**

### Ahmendril = The Death Tyrant
- Bathere identifies *"the one you met"* as **Ahmendril**
- This is almost certainly [[The Death Tyrant]] from Session 4 — the humanoid with the death tyrant head who took [[The Disk]] and ordered the bodies brought to Dragonspear
- **First time we have a name for him**

### Zhentarim Army
- Building a new HQ in the **Troll Claw Hills** (between Baldur's Gate and Daggerford)
- **Gathering an army** — escalation beyond the Daggerford occupation
- Intel came from the [[Harpers]] via a wood elf messenger to [[Viseli Bar]]

### Dragonspear Castle
- Infested with undead: skeletons, revenants, undead horses, piles of reanimated bodies, shadows, wraiths, undead mobs
- Magic Mouths suggest an operating supply chain (*"just leave it on the platform"*)
- The castle is actively occupied and defended, not abandoned
- Multiple revenants serve as sentinels — powerful undead with purpose

### Viseli's Harper Connection
- The Harper message was delivered **to Viseli specifically** — not to [[Kane]], who is the party's actual Harper agent
- This is the first indication that Viseli has her own [[Harpers]] connection

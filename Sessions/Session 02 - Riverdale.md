# Session 2: Riverdale
**Date:** 1/29/26

## The Plan
- Find the person murdering council members. Is it [[Viggio Martel]]?
- Help [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|Duchess Morwen]] evacuate from the city
- [[Viggio's Party|Viggio's party]] is tomorrow - we are invited

## Evening
- Some want the pub, others want sleep
- Return to the Duchess's manor to sleep
- Find tabards waiting for us - we are officially Ducal Guard, reporting to [[Captain Hidar]]

## Morning Briefing with the Duchess

### New Intel
- The town of Riverdale, east up the [[Delimbiyr River]], has been taken over by bandits
- Likely place to look for the missing tax revenue

### Our Report
- Moore believed [[Viggio Martel]] killed him
- Struck from behind, did not see his assailant
- Right index finger missing post-mortem
- Gravedigger confirms recent victims all had right index fingers removed

### The Other Victims
- A seamstress, a worker
- Nothing in common with [[Councilman Moore]]
- No obvious pattern connecting the victims

### Council Discussion
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] draws a persuasive argument that bandits stealing taxes are likely connected to the corruption in town
- Celleborn tells his manservant Malcom to go home if we fail to return

## Captain Hidar's Intel
- A villager escaped from Riverdale
- A few dozen bandits occupying the town

## The Road East

### Daggerford Observations
- Things look more orderly than expected
- [[Zhentarim]] are doing a good job of imposing order
- Soup kitchen operating - likely [[General Martavis]]'s doing
- More refugees on the road

### Travel
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] summons his owl companion Icarus to scout ahead

## Riverdale

### The Bridge
- Two guards on the bridge into town - bandits
- [[Kane]]: "Where are your friends?"
- They deny, then run away
- We suspect ambush in the ditches and under the bridge
- Charge the bridge; Stormcrow lightning bolts it (?)
- Nobody there

### The Tavern
- A couple bandits come out of the tavern
- [[Kane]] and [[Viseli Bar]] move to cover the back door
- Bandits retreat inside and close the door

### Celleborn's Offer
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] talks to the doorman
- The deal: tell us the truth, and you can run
- "Where is the tax money you stole and we won't kill you"
- He spills the beans: **Merron's** bandit camp, about a mile north
- Doorman runs away

### Kane & Viseli at the Back
- Bandits heading out the back door
- [[Kane]] steps in: "Freeze." Intimidation roll succeeds
- "How many more of you are in there? What are they doing?"
- Hogties the bandit, turns him around, leaves him
- [[Viseli Bar]] does the same with another

### Clearing the Tavern
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] and Stormcrow enter front; [[Kane]] and [[Viseli Bar]] come in back
- Initiative rolled
- Celleborn firebolts a bandit's weapon: *"The next shot goes through your eye."*
- Stormcrow prepares a ball of lightning, tells the other guy to drop. Throws chromatic orb - the bandit seizes and falls over. Tells the remaining one: *"Drop to the ground."*
- Asks the innkeeper: "Any other troublemakers around here?"
- About a half dozen more; the others are "off to the north"

### Interrogation
- Kane and Viseli carry their hogtied captives into the bar
- [[Vaelin]] to a bandit: *"Do you know what you had for your last meal, or would you like it to be a mystery?"*
- The bandit goes for "mystery"
- [[Vaelin]]: *"We're going to find your friends. If you would like to survive this day and simply do some jail time, I would like to know everything about your friends to the north, and perhaps we can ease the pain in your hand."*
- Bandit: *"What would you like to know?"*
- *"How many, and we heard something important was taken."*
- "Taxes."
- Cael: *"Rosey palm and her five sisters are a sailor's best friend. You almost lost that friend today. You have an opportunity - take it."*
- **Who is Merron working for?** No clear answer. Just himself, apparently.
- [[Vaelin]] heals the bandit's hand
- *"You are at a crossroads. You get to decide what to do next."*
- Bandit ([[Roderick|the talkative one]]): *"Being poor brought me here. War put us out of a job. Oh my god, you're Stormcrow."*

### Disposition of Prisoners
- Discussion about what to do with captured bandits
- Plan: tie them up in the bar
- [[Vaelin]] and [[Kane]] go to the smith's widow and her son (the smith was killed by the bandits)
- A family friend comes by; we give her a sword and ask her to "guard" the prisoners
- They agree to watch them

## Merron's Camp

### Arrival
- Ride north toward the bandit camp
- Immediately roll perception checks
- Hear horses and clashing swords
- **100 [[Zhentarim]] cavalry** are already here
- They swept up the stragglers - took no prisoners
- [[General Martavis]] is present
- People leaving a tent with a box - presumably the tax revenue

### The Tax Dispute
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] and [[General Martavis]] discuss
- Disagreement over who the rightful owner of the taxes is
- [[Kane]] suggests the contents of the chest may not be legitimate
- Agreement reached: we escort the gold on the road back to ensure it reaches the Duchess

## The Return

### Recovering the Prisoners
- Party splits: Stormcrow and [[Vaelin]] go to recover the bandits from the tavern
- Two of the three bandits are dead - justice for the smith's widow
- The [[Roderick|surviving one]] is the cooperative bandit we'd been interrogating
- Take him with us; he sasses us, we sass him back

### Kane Interviews the General
- [[Kane]]: "How did you receive the order to recover the taxes?"
- [[General Martavis]]: "Let's not waste time."
- Kane makes no progress pecking at the General

### Kane's Recognition (Perception Roll)
- [[Kane]] noticed an emblem on [[General Martavis]]'s jacket - same emblem on all his soldiers' jackets
- Kane remembers the [[Zhentarim]] being hired by the traditional [[Red Wizards of Thay]] during the Thayan civil war
- He remembers the Zhents showing up for the actual battle
- But the soldiers wearing *this specific emblem* - Kane doesn't recall them being there for the real fight
- See: [[Martavis's Emblem]]

### Back to Daggerford
- [[General Martavis]] delivers the chest to the Ducal Palace

## Observations & Questions

### Zhentarim Behavior
- Maintaining order effectively - soup kitchens, organized patrols
- 100 cavalry deployed to recover taxes - significant force
- Took no prisoners at the bandit camp
- Arrived at Merron's camp before us - how did they know?
- Disputed ownership of the tax chest
- [[General Martavis]] deflected questions about his orders

### The Bandit Connection
- Bandits are displaced people - war refugees themselves
- Merron appears to be operating independently (?)
- But who told the Zhentarim where the camp was?
- And how did they mobilize 100 cavalry so quickly?
- Two of our three prisoners died while we were away

### The Chain of Command
We believe [[General Martavis]]'s orders came from the [[Daggerford Council]] - the same people who hired the [[Zhentarim]] for security. The council (or at least the corrupt members like [[Viggio Martel]]) are directing Zhent operations.

### Open Questions
1. How did the [[Zhentarim]] know about Merron's camp?
2. Is [[General Martavis]] taking orders from [[Viggio Martel]] or the council directly?
3. Did the full tax amount actually make it into the chest?
4. Merron - really working for himself, or was he told what to say?
5. The bandits are war refugees - connected to [[The Refugee Crisis]]?
6. Were the bandits set up to steal the taxes so the Zhents could "recover" them?
7. What does [[Martavis's Emblem]] signify? Why weren't these soldiers at the real fighting in [[Thay]]?

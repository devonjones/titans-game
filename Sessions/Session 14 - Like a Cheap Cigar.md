## Like a Cheap Cigar

**[[Samus]] runs into the fortification and slams the door behind him.**

[[Kane]]: ***"Stormcrow, blow that door."***

**[[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] Lightning Bolts the door. 33 damage. The doors blow open.**

**Samus slams the door closed again.**

**Stormcrow shoots him through the door.**

The bolt **splinters the door to shards** and takes Samus's head off. It rolls further into the room, **smoking, like a cheap cigar.**

> **[[Samus]] IS DEAD.** Killed by [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]], through a closed door, with lightning.
>
> The thing [[Samus]] built the entire previous session around was **not being seen.** Darkness from his mouth, a face that garbles his own speech, a door slammed twice. None of it mattered. A storm does not require line of sight and it does not require the door to be open -- it goes **through**. [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Last session]] the darkness cost Stormcrow every sight-dependent spell he had. This session he never needed sight at all.
>
> The man who beat [[General Martavis|Martavis]] in single combat, who survived [[Synaptic Static]] on a nat 20 and a *"chose to succeed,"* who out-negotiated the party and then out-fought them blind, died hiding behind a piece of wood.

## Key Discoveries

### Samus Is Dead
Killed by [[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] -- **Lightning Bolt through a closed door, decapitation.** The head rolled into the room smoking. The head of the [[Zhentarim]] in [[Daggerford]], the "S" who ordered [[Councilman Moore]]'s murder, the brutal warrior even [[General Martavis|Martavis]] feared, is off the board after **fourteen sessions.**

### The Brothel Was an Infernal Soul Farm
Per [[General Martavis|Martavis]]: **[[Samus]] made a deal with a devil to secure a brothel to recruit souls.** The pavilion he deflected on in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] was the operation. **Five succubi** staffed it. One is dead (dematerialized), **four escaped**.

### Killing Samus Opened a Gate to Hell
The keep collapsed, the ground gave way, and an **orange glow** opened where it stood. A swarm of small winged fiends came up, and **[[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] detected a Pit Fiend rising, with friends.** [[Vaelin]] named it: *Dukes of Hell show up at times like this, looking for payment.*

**The devil contract was collateralized on Samus.** The party settled his debt for him. See [[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]].

**Strategic consequence:** there are now **two infernal breaches within a short ride of each other** on the Trade Way -- this new one in the [[Troll Claw Hills]], and the sealed [[The Avernus Portal|Avernus portal]] beneath [[Dragonspear]] that **the party is personally responsible for.** The party rode past the new gate on its way to the old one.

### Martavis Was Charmed -- and Something Else Is Going On With Him
He was **mind-controlled** and attacked [[Kane]] until [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] **kissed him** and broke it. In a succubus camp, that is a succubus charm.

But then [[Vaelin]] watched **his wounds visibly close** and concluded he has **regeneration.** Later the party noticed **he doesn't need any healing at all.** And his behavior in the back half of the session -- murdering a surrendering warlock, spitting on the succubus's spot, abandoning his own inheritance of the Zhent forces as *"devil worshippers,"* and then **assassinating every one of Samus's personal troops after being advised not to** -- is a long way from the man who ran soup kitchens under deep cover.

**Counter-argument, for fairness:** Martavis has killed surrendering enemies before ([[Session 09 - The Wolf and the Phoenix|Session 9]], *"no witnesses"*), he has just been beaten, held hostage, and magically violated, and regeneration could be a ring, a boon, or a Harper asset the party has never been told about.

**But four shapechangers escaped this camp**, and nobody has cast anything on him. See [[General Martavis]].

### Martavis Inherited the Zhentarim and Threw It Away
*"Samus is dead, I now command these forces! Stop fighting!"* -- **and they stopped.** The single objective the Phoenix arc has been building toward since [[Session 09 - The Wolf and the Phoenix|Session 9]], achieved in one sentence. He renounced it within the day and killed the men instead.

### Eldrick Is Martavis's Owl-Mage, and He's Free
The [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]] open question -- *captive or turned?* -- is **answered: captive.** He was among the disgruntled casters who surrendered, he was badly hurt, [[Kane]] stopped his bleeding, and by the road march he is **scouting ahead with his owl up.** See [[Eldrick]].

### The Succubi Alliance Is Real and Confirmed
[[Kane]]'s [[Session 01 - Dead Men's Fingers|Session 1]] intel -- *"the Zhentarim have succubi allies"* -- is confirmed with a body. **Hold Monster worked on her.** Detect Invisibility and See Invisibility both found her. **They are killable and they are catchable.**

# Session 3: Through the Wall
**Date:** 2/19/26

## Morning - The Tax Question
- Discussion: did [[General Martavis]] actually deliver the tax revenue, or only because we were watching?
- Storm's read: [[Viggio Martel]] will claim the taxes belong to "the people" — i.e., the [[Daggerford Council]]
- Kane makes a knowledge roll about the missing index fingers
- GM floats a theory: the fingers could be part of a spell. Maybe related to bounties.

*[OOC: Bane, Myrkal, and Ball are the big three gods in this world.]*

## Briefing with the Duchess

### Reporting on the General
- We inform [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|Duchess Morwen]] about our encounter with [[General Martavis]] on the road
- Report that we handed the General over to [[Captain Hidar]]
- [[Vaelin]] suggests the General knows how the taxes were taken and who the bandits were
- Kane asks the important question: who gave the General his orders?
- **Morwen's answer:** The council did. She was only informed after we had already left for Riverdale.

### New Orders
- Morwen sends us to [[Viggio Martel|Viggio]]'s party to spy
- Primary objective: learn more about the recent disappearances
- *"I can't help but think there's more to all of this. A [[Zhentarim]] plan."*
- She assumes Viggio has more information about the General's movements

## The Locate Object Hunt

### Planning
- We discuss using *Locate Object* to track down the missing index fingers
- Vaelin is a little grossed out, but he would be the one to cast it
- Vaelin rolls a knowledge check; GM says the fingers could be part of a spell — perhaps connected to bounties
- Morwen arranges horses for the trip into town

### Following the Ping
- Pick up a ping at the stables
- Follow it to **location #23 on the map** — a large manor house
- It's [[Viggio Martel|Viggio]]'s house
- Guarded by [[Zhentarim]] soldiers

### At Viggio's Gate
- We're too early for the party
- The guard is a "grey wolf" — a [[Zhentarim]] faction designation
- Grey wolf commander: **Bennet**
- [[Zhentarim]] members are marked with a **Phoenix tattoo**
- [[Viseli Bar]] challenges the guard to an arm-wrestling contest. He declines.

### Narrowing the Locate
- Recast with a more specific focus: the dwarf's finger (one victim was a dwarf)
- Gets a ping — confirmed: fingers are inside Viggio's house

## Reporting Back to Lady Morwen
- Return to the Duchess with the news
- She is visibly shaken. Sits down.
- **"BRING ME PROOF."**
- Physical possession of the fingers would constitute proof

> *The weather shifts. Stormcrow lets some thunder loose.*

## Viggio's Party

### Arrival
- We dress for the occasion and arrive at the manor
- Enter the foyer: coat racks, coat check
- *Celleborn smells of jasmine. Stormcrow smells of ozone.*
- Kane decloak to chat up the coat check staff

### The Ballroom
- Musicians. Stage. ~50 guests milling around
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] clocks a few council members in the crowd
- **Stormcrow radiates ominousness**
- [[Vaelin]] glances up the stairs: a couple of guards posted up there
- Three sets of doors off the foyer

### Locate Spell — Ground Floor Ping
- Vaelin's *Locate Object* pings on the **ground floor**
- The fingers are here. At the party.

### Working the Room

#### Viggio
- [[Viggio Martel|Viggio]] approaches Stormcrow directly; they chat
- We ID the door to the kitchen
- Viggio introduces us to **Padon**, a cobbletoner — Builder's Guild

#### Council Member Maren Thane
- Storm chats with [[Maren Thane]]
- Maren: *"Are you anticipating trouble?"*
- Storm: *"Soldiers solve certain problems. I'm a different kind of weapon."*
- Maren: *"What problems do you foresee?"*
- Storm: *"Clearly the town is suffering. The taxes were stolen."*
- We end up having to admit that Lady Morwen summoned us

#### Celleborn
- Introduces himself, says his family *"trades in luxury goods"*
- Looking for [[Margeet Patel]]
- Acquainted with **Lord Michah** — they chat

#### Stormcrow
- Looking for [[Elaine Drago]] — her motif is a gauntleted fist
- Asks Viggio where Elaine might be. *"She'll be here."*
- Tries to find a working-class person to chat with — no luck; it's an upper-class crowd

#### Kane & Margeet
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] wings for [[Kane]], approaching [[Margeet Patel]]
- Margeet appears to be an opinion-maker in the room
- Kane and Margeet exchange flirtatious pleasantries
- They slip away to the sitting room. *Without a chaperone.*

#### Vaelin & Elaine Drago
- [[Vaelin]] chats with [[Elaine Drago]], master of carpet and glasswork
- Drago: *"Oh, you're one of Morwen's friends from out of town."*
- Asks what he's doing here. Vaelin: *"I solve mysteries."*
- Drago asks if we're here to investigate the deaths — she positions herself so Viggio cannot see her ask this
- She does not like the direction the council is heading
- **Viggio has been openly manipulative of other members** — doesn't even try to hide it
- She does not like the mysterious disappearances

### Viggio Disappears
- Storm, Celleborn, and Vaelin all make Perception checks
- **Viggio is gone.** He slipped out — looked like he was deliberately trying not to be seen doing it.

### The Sitting Room
- Kane is trying to work toward a **locked door**, using his smooch session with [[Margeet Patel|Margeet]] as cover
- Doesn't get through
- **Kane notices dampness on the floor**
- Identifies wet footprints that seem to walk directly through a wall

> *Note: The raw session notes say "Kane is planning to break the locked door, with some advanced smooching with Drago." This is almost certainly a note-taking slip — Kane was in the sitting room with Margeet, not [[Elaine Drago]]. Recorded here as Margeet.*

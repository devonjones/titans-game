### Unthar's Plan

**He wants to round up paladins and go at the pit fiend.** He proposes meeting soon — **within the next half hour.** Maybe [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] will teleport them.

> **He was already planning this before the party walked in.** The Order of the Fist was mobilizing against the gate independently. The exclusion from Session 12 looks worse and worse.

### The Briefing

**Pit fiends are immune to fire and poison. They resist spells. The party has magic weapons.**

> **The tactical shape of the fight, stated up front:**
> - **Immune to fire and poison** — irrelevant to [[Cael Stormcrow|Cael]], who casts nothing but lightning and thunder. **This is the one fight where the standing GM trade costs him nothing**
> - **Magic resistance** (advantage on saves vs. spells) — this is the problem. It makes [[Sorcerer/Metamagic/Heightened Spell|Heightened Spell]] (3 SP, disadvantage on the first save) **the single best sorcery-point purchase available**, because it cancels the advantage outright
> - **Magic weapons** — [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]]'s new [[Frost Brand Maul]] and [[Kane]]'s kit bypass its resistances. **Devils resist cold... but Frost Brand also grants its wielder fire resistance, which is exactly right here**

**[[Vaelin]]: we have to meet before the fight, to eat the Hero's Feast.** 10 minutes to cast, 60 minutes to eat.

**[[Vaelin]] will attempt to banish it.**

**[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] gives [[Vaelin]] a scroll of Banishment.**

> **Two banishments.** Vaelin's own slot plus the scroll. **A pit fiend has magic resistance and a strong CHA save** — two attempts roughly doubles the chance one lands, and banishing the thing carrying the gate-anchor removes it from the field without having to kill it.

### The Reinforcements

**[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] presents the party with Lady Estrell. A wizard.**

> **Net party for this fight: 4 PCs + 6 Order of the Fist + 1 wizard = 11.** With [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] absent, **Estrell is the arcane replacement** — and Silverhand assigning her personally is worth noting. See [[Lady Estrell]].

### The Note

> **Good practice, bad custodian.** [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] has a **hole in her memory shaped like [[Ahmendril]]'s owl mask** ([[Silverhand's Missing Memory]]). The party just handed a written summary of everything they know to the one person confirmed to have been magically got at.
>
> **Not a criticism of the instinct** — it is the right instinct. But a copy should exist somewhere else. [[Lady Morwen Daggerford|Lady Morwen]], the [[Emerald Enclave]], [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s father, or the [[Order of the Fist]].

## The Feast

**Hero's Feast:**
- **Immune to poison and being frightened**
- **Wisdom saves at advantage**
- **Max HP increased by 14** (and current HP with it)

**They eat the feast.**

> **Precisely counter-tuned to a pit fiend.** Pit fiends have a **Fear Aura** and their attacks carry **poison**. Hero's Feast deletes both. This is the single best possible pre-buff for this specific enemy, and [[Vaelin]] called for it in advance.

**[[Vaelin]] gives the mage extra HP (39), and extra HP to others.**

**[[Viseli Bar|Viseli]] is at 208.**

### Formation Discipline

- **Stay within 30 feet of [[Vaelin]]**
- **Stay within 10 feet of [[Kane]]** (and any paladins) — for a **+10 bonus to saves**

> **+10 to saves is enormous** — multiple stacked paladin auras (Aura of Protection, +CHA to all saves, from Kane plus six Order paladins). Against a pit fiend's DC and a battlefield full of devil spellcasting, **the formation is the single most important mechanical fact of the fight.** Breaking formation to chase something is how this goes wrong.

## "The Masks I Am Owed"

**The pit fiend speaks:**

> ## ***"Have you brought the masks? The masks I am owed?"***

The party: *"What masks?"*

**He shouts: "SEARCH THEM!"**

**The two Erinyes fly forward — and combat.**
# ⚠️ THIS IS THE BIGGEST LINE OF THE CAMPAIGN SO FAR
>
> **The devil is owed the masks.**
>
> Everything reorganizes around this:
>
> **1. [[Samus]]'s devil contract was never about a brothel.** [[General Martavis|Martavis]] said Samus *"made a deal with a devil to secure a brothel to recruit souls"* ([[Session 14 - Like a Cheap Cigar|Session 14]]). That was the *consideration* — what Samus got. **The masks are what the devil was promised in return.**
>
> **2. [[Samus]]'s bargain with the party was him trying to pay his own debt.** *"Bring me their helms"* ([[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]]) — **not heads. Helms.** He wasn't collecting trophies and he wasn't just trying to kill his colleagues. **He was sourcing the masks he owed a devil**, using the party as the collection agent. The vault flagged the word choice as deliberate; this is why.
>
> **3. The chain had two ends.** [[Ahmendril]] held Samus's chain *because the mask resurrects him*. **The devil held the other end** — and the price was masks. Samus was leveraged from both directions and told the party about neither.
>
> **4. The party is carrying one.** [[Kane]] has **[[Samus's Helm|the ram mask]]**. The pit fiend asked if they'd brought the masks and then **ordered them searched** — he may not know which masks are where, but he knows mortals carrying masks when he sees them. **[[The Goat Head Mask]] is in storage in [[Waterdeep]]**, which is now a very good place for it not to be.
>
> **5. Devils and masks suggest where the masks come from.** [[Ahmendril]] *crafts* some — but [[The Goat Head Mask|the goat mask]] was **recovered**, not made: hidden in acid in a dragon's lair, older and tougher than his workshop ([[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]]). **If a devil has a claim on these objects, the masks may be infernal in origin** — which would also explain the non-spell darkness on [[Samus]] and [[The Assassin]], and why a Chosen of Mystra couldn't destroy one.
>
> **6. He expected a delivery.** He opened with a question, not an attack. **Someone was supposed to bring him masks, and he thought it might be these people.**
>
> See [[The Devil's Claim]].

**The pit fiend looks surprised and shouts "FALL BACK!"**

> **A CR 20 devil ordered a retreat in round one.** He expected a delivery and got a war party of eleven with stacked paladin auras and a Hero's Feast. **The party's preparation visibly broke his plan.**

**[[Kane]] attacks the other Erinyes: 57 damage. It dies.**

### Estrell Opens

**Lady Estrell tries something: tendrils of earth break from the ground and wrap around the pit fiend.**

**He breaks out of them.**

> *Likely **Maelstrom**, **Transmute Rock**, or a grapple-effect spell. His STR is monstrous — but it cost him a turn.*

**The other force of devils is moving up.**

### Static Cage

**[[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] casts [[Static Cage]]. The pit fiend takes 8d6 — and because of [[Elemental Adept]] he rolls no 1s. 30 damage.**

> **[[Elemental Adept]] (Lightning) earning its keep:** 1s count as 2s, and **lightning resistance is ignored entirely.** A pit fiend's immunities are fire and poison; **lightning goes straight through.** Cael is the one party member whose entire kit is unresisted here.

### Magic Leaves

**Everyone rolls DEX saves.**

**Magic leaves.**

**[[Ahmendril]] appears.**

> ## ⚠️ AHMENDRIL IS ON THE FIELD
>
> **First time the party has faced him since [[Session 04 - Good Timing|Session 4]].**
>
> And read the sequence: **DEX saves → "magic leaves" → he appears.** [[Ahmendril]]'s confirmed signature is **magic suppression via his death-tyrant eye** ([[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]]). An antimagic cone **shuts down [[Static Cage]], the Hero's Feast, concentration spells, and Estrell entirely** — and it would **not** touch [[Kane]], [[Viseli Bar|Viseli]], or the paladins' weapons.
>
> **Clarify at the table what "magic leaves" meant** — antimagic field, a mass dispel, or the Static Cage simply ending. It changes what everyone can do next turn.
>
> **He came for the masks.** That is now the only reading that fits: a devil is demanding masks he is owed, and the mask-maker turns up in person.

### Kane Plays the Only Card Available

**[[Kane]] shouts at the pit fiend: why attack us, when [[Ahmendril]] has all the masks?**

> **This is the right move and it may be the whole fight.**
>
> The devil is owed masks. **[[Ahmendril]] is the mask maker and holds the collection.** [[Kane]] is attempting to **redirect an infernal debt onto the actual debtor** — and he is telling the truth. [[Samus]] is dead; the man who owes this devil is standing right there.
>
> This is the same fracture [[Samus]] tried to sell the party in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]], from the other direction: **turn Ahmendril's creditors against him.** If it lands, a pit fiend and his legion change targets.

**Fireballs rain on the party. They take injuries.**

*(Session ends here — mid-combat.)*

## Key Discoveries

### The Devil Is Owed The Masks

> ***"Have you brought the masks? The masks I am owed?"***

**This recontextualizes three sessions at once.** [[Samus]]'s devil contract was paid *in masks*. His *"bring me their helms"* bargain was **him trying to settle that debt using the party as collectors.** He was leveraged by [[Ahmendril]] (who holds the resurrection) **and** by a devil (who holds a claim on the objects), and he disclosed neither.

See [[The Devil's Claim]].

### Something Physical Holds The Gate Open, And The Pit Fiend Has It
[[Vaelin]]'s read, corroborating [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s [[Session 15 - The Wall of Masks|Session 15]] line about the unidentified item. **Kill or banish the devil, take the object, close the gate.** That is the win condition for this fight.

### Ahmendril Came In Person
First appearance since [[Session 04 - Good Timing|Session 4]]. Arrived immediately after **"magic leaves"** — consistent with his death-tyrant antimagic. **The mask maker showed up to a fight about masks.**

### Two Pit Fiends
[[Kane]]'s vision of pit fiends laying waste to the countryside was literal. A second one stands a few hundred yards back. **This gate is producing CR 20 devils, and the party is level 14.**

### The Order Of The Fist Is A Real Ally
[[Unthar Froom]] was **already mobilizing** against the gate before the party walked in, and brought **five heavily armored veterans** on half an hour's notice. The [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]] decision to withhold intelligence from him — made on [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s now-compromised judgment — looks like a straightforward mistake.

### Kane's Sources Independently Confirm The Farraday Intel
Important precisely **because [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] cannot be fully trusted.** [[Kane]]'s street network agrees with her sewer location. **The lead is real.**

### Chultans Are In Waterdeep, Magically Disguised
[[Vaelin]] felt **scales** under a disguise. They claimed to be visitors from [[Chult]] avoiding offense. **[[Chult]] is [[Draymon]]'s posting.** Nobody cast anything on them and nobody got a name. See [[The Chultan Visitors]].

### Preparation Visibly Worked
Hero's Feast counters a pit fiend's two signature tools (fear aura, poison). Stacked paladin auras gave **+10 to saves.** Magic weapons bypass its resistances. **A CR 20 devil called for a retreat in round one** — the party out-prepared it.

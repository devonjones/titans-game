### Commune with Nature -- what it actually does

3 miles outdoors; 300 feet in natural caves; **does not function in dungeons or towns.** Three facts of your choice about: terrain and water, prevalent plants/minerals/animals/peoples, **powerful celestials, fey, fiends, elementals, or undead**, **influence from other planes of existence**, buildings.

> **This is a purpose-built tool for [[The Troll Claw Oblivion Gate]]** -- *powerful fiends* and *planar influence* across three miles, from a safe distance, outdoors.
>
> **It is useless for finding [[Farraday]].** [[Waterdeep]] is a town and the sewers are construction.

## Legend Lore: Farraday

Discussion follows. **[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] casts Legend Lore on [[Farraday]].**

### The Vision

A **striking, beautiful, raven-haired woman** in the streets of [[Waterdeep]]. She slips down an alley **into the sewer system.**

- She is **joined by her friends who dwell in the shadows**
- They move through **complex tunnels**
- They find a **meeting of underground criminals.** Her crew takes shelter in the shadows, **surrounds them, and kills them all.** The sense is that this was **a long time ago**
- They move on, **killing along the way**
- Until they reach **a Beholder. A [[Zanithar]].**

**And they kill him.**

- **Elementals rise in the room from her hand**
- **They come at him from all angles, so he doesn't know where to attack** -- textbook counter to a beholder's antimagic cone
- She is **casting**
- **They gank him**

She comes out of the sewers and goes into a home. **Standing there is another owl-faced figure -- probably [[Ahmendril]].**

**They have tea.** They talk for a while. **They come to an agreement.**

They are transported to **the same wizard's workshop.** A **different array of masks; new ones added.** The collection seems to focus on **animal masks and mystical masks.**

**He crafts a raven mask for her.** She nods and disappears into the city.

### The Battle

Later, he appears before her again. **They go up an invisible flight of stairs.**

**A group is there, all wearing masks.**

**They all see a god. It seems like it might be Helm.**

**They fight.** A group of heroes joins the fray, and **eventually [[Ahmendril]]'s side loses.**

- **A ram's-head figure is stabbed in the chest by a dark elf paladin -- female, white hair, BLUE FIST ON HER SHIELD**
- **[[Farraday]] dies**

**And then she is kneeling before [[Ahmendril]]. The era has changed between the fight and the kneeling.**

> **BLUE FIST = [[Order of the Fist]].** [[Unthar Froom]]'s order has been fighting this exact cabal for at least a century. And this is [[Samus]]'s death from the *first* vision, seen from the other side -- **Samus and Farraday were in the same masked group at the same battle.**

### The Dragon's Lair

They talk again. She nods. Eventually she is **outside the city.**

**She and her crew are scaling a mountain.** They hang out in the area. **They see a dragon leave.**

**Farraday's group enters the dragon's lair.** She finds **another mask settled in a pool of acid.** It was **well-hidden.**

**They bring the dragon's lair mask back to [[Ahmendril]].**

> ## IT WAS [[The Goat Head Mask|THE GOAT MASK]].

### And Then Home

**She goes back into the sewers of [[Waterdeep]] and sets up a home there. Keeps a low profile.**

She talks to [[Ahmendril]].

**She descends on [[Ellenfear Manor|Celleborn's parents' house]].**

**The party sees themselves there, under attack.**

**She flees.** End of vision.

> **THE [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|SESSION 10]] IDENTIFICATION IS CONFIRMED.** Not circumstantial any more. [[Farraday]] is the raven-masked attacker who nearly killed [[Cael Stormcrow|Cael]] -- two failed death saves -- and went out the window. **[[Ahmendril]] briefed her personally immediately before the hit.**

## Key Discoveries

### The Masks Are How Ahmendril Brings People Back
Two visions, two lieutenants, two eras, **the same three beats**: die → time passes → **kneel before [[Ahmendril]], wearing the mask.**

This explains the thing nobody could crack: **[[The Goat Head Mask]] is still attuned to a dead [[Bathere]]**, [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] called it *"might be a phylactery"*, and **she could not destroy it.**

**The party is holding two of these.** See [[The Masks of Ahmendril]].

### Ahmendril Is -- Or Was -- A Humanoid Wizard With A Sword
Elemental aura, drawn blade, a thrown 6th-level evocation. **A gish, not a monster.** The death-tyrant beholder presentation is a later form or a different body wearing the same [[Zanithar]] mantle.

He wears an **owl mask**. **There was a second owl mask on the wall.**

**His pendant** -- the vault's primary candidate for [[Notes/Items/The Key|the Key]] -- was around his neck in a vision from over a century ago.

### He Recruits The People Who Beat Him
[[Samus]]: defeated in single combat, **surrender accepted**, negotiation, mask. [[Farraday]]: **killed a [[Zanithar]]**, then tea at her house, an agreement, a mask.

**He doesn't command loyalty. He buys it with resurrection.** These are contractors on a very long contract -- which is exactly why [[Samus]] was trying to break his.

### He Does Not Make All The Masks
**[[Farraday]] recovered the goat mask from a dragon's lair**, hidden in a pool of acid, well-concealed, and brought it to him.

So **the goat mask is older than his workshop.** That explains its non-detection, its resistance to investigation, and why a Chosen of Mystra couldn't destroy it -- while the ram mask surrendered a full vision on the first casting.

**And it means there is a collection program.** Someone hid that mask deliberately. There are likely more, and **[[Celine]]'s Astral Plane posting and [[Draymon]]'s [[Chult]] posting start looking like search assignments rather than territory.**

**Acid pool means black dragon.** The campaign has one: **[[Ravica]]**.

### Farraday's Whole Life
Orphaned by a battle at ~11. Street urchin. Cornered, called out for power, and **something in the shadows answered** -- purple energy, a warlock pact. Rose, joined adventurers, and went down into the [[Waterdeep]] sewers with a shadow crew to **kill a beholder [[Zanithar]] and clean up her city.**

Then [[Ahmendril]] came to her house for tea.

**She died ~100 years ago and came back ~80 years later.** She has been back about twenty years, living in the sewers, keeping a low profile.

### The Order of the Fist Has Fought This Before
A **female drow paladin, white hair, blue fist on her shield**, killed the ram-masked figure a century ago. That is [[Unthar Froom]]'s order.

**[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] declined to share intel with Unthar in [[Session 12 - The Gate of the Far Realms|Session 12]] because [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] had excluded him.** Given that Silverhand now appears to have been magically edited, **that exclusion deserves a second look.** The Order may have institutional memory the Open Lord no longer has.

### Silverhand Has Been Compromised
Charmed into forgetting something important. **She doodles an owl mask.** Greater Restoration and Heal were floated; **she suggested Wish**; [[Vaelin]]'s Divine Intervention failed.

See [[Silverhand's Missing Memory]].

### Legend Lore Scales With What You Already Know
[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]]'s casting gave the middle and late biography. **[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]'s casting -- made after [[Kane]] showed her Farraday's face with Minor Illusion and the party described her exploits -- reached all the way back to childhood.**

That is the spell working as written: *the more you already know, the more precise and detailed the information.* **Gather descriptive detail before casting, every time.**

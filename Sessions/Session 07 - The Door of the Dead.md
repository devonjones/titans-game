## The Finish — Bathere's Throne Room

- **Kane** — Stormblade for 17 + Booming Blade rider for another 16. Bloodied
- The Death Knight (Aspirant) tries to reach us through the wall. Fails again
- A revenant hits Viseli for 14 (after reduction). Absorbed by temp HP. She makes the Wisdom save

- Celleborn: Magic Missiles, then Sanctuary on himself
- Revenant hits Stormcrow for 28. Storm fails his save but has a reaction — the revenant takes 11 bludgeoning from the Storm Sphere, plus 11 more damage. Storm 5-foot-steps back
- **Viseli crits for 26**, then hits again. The revenant is now badly fucked
- **Stormcrow** — 21 damage to the revenant. Moves closer to Vaelin. **Lightning Ball for 29.**
- A flying wraith attacks Kane. Kane casts **Shield**
- Vaelin attacks with spiritual weapon (the floating book). **Spirit Guardians** active
- Kane kills the revenant: **Smite, motherfucker.** Slashes the wraith, installs Booming Blade. 13 damage

### The Big Bads Break Through
- Footsteps downstairs — reinforcements inbound
- **One of the goat-heads dim-doors the Death Knight through the Wall of Force to our location** — the barrier does nothing against teleportation
- Celleborn paints the room with an **eerie green glow — Sickening Radiance** (maybe Fireball too, notes are fuzzy)
- Perception check: **one of the goat-heads is regenerating**
- **Viseli** jumps down, sprints through the electrical storm, shoves the winged goathead out of the way to reach the Death Knight
- **Stormcrow** casts **Lightning Bolt** on the winged goathead. 27 damage. Bloodied
- The Storm Sphere eats more zombies on its way through

- The mage (winged goathead = Bathere) looks stunned. **Kane moves his Hexblade Curse to him**
- Sickening Radiance chews Bathere for 11
- **Stormcrow lightning-balls the f out of him. Bathere dies.** All the zombies also die
- Flying goathead = the mage = **[[Bathere]] is dead**

### Cleanup

## Key Discoveries

### Ahmendril's True Game
Bathere's research suggests **Ahmendril is pursuing a lich or dracolich transformation.** The dead dragon in the basement was raw material. The ritual at [[The Dead Three Temple]] (innocents → disk → Mount Hotenow → "raise the ancient ones") may be part of the same arc — we just didn't know to connect those dots until now.

### The Door of the Dead
A Dead Three artifact. Anyone who touches it dies instantly. **Ahmendril has it.** What's he using it for — a gate to the realm of the dead? A weapon? A key to something? Bathere couldn't pry an answer out of it with Legend Lore.

### The Avernus Portal
Dragonspear's historic curse is real and sits in the basement. The **statue in the fountain** is the relic holding it closed. Whatever happens with this castle going forward, that fountain **must not be disturbed.**

### Bathere's Political Position
Bathere was researching lichdom, hoarding undead, commissioning a dead dragon, collecting the Door of the Dead — and he dismissed [[Viggio Martel]] and the [[Zhentarim]]. He was **not** part of the Zhent project. He was pursuing his own apotheosis. Ahmendril was using him, or working alongside him — but the Zhent occupation of Daggerford and Bathere's research are parallel operations, not the same one.

### The Helm Problem
The Goat Head Mask is an **evil artifact, still attuned to Bathere's corpse.** If we destroy Bathere's body, the attunement may break — but artifacts often have their own resurrection clauses. We need to understand what the helm does before we decide how to handle it.

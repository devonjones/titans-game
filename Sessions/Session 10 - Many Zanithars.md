## Key Discoveries

### Ahmendril = Zanithar (And There Are Many)
- **[[Ahmendril]] is "a Zanithar"** -- a recurring role/entity that is periodically killed and replaced
- Killing [[Ahmendril]] does not end the threat; another Zanithar will follow
- This is the campaign's biggest reframe since the Martavis reveal
- [[The Death Tyrant]] head is presumably the *visual signature* of a Zanithar -- not an injury, a uniform

### The Dragoncult Wars
- Historical event referenced by [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]
- Apparently connects [[Ahmendril]]'s dragon-revival plan to a known precedent
- Need to research what these were, when they happened, what the outcome was

### Two Dragons, Two Discs
- [[Ahmendril]] is trying to bring back **TWO more dragons**, not one
- There are **two discs** being drained -- **black and red**, mixing
- We have only seen one ([[The Disk]] from the temple); the other is somewhere else
- This recontextualizes [[The Dead Three Temple|the Daggerford temple ritual]] -- it was one of two parallel preparation sites

### The Scroll = Dragon Control
- The vellum/Abyssal scroll from [[Session 05 - Signed S|Session 5]] wasn't just ritual instructions
- It contains **directions to CONTROL the dragons after revival**
- Whoever holds the scroll **commands the resurrected dragons**
- This is now a top-priority artifact -- the scroll is currently in [[Vaelin]]'s possession

### Silverhand Is a Harper
- [[Laeral Silverhand]], Open Lord of Waterdeep, is a [[Harpers|Harper]]
- She already knew [[General Martavis|Martavis]] was a Harper
- The Harpers have penetrated to the very top of the [[Lords' Alliance]]

### Bathere Was Not Working for Ahmendril
- The necromancer ([[Bathere]]) **did not know Ahmendril**
- Bathere was either being used unwittingly, or running a parallel project that Ahmendril co-opted
- This complicates the timeline -- Ahmendril did not direct Bathere; their goals just aligned at [[Dragonspear]]

### The Pencheska Conspiracy is Operating in Waterdeep
- The night attack at the Ellenfear estate was professional, coordinated, fast, and lethal
- The attackers wore raven masks (or one did)
- The Pencheska method is **replacement of nobles** -- the attack was almost certainly an attempted kidnapping/replacement of one or more party members or the Duchess
- See: [[The Pencheska Conspiracy]]

### Vaelin's Divination Was Correct
- Vaelin's castle quarters would have been a "terrible idea" tonight
- The attackers may have hit the castle as well, or specifically targeted Vaelin's place
- Either way: the assassins knew where the party would have been if not for the divination

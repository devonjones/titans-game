## Morning -- The Silverhand Briefing

### Looking for a Senechal
- [[Vaelin]] seeks a senechal to make contact
- The party had previously left [[The Goat Head Mask|the Mask]] and [[Bathere]]'s necromancer notes with [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]]
- No one comes for the party -- they have to seek the meeting themselves

### The Library
- Party heads back to the wing where they met Silverhand the previous night
- Find a functionary: *"Ah. I've been expecting you. Come with me."*
- Led to a library
- **[[Laeral Silverhand]] is there, buried in books**
- She looks at a clock. *"How are you?"* Then: ***"Get me a report on the army."***

### The Dead Three Briefing

[[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] asks what the party knows about the **Dead Three** and holds forth on the subject. She covers:
- The Dead Three (Bane, Bhaal, Myrkul)
- The **Time of Troubles** (the gods walked the earth)

[[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] **already knows everything she's covering** -- he wrote his **doctoral thesis on the Dead Three.** Confirmed: Celleborn is a published academic on the topic.

But **Silverhand knows more than the published record:**

### **The Door of the Dead = Piece of a Far Realm Gateway**

> The Dead Three made a **magical gateway to the Far Realm** -- a place of the **old gods**, a place of **raw chaos and power**. They **got in over their heads**, **closed the door, and spread the pieces around** the world to keep it from being reassembled.

> ***"[[The Door of the Dead|The door we found in Dragonspear]] -- I'm afraid that was a part of it."***

This is a massive reframe. [[The Door of the Dead]] is not just a Dead Three artifact -- **it is a fragment of a sealed gateway to the Far Realm.** Whoever assembles the pieces opens the door.

> ***"[[Ahmendril]] bears further investigation."***

The notes the party recovered confirmed Ahmendril has the Door. He's holding a Far Realm gateway piece. Combined with the [[The Disk|two-disc dragon revival]], the [[The Dragoncult Wars|Dragoncult Wars]] connection, and the [[Zanithar]] cycle, **Ahmendril is doing something far bigger than reviving two dragons.** The dragons may be a *cover, byproduct, or means* -- the Far Realm gateway is the prize.

### The Phylactery Theory

[[Vaelin]] presses on [[The Goat Head Mask|the helmet]].

Silverhand:
- More concerned about the **Door** than the helmet
- Says the helmet **might be a phylactery**
- **She attempted to destroy it and could not**

[[Cael Stormcrow|Stormcrow]] proposes: ***"Toss it in a volcano."*** ([[Mount Hotenow]] available.)

### Troll Claw Hills
- That's where [[General Martavis|Martavis]] was going -- about a **four-day ride**

- Silverhand: **"Wait. I need to talk about it in the committee."**

## Key Discoveries

### The Door of the Dead = Far Realm Gateway Piece
**Massive reframe.** The Dead Three built a gateway to the **Far Realm** -- the place of the old gods, raw chaos, raw power. They got in over their heads, closed it, and **scattered the pieces.** [[The Door of the Dead]] is one of those pieces. [[Ahmendril]] has it. Putting the pieces back together opens the door.

### Goat Head Mask = Probable Phylactery
- [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] thinks the [[The Goat Head Mask|Mask]] is a **phylactery**
- **She attempted to destroy it and could not**
- If it IS a phylactery, [[Bathere]] is not gone -- he can come back. Possibly to the Mask itself
- Stormcrow's "toss it in a volcano" is now seriously on the table

### Celleborn the Dead Three Scholar
- [[Lord Celleborn Ellenfear|Celleborn]] **wrote his doctoral thesis on the Dead Three**
- Knew everything Silverhand said about them already; she just had the classified appendix
- This makes him the party's subject-matter expert on the Dead Three going forward
- Worth asking him about the Mask, the Door, and Ahmendril's symbolism

### The Two Dragons -- Rofidian and Ravica
- **CONFIRMED (will be revealed in-fiction next session):** the two dragons are **Rofidian (red)** and **Ravica (black)** -- the dragons [[Ahmendril]] was reviving per [[Session 10 - Many Zanithars|Session 10]]'s briefing. Colors match the two discs (red + black)
- **Ravica (black)** -- the one toward the docks. **Killed by the Tarrasque**
- **Rofidian (red)** -- the giant fire-breather Kane saw on the roof. **Escaped** through the broken city wall, pursued by the Tarrasque
- The ritual succeeded faster than expected. Both discs were drained somewhere out of sight; Rofidian and Ravica rose and attacked Waterdeep

### The Tarrasque
- **Appeared from the sea at dawn** -- consistent with the lore that Titans manifest when world-threatening events occur
- Singled out one of the attacking dragons; killed it; pursued the other
- **Wizard spells bounced off** (full Magic Resistance / Magic Items immunity)
- Crashed through the wall and the besieging army on its way out
- **Now somewhere outside the city, chasing the surviving dragon**
- Whether the Tarrasque is allied, hostile, or just hunting is unclear

### The Stolen Keystone
- The **castle's main entrance keystone arch** was deliberately removed during the dragon attack
- Already more than 1000 feet away when Vaelin cast Locate Object
- **The dragon attack may have been cover** for whoever took it
- Cui bono? Who needs a Waterdeep castle keystone, and why now?
- This is a new plot thread

### Silverhand Played Things Close
- Public posture vs the army: noncommittal, defensive, plays it close to the chest
- Private posture in the library: studying the Dead Three, the Mask, the necromancer notes -- already deep into the artifact problem
- She's a [[Harpers|Harper]], a [[Chosen of Mystra]], and she's running the city through a siege, two dragons, a Tarrasque, AND assassination attempts. She is the central node

# Read This First — Before Session 18

*Read aloud at the table. About a minute. Folds into the Session 18 write-up when the session is done.*

---

## A dragon is standing in front of us and it wants something. Here is how we got here.

We killed **Samus** — Stormcrow put a lightning bolt through a closed door and took his head off. What we did not know is that Samus had **sold a soul-harvesting brothel to a devil**, and he was the collateral. The moment he died the debt came due, his keep collapsed, and a **gate to Hell** opened where it had stood.

We also learned what this war is actually about. **Ahmendril is a mask maker.** His masks **bring his lieutenants back from the dead** — we watched Samus die on a battlefield a century ago and then kneel before Ahmendril, masked and alive. **Silverhand has a hole in her memory** shaped like Ahmendril's owl mask, and she says it would take a *Wish* to fix.

So we went to close the gate. Vaelin worked out that **something physical is holding it open, and the pit fiend is carrying it.** Unthar Froom brought five armored veterans, Silverhand loaned us the wizard **Lady Estrell** and a scroll of Banishment, and Vaelin cast Hero's Feast.

The pit fiend's first words were not an attack: ***"Have you brought the masks? The masks I am owed?"***

---

## Last session we broke the devils.

Silverhand teleported **Celleborn** straight onto the battlefield. *(Peter was out, so Kane was run at the table.)*

**Vaelin's Banishment failed** — a pit fiend has magic resistance and legendary resistance, so one big save-or-die was never going to land. So we did it the hard way.

**Estrell threw up a twenty-five-foot Globe of Invulnerability** and it visibly ate an incoming Wall of Fire — the wall stopped dead at the edge and carried on out the other side. **Celleborn hypnotised the entire second wave** of devils as they closed on the paladins. **Unthar turned to Vaelin and asked where he wanted his men**, and Vaelin told him.

**And Viseli took the whole war on her body.** A chain devil wrapped her up and the army focused her — a hundred and three in one pile-on, then a hundred and fifty-three in a single round. **She went from two hundred and eight hit points down to sixty.** Rage halved almost all of it. Vaelin healed her twenty-six, then cast Heal for seventy more.

**Stormcrow lightning-bolted the ravaged flier out of the sky.** Then the primary pit fiend landed next to him, and **Stormcrow went down.**

Celleborn threw everything at it — a stasis, an action surge into Finger of Death, and a Chronal Shift to force a reroll. **It chose to succeed**, and took thirty-seven anyway.

Then **Stormcrow got up, misty stepped into the middle of the paladins, and killed the pit fiend with Lightning Lure.** A cantrip. Pit fiends are immune to fire and poison. They are not immune to lightning.

**The devils fled. We killed them. One got away.**

---

## And then the dragon.

It flew over us and **the magic went out of the air.** It is a **red dragon**. It landed.

It doesn't seem to like the paladins. And it said:

> ## *"I believe you have something that belongs to me."*

**It is almost certainly Rofidian** — the ancient red dragon Ahmendril raised from the red disc, who attacked Waterdeep and then fled through the broken wall with the Tarrasque on his tail. **He has evidently lost the Tarrasque.**

**And Vaelin is carrying the scroll that commands him.** The Abyssal scroll, the one Silverhand told us is dragon-control instructions — *whoever holds it commands the revived dragons.* It has been called the only leash on this dragon for seven sessions, and it is in Vaelin's pack right now.

---

## Things to remember when we pick up

- **The gate is still open.** We never finished the job
- **Nobody looted the pit fiend.** The thing holding the gate open was supposed to be *on him*, and he is dead on the field a few yards away
- **A dead-looking third pit fiend** is lying across the field and nobody went to check it
- **The caged pit fiend** may still be alive — it was still throwing fireballs late in the fight
- **One devil escaped** to report
- **Viseli is on sixty hit points** out of two hundred and eight
- **Ahmendril showed up at the end of two sessions ago and we never saw him last session.** We do not know where he is
- **The Tarrasque is loose somewhere**, unaccounted for since Waterdeep
- Two things we now know for certain: **"chooses to succeed" means legendary resistance** — so burn the first three with cheap spells before committing a real one — and **Celleborn is a chronurgy wizard**, with two Chronal Shifts a day to force a reroll on anything within thirty feet, including enemies

## The question on the table

**Do we give the dragon the scroll?**

Hand it over and we throw away the only control anyone has over an ancient red dragon — and hand it to him. Refuse, and we start a fight we are in no condition to have.

He opened by talking. So did the pit fiend. **There is a deal here somewhere, and that scroll is the only card we are holding.**

*(He may not even mean the scroll. Ravica's carved-up corpse is in Waterdeep under Celleborn's father's knives. And Kane is carrying Samus's mask — while the goat mask was stolen out of a dragon's lair in the first place. Worth finding out what he means before we offer anything.)*

## Theories & Hypotheses

### The Zhentarim Destabilization Strategy
The [[Zhentarim]] may be deliberately fomenting war and chaos across the region (monster tribes, Dalelands civil war, banditry) to drive refugees toward Daggerford. This serves multiple purposes:
1. **Overwhelm local resources** - Town can't handle the influx
2. **Create social tension** - Locals angry about migrants, divided population
3. **Justify their presence** - "Security" contract seems necessary
4. **Weaken Lady Morwen** - Crisis she can't solve undermines her authority
5. **Economic disruption** - Taxes stolen, trade routes unsafe

The organized nature of the monster tribes and the Zhentarim's convenient readiness to provide "security" suggests coordination rather than coincidence.

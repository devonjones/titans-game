> **Tactically:** it means a pit fiend will eat your first three save-or-die effects for free. **Burn them deliberately with cheap spells before committing a real one.** Between Banishment, Momentary Stasis and Finger of Death, the party may have already burned them all.

## Key Discoveries

### "Chooses to succeed" is Legendary Resistance
A pit fiend has it by stat block, and the GM narrated it with the **exact phrase [[Samus]] used** in [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]]. **That retroactively confirms Samus had Legendary Resistance.** Going forward: **burn the three with cheap effects before committing a real save-or-die.**

### Celleborn is a Chronurgy Wizard
**Momentary Stasis** (Chronurgy 2) and **Chronal Shift** (Chronurgy 2, 2/long rest, reaction, force a reroll). Explains the "Fae point" and Action Surge economy he has been spending since [[Session 13 - No Honor in Here|Session 13]].

### The anchor-object was never recovered
[[Vaelin]]'s whole premise was that **the thing holding the gate open is on the pit fiend.** The primary pit fiend is **dead** — and **nobody looted it.** The gate is still open and a red dragon just landed on the field.

### Banishment failed
The centrepiece of the plan. **Magic Resistance plus Legendary Resistance** makes a pit fiend close to immune to single save-or-suck effects. Whether the [[Laeral Silverhand|Silverhand]] scroll is still unspent is unrecorded.

### Lightning is the answer on this battlefield
Pit fiends are immune to fire and poison. **[[Cael Stormcrow|Cael]] killed two of the three big fliers**, one with a **Lightning Bolt** and one with a **cantrip**, because **[[Elemental Adept]] ignores resistance** and nothing here resists lightning.

### A red dragon has arrived and wants something
**[[Rofidian]]**, almost certainly — the ancient red [[Ahmendril]] revived, last seen fleeing [[Waterdeep]] with the [[The Tarrasque|Tarrasque]] behind him. **He has lost the Tarrasque, he dislikes paladins, and he says the party has something of his.**

**[[Vaelin]] is holding the Abyssal scroll that commands him.**

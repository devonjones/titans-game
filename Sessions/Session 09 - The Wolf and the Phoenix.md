## The Reveal

### After the Battle
- [[General Martavis|Martavis]] asks [[Ono]]: *"Did any get away?"* -- *"None observed."*
- Martavis asks for **a private word with the Duchess**

### Kane's Question
- [[Kane]] asks: *"What about Thorn's Gap?"*
- Martavis says he was right where he wanted to be

### Martavis's Intel
- *"You can enter the city safely. But it will be under siege."*
- On further questioning, **Martavis shows the party his [[Harpers|Harper]] pin**

### **General Martavis is a Harper.**

Everything recontextualizes:
- The argument with the Duchess in Session 1 -- was he warning her, not threatening?
- Deflecting Kane's questions ("let's not waste time") -- maintaining cover
- Running soup kitchens and maintaining order -- genuine good governance, not a PR front
- Recovering the taxes from the bandits -- actually trying to help
- The "lockdown" of Daggerford -- was he locking down the *Wolf* Zhents?
- Being "aghast" on the hilltop -- not shock at resistance, but concern for the party fighting his own plan
- His emblem: not a necromancer command unit, but a **Harper infiltration team** embedded during the Thayan war

### Key Revelations

The unnamed Wolf cavalry captain the party killed and looted last session was one of [[Samus]]'s operational officers (Wolf chain of command).

**Martavis killed Viggio.** Viggio's job was to **replace the Duchess** -- confirming the Pencheska conspiracy angle. Martavis pinned him to a tree as a message.

**Ahmendril is a superior of Samus.** The hierarchy:
- **Ahmendril** (top -- Death Tyrant, lich aspirant)
- **Samus** (Zhentarim head in Daggerford, reports to Ahmendril)
- **Viggio** (operative)

**Martavis is headed for the Troll Claw Hills** (the Zhent headquarters from Session 6 intel).
